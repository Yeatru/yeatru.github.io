Amazon FBA Product Research for China Sourcing

July 13, 2026

What Is FBA Product Research for China Sourcing?

Amazon FBA product research for China sourcing is the process of validating a product idea before you commit to a Chinese supplier — checking that demand exists, the competition is beatable, the margin survives FBA fees and freight, compliance is clear, and the spec is manufacturable at the right MOQ. The goal is a GO/NO-GO decision grounded in numbers, so you never pay a 50% deposit on a product that cannot win. This is the upstream partner to Amazon FBA sourcing from China.

The 5-Filter Research Framework

  • Demand — monthly search volume (Helium 10/Jungle Scout), BSR trend and review velocity (Keepa). Steady or rising demand, not a one-week spike.
  • Competition — top-10 review count and quality, brand dominance, Amazon’s Choice badges. Pass if the top 10 averages under 300 reviews and no brand locks 60%+ of sales.
  • Margin math — sale price minus FBA fees, freight DDP, duty, EXW, and prep leaves 30%+. Below 15% is a commodity trap.
  • Compliance gate — CPSC, FDA, Prop 65, section 301 tariff; gated categories and IP-encumbered designs are automatic NO-GOs.
  • Sourcing feasibility — MOQ, customization, IP clarity on Alibaba/1688/Yiwu. If you cannot source the spec at the right MOQ, demand does not matter.

Candidate: a silicone baby feeding set (bowl + suction mat + bib). Run it through the 5 filters.

  • Demand — “silicone baby bowl” ~18,000 searches/month, up 12% YoY; BSR top 10 stable. Pass.
  • Competition — top-10 average 210 reviews, top 3 hold 38% of sales, no Amazon’s Choice lock. Pass.
  • Margin math — sale $24.99; FBA fee $4.20; freight DDP $1.80; duty (HS 3924, 3%) $0.75; EXW $4.50; prep $0.60; net $13.14 (52.6%). Pass.
  • Compliance — food-contact silicone (FDA 21 CFR 177.2600), CPSIA lead/phthalate test, Prop 65. Certs obtainable from a vetted factory. Pass.

Decision: GO. Differentiation angle: bundle bowl + bib + suction mat, BPA-free certified, in a brand-exclusive colorway. Next step: source samples via low-MOQ sourcing and prep per FBA shipping from China.

Red Flags: Automatic No-Gos

  • Saturated category — top 3 hold 60%+ of sales with 1,000+ reviews each.
  • Brand-locked — one brand owns the keyword and the Buy Box.
  • Commodity with margin under 15% — a price war will wipe profit.
  • Gated/restricted — baby, supplements, topicals, batteries need pre-approval; see Amazon restricted products.
  • IP-encumbered — active patents or trademarks blocking your design.
  • One-week viral spike — demand that collapses after the trend passes.

Tools & Where to Source

  • Demand: Helium 10 (Cerebro/Black Box), Jungle Scout, Keepa for BSR and price history.
  • Competition: Amazon Best Sellers, review scan for gaps and complaints.
  • Sourcing feasibility: Alibaba for verified suppliers, 1688 for domestic Chinese prices, Yiwu for small-commodity stock.
  • Compliance: CPSC, FDA, Prop 65, and section 301 tariff lookup by HS code.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I find products to sell on Amazon FBA from China?

Use Helium 10 or Jungle Scout for search-volume and BSR data, scan Amazon Best Sellers and review gaps for differentiation angles, then check Alibaba, 1688, or Yiwu for sourcing feasibility at the right MOQ. A valid candidate passes demand, competition, margin, compliance, and sourcing checks together.

2. What margin do I need for an FBA product sourced from China?

Target at least 30% net margin after FBA fees, freight DDP, duty, unit EXW, and prep. Below 30% leaves no room for PPC and returns; below 15% is a commodity trap where a price war wipes your profit.

3. How many reviews is too many for an FBA product?

If the top 3 listings each have 1,000+ reviews and hold 60%+ of category sales, the barrier is too high for a new entrant. Look for niches where the top 10 averages under 300 reviews and no single brand dominates.

4. What is a gated category on Amazon?

A gated category requires pre-approval, invoices, and often lab certificates before you can list (e.g., baby products, supplements, topicals, batteries). Factor approval lead time and compliance cost in before sourcing. See Amazon restricted products.

5. Can I sell branded products sourced from China?

Only if you are the brand owner or an authorized distributor. Sourcing a third-party brand and reselling on Amazon triggers IP complaints and account suspension. For private-label, register your own trademark (Brand Registry) before launch.

6. How do I check demand for an FBA product?

Use Helium 10 Cerebro or Black Box, or Jungle Scout for monthly search volume, then Keepa for 90-day BSR trend and price history. Confirm demand is steady or rising, not a one-week viral spike that will collapse.

Common Pitfalls to Avoid

  • Using the supplier’s test report without verification. Many factories provide fake or expired CE/FCC reports. Verify the report number on the certification body’s website before placing a bulk order.
  • Applying the wrong certification for the destination market. CE for EU, FCC/CPC for US, UKCA for UK, RCM for Australia. Shipping CE-marked goods to the US = customs seizure and $10k+ fines.
  • No test report for the exact product variant. A CE report for a red 220V version does not cover a blue 110V version. Each variant needs its own test or a report covering all variants.
  • Forgetting ongoing compliance. Regulations change (e.g., EU GPSR replacing GPSD, US CPSC updates). A cert valid today may be invalid in 12 months. Track renewal dates and budget for re-testing.
  • Under-declaring product category to skip certification. Listing a children’s toy as ‘home decoration’ to avoid CPC is customs fraud. The buyer of record (you) carries the liability.
