Sourcing Prep Checklist — Before Contacting Agent

July 7, 2026 Neil 7 min read

Key Takeaways

  • Bring a spec sheet (dimensions, materials, target cost, certifications) — "just give me a price" wastes 1–2 weeks of back-and-forth.
  • Know your landed-cost target, not just ex-works price: include freight, duty, FBA fees, and 5% buffer.
  • Prepare brand assets (logo vector file, packaging dieline, color codes) so OEM quotes come back fast.
  • Have a budget tier (good/better/best) — agents can quote 3 supplier options per product.
  • Pre-decide MOQ and lead time tolerance — these drive supplier selection more than unit price.

The "Just Give Me a Price" Problem

Every China sourcing agent has received the same message: "I want to buy products, please send me a price." No product photo. No quantity. No specification. No target market. The buyer expects a fast, accurate quote, but the agent or factory cannot answer without key parameters. The result is days of back-and-forth, wasted samples, and quotes that miss the mark. Proper sourcing preparation is what separates serious buyers from window shoppers, and it is the single fastest way to get accurate China factory quotes.

Why Preparation Beats "Bulk Inquiry"

Factories receive dozens of inquiries every day. Vague requests without order quantity or product specification are flagged as "price shoppers" and pushed to the bottom of the queue. When you arrive with a complete China sourcing checklist, suppliers take you seriously, quote faster, and offer better pricing. Preparation also helps your sourcing agent filter the right factories instead of guessing what you actually want.

Rule of thumb: The more specific your brief, the more accurate your quote. A 5-minute preparation call with your agent can save 5 weeks of miscommunication.

The 6 Items Every Sourcing Brief Must Include

Use this sourcing preparation checklist before you message any China factory or agent. These six items are the minimum a supplier needs to quote accurately.

1. Product Style or Model Reference

Clearly show what you want to source. The best references are:

  • Photos of the exact product (front, side, back, packaging)
  • A link to a similar product on Amazon, Alibaba, or a competitor site
  • Original product design files (CAD, 3D render, or sketch) for custom products

Saying "a kitchen organizer" is not enough. Saying "a 3-tier bamboo kitchen organizer, 30x20x15cm, with removable trays" gives a factory something to quote against.

2. Target Order Quantity per SKU

Order quantity drives the price more than any other variable. Factories price by tier: 500 pcs, 1,000 pcs, 5,000 pcs, 10,000 pcs. Always state the estimated order quantity per SKU, not a vague "container load". This also helps your agent identify whether your volume fits the factory's MOQ or whether you need a smaller-batch supplier.

  • Trial order: 100-500 pcs (higher unit price, often Yiwu market)
  • Standard MOQ: 1,000-3,000 pcs (typical factory MOQ)
  • Production run: 5,000+ pcs (best pricing, dedicated line)

3. Product Specification Sheet

The product specification is the backbone of every quote and every QC inspection. At minimum, include:

  • Material: e.g. 304 stainless steel, food-grade silicone, ABS
  • Dimensions: length x width x height in cm or mm
  • Color: Pantone code or sample photo
  • Weight: net weight and gross weight per unit
  • Packaging: retail box, bulk polybag, master carton qty
  • Finishing: matte, glossy, anodized, electroplated

4. Target Market and Certifications

Your sales country determines which certifications the product needs. This affects factory selection, customs requirements, and cost.

  • USA: FCC, FDA, CPSIA, UL, CPC for children's products
  • EU: CE, RoHS, REACH, EN71 for toys
  • UK: UKCA
  • Middle East: SASO, GCC
  • Japan: PSE, METI

Telling your agent "selling on Amazon US" lets them filter for factories with existing FCC or FDA documentation, avoiding last-minute certification delays.

5. Budget Range or Target Price

Many buyers refuse to share a target price, fearing they will be overcharged. In reality, the opposite happens. Without a budget, factories either quote high to protect margin or low to win the order and cut quality later. Sharing a realistic target price lets your agent match you with the right factory tier. If your target is $2 and the market price is $5, you will know immediately and can adjust the design, materials, or quantity before wasting weeks of sampling.

6. Timeline and Launch Plan

State your required delivery date and any launch deadlines (e.g. Q4 holiday season, Amazon Prime Day). A typical China sourcing timeline is:

  • Factory sourcing and quoting: 1-2 weeks
  • Sampling and revisions: 2-4 weeks
  • Bulk production: 25-45 days
  • QC and shipping: 2-6 weeks (sea) or 5-10 days (air)

If you need product in 4 weeks total, your agent must know on day one to plan air freight or stock products.

Design Files and Branding for OEM/ODM Orders

If you are doing OEM or ODM customization, prepare these product design files before contacting a sourcing agent:

  • Logo files: vector format (.AI, .EPS, .SVG) plus high-res PNG
  • Brand guidelines: color codes, fonts, placement rules
  • Product design files: 3D CAD (.STEP, .IGES), 2D drawings with tolerances
  • Packaging artwork: dieline, CMYK print files, barcodes (UPC/EAN)
  • Sample or prototype: physical reference is worth a thousand renders

For custom molds, also prepare an NNN (Non-Disclosure, Non-Use, Non-Circumvention) agreement to protect your IP before sharing files with any factory.

Common Mistakes That Slow Down Sourcing

  • Sending an entire catalog and asking for prices: Factories treat this as spam. Pick 3-5 target SKUs.
  • No quantity stated: Without MOQ context, quotes are meaningless.
  • No target price: Leads to either inflated quotes or cheap-and-bad samples.
  • Vague specs: "Good quality, nice packaging" is not a spec.
  • Skipping certifications: Discovering CE or FCC is needed at the customs stage is expensive.
  • Asking for free samples of 20 products: Treat sampling as an investment, not free shopping.

How Yeatru Sourcing Helps You Structure the Brief

Once you provide the items above, our team turns that brief into a structured sourcing plan: target factories in Yiwu, Guangzhou, or Shenzhen; realistic MOQ and pricing tiers; certification roadmap; and a sampling schedule. Quick Pre-Contact Checklist

  1. Product photos, reference links, or design files ready
  2. Estimated order quantity per SKU
  3. Complete product specification (material, size, color, packaging)
  4. Target sales market and required certifications
  5. Target unit price or budget range
  6. Required delivery date and launch plan
  7. Logo and packaging artwork (for OEM/ODM)
  8. NNN agreement ready (for custom mold or IP-sensitive projects)

Final Thoughts

Sourcing preparation is not paperwork. It is the difference between a 3-week sampling cycle and a 3-month mess. Spending one hour organizing your brief before you contact a China sourcing agent saves you weeks of back-and-forth, multiple sample rounds, and the cost of misquoted products. Come prepared, and your agent can focus on what they do best: finding the right factory and getting you the best price.
