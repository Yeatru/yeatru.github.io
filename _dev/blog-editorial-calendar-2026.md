## GEO + SEO Strategy Framework

### Why GEO matters (2026 data)
- 88% of informational Google queries now show AI Overviews
- 37% of consumers start searches with AI (ChatGPT/Perplexity) not Google
- AI-referred sessions grew 527% YoY through 2025
- Only 11% of domains are cited by both ChatGPT and Perplexity
- Content with citations/statistics gets 40% more AI visibility (Princeton GEO research)

### The 7 GEO pillars applied to every blog
1. **Citation-grade answer block** — 40-60 word direct answer at the top, self-contained, quotable
2. **Question-shaped H2/H3 headings** — match how buyers actually prompt AI ("How do I verify a Chinese supplier's business license?")
3. **Specific numbers + named sources** — statistics, price ranges, % figures LLMs can attribute
4. **Self-contained passages** — each section readable alone, no pronoun dependency
5. **Multi-schema markup** — Article + HowTo + FAQPage + BreadcrumbList + BlogPosting (existing pattern)
6. **Freshness signals** — datePublished/dateModified, "2026" in title, quarterly updates
7. **Entity authority** — consistent "Yeatru Sourcing" + "Neil Liu" + "Yiwu" + "China sourcing agent" entity signals

### Technical requirements per blog
- `llms.txt` allow AI crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended)
- H1-H2-H3 hierarchy (87% of AI-cited pages have this)
- Lists/bullets (80% of AI-cited pages use them)
- 2-3 data points per 300 words (citation density)
- FAQ section with FAQPage schema (FAQ pages get disproportionately more AI citations)
- Key Takeaways box after intro (TL;DR — LLMs extract these first)
- Conclusion with quotable summary sentence
- Author bio (Neil Liu, E-E-A-T signal)
- Internal links to 4 related articles + service pages
- og:image, canonical, hreflang en/x-default

## Distribution & Indexing Strategy
1. **Publish** each blog to `main` branch → Cloudflare Pages auto-deploys
2. **Update** `blog.html` index + `atom.xml` feed + `sitemap-static.xml` with each new post
3. **Submit** to Google Search Console (URL inspection + request indexing)
4. **Ping** IndexNow (Bing) on each publish
5. **Internal link** from 4 related articles + relevant service/product pages
6. **AI crawl allowance**: ensure `robots.txt` allows GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended
7. **Quarterly refresh**: update dateModified + stats to maintain AI citation status
