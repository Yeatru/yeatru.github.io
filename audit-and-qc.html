Audit & Inspection
Why Audit Before Production

Don't Find Out Your Supplier is a Scam — After You've Paid

Every week, importers lose thousands to fake factories, shell companies, and undisclosed subcontracting. A professional factory audit is the cheapest insurance you'll ever buy.

Real Factory Verification

We physically visit each factory to verify its existence, size, production capacity, and equipment — eliminating shell companies and trading companies posing as manufacturers.

License & Compliance Check

We verify business licenses, export rights, ISO certifications, and product compliance (CE, FCC, FDA, etc.) to ensure your supplier meets legal requirements.

Quality Management System

We assess the factory's QC processes, employee training, inspection equipment, and documentation systems — predicting whether they can consistently meet your standards.

Production Capability Audit

We evaluate production lines, machinery, capacity, and workflow to confirm the factory can handle your order volume without compromising quality or lead times.

Subcontracting Alert

Many factories quietly outsource production to unknown third parties. We identify this practice and assess the sub-supplier's capabilities to protect your brand.

Financial Stability

We check the factory's business registration, operating history, and credit standing to reduce the risk of production delays or supplier bankruptcy mid-order.

Audit & Inspection Services

Three Levels of Factory Audit & QC

Comprehensive coverage from pre-production verification to final shipment inspection.

Initial Factory Audit

Before you commit to a supplier. We conduct a thorough on-site verification covering:

  • Factory existence & address verification
  • Business license & export rights
  • Production lines & equipment
  • QC team & inspection procedures
  • Worker conditions & safety
  • Full photo & video report

Production Process Audit

During production, before mass run. We verify the production setup meets your exact requirements:

  • Raw material inspection
  • First article inspection
  • Production line calibration
  • Process flow verification
  • In-line QC checkpoints
  • Defect trend analysis

Pre-Shipment Inspection

Before goods leave China. Final quality verification using AQL 2.5/4.0 standards:

  • AQL 2.5 random sampling
  • Visual & dimensional check
  • Functionality testing
  • Packaging & labeling check
  • Container loading supervision
  • Pass/fail report in 24h
What We Check

Factory Audit Checklist

Our comprehensive audit covers 50+ checkpoints across these categories.

Audit Checklist
Factory Profile: Registration, address, size, employee count, year established
Production Capability: Equipment, production lines, monthly capacity, lead time
Quality System: ISO 9001, QC team size, inspection procedures, defect rate tracking
Material Sourcing: Supplier network, raw material quality, inventory management
Export Capability: Export license, freight experience, documentation, Incoterms knowledge
Compliance: Product certifications (CE, FCC, FDA, RoHS, REACH), testing reports
Financial Health: Operating history, credit check, bank references, payment terms
Workplace Conditions: Employee safety, working hours, facilities, labor compliance
FAQ

Factory Audit & QC FAQs

A standard on-site factory audit starts at $350 per factory location. This includes our auditor's travel, full inspection, and a detailed photo report. Additional factories in the same region are typically discounted.

An on-site audit typically takes 1 day per factory. Our auditor visits in the morning, conducts the full inspection, and compiles the report within 24 hours. Rush audits can be arranged with 48-hour notice.

We generally notify the factory in advance to ensure someone is available to show us around. However, we can also conduct surprise audits if you suspect the factory may be misrepresenting their operations.

We'll provide a detailed report rating the factory across all checkpoints. For critical issues (fake factory, no production capability), we'll recommend immediate termination. For minor issues, we'll provide a corrective action plan and can help you monitor improvements.

Our primary coverage is China, but we can arrange audits in Vietnam, India, Bangladesh, and other manufacturing hubs through our partner network. Contact us for specific location requests.
