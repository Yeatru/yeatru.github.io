Yiwu Market Sourcing Guide 2026: 75,000+ Suppliers

How to source from Yiwu International Trade City.

Key Takeaways

  • The Yiwu International Trade City (义乌国际商贸城) covers 7.5 million sqm across 5 districts with 75,000+ booths and 2.1M+ SKUs — the world's largest wholesale small-commodities market, where one buyer can fill a container in a 3-day visit.
  • Each district specializes: D1 toys/flowers/jewelry, D2 hardware/electronics/bags, D3 kitchen/hardware/tools, D4 socks/garments, D5 imports/auto accessories — map SKUs to districts before you fly.
  • A booth is a trading company, not a factory; MOQ is 1–10 cartons, paid on-the-spot with a 30% deposit. The mechanics are explained in our Yiwu agent for foreigners guide.
  • Three sourcing channels: walk-in (best for first trips, mixed categories), Yiwu agent-assisted (cuts list 10–25%, handles QC/export), or the Yiwu online app (best for repeat SKUs, no flight).
  • Yiwu is inland — goods truck 2.5 h to Ningbo for sea LCL/FCL, or air-freight via PVG/HKG. Quote DDP door-to-door so export declaration is handled; pair with our consolidation warehouse to merge multi-booth orders.

What Is the Yiwu Market?

The Yiwu International Trade City (义乌国际商贸城, Yiwu International Trade Mart) in Yiwu, Zhejiang is the world's largest wholesale market for small commodities — a permanent, indoor complex covering 7.5 million sqm across 5 districts, with 75,000+ booths selling 2.1 million+ SKUs across toys, hardware, kitchenware, jewelry, electronics, garments, and imported goods. Unlike 1688.com (online B2B) or the Canton Fair (twice-yearly), Yiwu is open daily year-round and built for walk-in cash-and-carry wholesale: a buyer can source 200 SKUs from 60 booths, pay 30% deposits, and have everything consolidated and shipped within 3 weeks. It is the default destination for Amazon FBA sellers, dollar-store chains, and TikTok Shop creators running low-MOQ mixed-category orders.

Booth ≠ Factory: How Yiwu Actually Works

The single most misunderstood thing about Yiwu is that a booth is a trading company, not a factory. The person behind the counter sources from one or several factories behind them, marks the goods up 5–20%, holds inventory or orders it on demand, and handles export paperwork through a licensed export-agent entity. Consequences for you:

  • Price is not factory-direct — but it is not absurd either; the booth markup covers small-quantity flexibility and instant availability.
  • You cannot visit "the factory" from a booth — the factory is often 1–4 hours away in Wenzhou, Taizhou, or Shantou; an agent can arrange a separate factory audit.
  • The booth's business license (营业执照) lists "销售" or "贸易" (sales/trade), not "生产" (manufacturing) — verify this before any deposit, using the same method as verifying any Chinese supplier license.
  • The booth holds the export license — or, more commonly, uses a separate licensed export agent in Yiwu; you cannot legally clear export yourself without one.

This structure is also why "MOQ 1 carton" is real: the booth already stocks goods, so 1-carton orders cost you list price; 50-carton orders earn 15–25% off.

Sample & Deposit Workflow at a Yiwu Booth

  1. Walk the aisle, photograph SKUs — note the booth number on the orange cardstock each booth provides; this is your record.
  2. Ask for a sample — most booths hand one free for orders ≥100 cartons, otherwise charge $5–$50 per sample; pay in cash or WeChat.
  3. Negotiate the bulk price — list drops at 10, 50, and 100 carton tiers; quote your real volume, not aspirations.
  4. Pay 30% deposit, get the order card stamped — the stamped booth card is your contract; specify spec, qty, delivery date, carton marks. Avoid 100% upfront — the deposit logic in China deposit terms applies equally at Yiwu booths.
  5. Production 7–15 days — stock SKUs ship in 2–5 days; OEM/private-label runs 15–40 days plus a sample-approval round.
  6. Pre-shipment QC at the booth warehouse — AQL 2.5 inspection with photo/video; reject and rework before balance is paid.
  7. Move to consolidation warehouse — pay the 70% balance only after QC passes; the warehouse combines all your booths into one shipment.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is Yiwu market?

Yiwu International Trade City (义乌国际商贸城) in Yiwu, Zhejiang is the world's largest wholesale small-commodities market: 7.5 million sqm across 5 districts, 75,000+ booths, 2.1M+ SKUs. Booths are trading companies that aggregate from factories; MOQ starts at 1–10 cartons and the market is open daily year-round.

2. Can I buy just one piece at Yiwu?

No — the minimum is one carton (often 12–144 pcs depending on SKU). For true 1-piece or sample-quantity orders, use the Yiwu online app or work with an agent who consolidates. For sub-carton volumes, 1688.com with a sourcing agent is usually the better channel.

3. Are the booths factories?

No. A booth is a trading company that sources from one or more factories behind it and handles export paperwork. The business license (营业执照) lists "销售" or "贸易" (sales/trade), not "生产" (manufacturing). For factory-direct pricing, ask the booth to disclose the factory or have your agent arrange a separate factory audit.

4. Do I need a Yiwu agent?

Not legally — but for first-time or non-Chinese-speaking buyers the math almost always works: an agent saves 10–25% on list price through accumulated volume, handles booth verification, AQL QC, consolidation, and the export declaration you cannot legally do solo. See Yiwu agent for foreigners for the full breakdown.

5. When is Yiwu market open?

Daily 8:30–17:30, year-round except Chinese public holidays (Spring Festival ~7–10 days in Jan/Feb, National Day Oct 1–7). Best buying windows: March–June and September–early October; avoid late January and the 2 weeks around Spring Festival when most booths close.

6. How do I ship from Yiwu?

Yiwu is inland — there is no seaport. Goods truck 2.5 hours to Ningbo (NGB) for sea LCL/FCL, or air-freight via Yiwu-Dongyang airport or PVG. Quote DDP door-to-door through an export agent so the export declaration, customs broker, and last-mile are handled; expect $1.10–$2.20/kg sea LCL DDP to the US for a 7 CBM mixed-SKU consolidation.
