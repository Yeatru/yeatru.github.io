FAQ

Sample Order FAQs

How long does it take to receive samples?

Sample production: 3-7 days. Express shipping: 5-10 days worldwide. Total: 8-17 days from order to your door. Custom/OEM samples: 15-30 days.

Is the sample fee really refundable?

Yes. For most products the sample fee is 100% refundable when you place a bulk order of 1,000+ pieces with the same factory. The refund is credited to your bulk order invoice.

Can I order samples from multiple suppliers at once?

Yes. We can coordinate samples from 3-10 different suppliers and consolidate them into one shipment. Saves you time and up to 60% on shipping.

What if the sample quality is not acceptable?

We send a detailed photo/video report before shipping. If you reject the sample we work with the factory on revisions at no extra production cost. We don’t ship until you approve.

Can you send just photos without sending physical sample?

Yes. For $0-30 we can send a detailed photo and video report with measurements. This is perfect for quick quality checks on repeat orders without paying full express shipping.
