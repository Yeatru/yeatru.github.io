FAQ

Individual Seller Sourcing FAQs

What’s the minimum order quantity you handle?

We handle orders as small as 10-50 pieces depending on the product. For 1688 items MOQs are typically 10-100 pcs. For custom manufacturing MOQs start around 500-1,000 pcs. We’ll always try to find the lowest MOQ possible for your needs.

How do you handle payment for small orders?

For small orders under $2,000 we typically use 100% payment in advance via PayPal or bank transfer. For larger orders we offer 50% deposit + 50% balance before shipping. We never hold your money — payment goes directly to the supplier.

Can you help me sell on Amazon FBA / TikTok Shop / Shopify?

Absolutely. We have extensive experience with all three platforms. For Amazon FBA we handle FNSKU labeling poly bagging and prep. For TikTok Shop and Shopify we can dropship directly or handle bulk inventory. Just tell us your platform and we’ll tailor the service.

How does your fee work for individual sellers?

For small orders we charge a flat fee of $50-$100 per sourcing task or 5-8% of the order value (whichever is greater). This includes sourcing supplier communication QC and shipping coordination. No hidden costs.

Can you source from 1688 if I can’t read Chinese?

Yes that’s one of our core services. We browse 1688 on your behalf translate all product details verify supplier credibility and handle all communication in Chinese. You only need to tell us what you want in English.

Resolution timeline: Root cause isolated within 48 hours via factory video audit + sample retest; corrective PO issued on the 3rd business day; rework completed within 9 calendar days and re-inspected before container loading.
