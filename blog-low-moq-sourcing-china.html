Low MOQ Sourcing from China: 50-500 Pieces Guide

Where to find low-MOQ suppliers — Yiwu, 1688, stock-lot, OEM split — 2026 playbook.

Key Takeaways

  • MOQ tiers in China: Yiwu Trade City accepts 1–10 pc cash-and-carry; 1688.com stock wholesalers take 2–50 pc; stock-clearance job lots run 50–200 pc; OEM private label needs 300–1,000 pc per SKU.
  • Lower MOQ always costs more per unit: factories charge a 15–40% per-unit premium on 100 pcs vs 1,000 pcs because line setup, mold changes, and material minimums amortize over fewer units.
  • Low-MOQ sourcing is mostly trading-company territory — verify every supplier on gsxt.gov.cn and pair with a pre-shipment inspection to catch stock-lot defects.
  • Consolidation is the secret weapon: ship 5–10 SKUs at 50–100 pc each in one DDP shipment to hit air-freight 45 kg breakpoints and sea-freight LCL minimums, cutting per-unit freight 25–50%.
  • Even on a $500 order, apply the 50/50 deposit rule: 30–50% deposit to start, balance only after AQL 2.5 QC photos clear. The $25 wire fee is cheaper than losing $500 to a defective lot.

What Is Low-MOQ Sourcing from China?

Low-MOQ sourcing is the practice of buying Chinese products at order quantities below the factory's standard production minimum — typically 50–500 pieces per SKU instead of the usual 1,000–10,000. It is the only way for first-time Amazon FBA sellers, TikTok Shop creators, Kickstarter creators, and small retailers to test a market without committing a full container. The trade-off is always a higher per-unit cost: factories amortize line setup, mold changes, and material minimums over fewer units, so a 100-pc run prices 15–40% above a 1,000-pc run. Sourcing agents and Yiwu-based consolidators — like Yeatru — are the bridge between foreign small-order buyers and the Chinese low-MOQ ecosystem (Yiwu Trade City, 1688.com, stock-lot wholesalers, and OEM split-MOQ).

Where to Find Low-MOQ Suppliers in China

  1. Yiwu International Trade City — 75,000 stalls over 5 districts; cash-and-carry on most categories; 1–10 pc minimums; best for sample buying and replenishment. Pair with a Yiwu agent for QC and export handling.
  2. 1688.com — Alibaba's Chinese-language wholesale marketplace; 2–50 pc stock minimums; 30–60% cheaper than Alibaba.com because it's the domestic price tier. Use an agent for verification and DDP.
  3. Stock-clearance wholesalers in Yiwu and Shantou — 50–200 pc job lots at 30–50% off list; ideal for liquidation and promo bundles. Inspect carefully: stock lots often hide defects.
  4. OEM factories accepting split-MOQ — negotiate 100 pc per color × 5 colors = 500 pc total to share the setup cost. Works best when the factory already runs your product line for bigger buyers.
  5. Alibaba "Ready to Ship" (RTS) — pre-stocked catalog with 7-day lead; 10–100 pc minimums at +20–60% premium vs custom OEM. Convenient but no private-label customization.

Common Low-MOQ Mistakes That Cost Buyers

  • Treating "low MOQ" as "low risk" — small orders are more likely to come from trading companies or stock lots, not the factory shown on Alibaba. Always verify on gsxt.gov.cn.
  • Paying 100% upfront to "save the wire fee" — even on $500 orders, the 50/50 rule applies; low-MOQ trading companies are more likely to substitute stock-lot defects when full cash is in hand.
  • Ignoring consolidation — shipping 5 separate 50-pc orders via express courier costs 2–3× what consolidating into one DDP air or LCL shipment costs.
  • Skipping pre-shipment QC — a 100-pc lot is exactly the size where trading companies quietly slip in B-grade stock-lot units. A $120 inspector day catches the swap.
  • Quoting EXW only — a 100-pc order at $5 EXW can land at $7.50+ DDP after $3/kg air freight and duty. Always compare DDP apples-to-apples.
  • Assuming OEM branding fits a 100-pc order — custom packaging MOQ is usually 500–1,000 pcs; private-label tooling starts at $1,500–$6,000. For 100 pcs, prefer stock products with sticker/sleeve branding.
  • Forgetting duty on small parcels — US de minimis is now $800; EU IOSS applies VAT on every order. Calculate landed cost before pricing retail.

How to Negotiate a Lower MOQ Without Paying 2× the Price

  1. Split MOQ across colors or sizes — 100 pc × 5 colors = 500 pc total. The factory hits its material minimum; you get SKU variety.
  2. Pay the setup fee as a one-off — instead of $6.80/unit on 50 pcs ($340), pay a $200 setup + $4.30/unit on 100 pcs ($630). You get 2× the units for 1.85× the price.
  3. Use stock product + custom packaging — buy 200 pc of stock product, then print a 500-pc run of custom retail boxes. The box MOQ amortizes over the next 2–3 orders.
  4. Commit to a reorder — a written commitment to a 1,000-pc reorder in 90 days often unlocks the lower 1,000-pc unit price on the 100-pc trial.
  5. Bundle with a Yiwu agent — an agent's order book across multiple buyers can hit the factory's MOQ, and each buyer gets the lower unit price. Yeatru runs this model weekly for Amazon FBA launches.

Low-MOQ Payment & Quality Control

Even on a $500–$2,000 order, the standard defenses apply:

  • 50/50 deposit rule — 30–50% to start, balance only after AQL 2.5 pre-shipment photos clear (see 50/50 deposit guide).
  • AQL 2.5 inspection on every lot over 50 pcs — one inspector day at $120–$200 catches 90% of stock-lot swaps. Use pre-shipment inspection.
  • Bank transfer to the licensed company — never to a personal account (see fake invoice scams).
  • Sample approval before mass production — even on 100 pc orders, a $40–$80 sample plus $25 DHL beats a 100-pc mistake.
  • DDP shipping so duty, customs, and last-mile are pre-paid — see our DDP shipping guide for the full landed-cost formula.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the lowest MOQ I can order from China?

Yiwu International Trade City accepts 1–10 pc cash-and-carry on most off-the-shelf items. 1688.com stock wholesalers accept 2–50 pcs. Stock-clearance job lots run 50–200 pcs. OEM custom private label typically requires 300–1,000 pcs per SKU. Lower MOQ always means a higher per-unit price (15–40% premium for 100 vs 1,000 pcs).

2. Why do factories in China quote high MOQs?

Because production-line setup, mold changes, and material minimums (e.g. 500 kg resin, 1,000 m fabric) all carry fixed costs that only amortize at scale. A 100-pc run shares the same setup cost as a 5,000-pc run, so the factory charges 15–40% more per unit to keep margin. Splitting MOQ across colors or SKUs at the same factory can reduce the floor.

3. Can I get my logo / custom packaging on a 100-pc order?

Often yes, but at a premium. Print-on-package MOQ is usually 500–1,000 pcs at $0.15–$0.40/pc; on 100 pcs expect $0.80–$2.50/pc. Private-label mold tooling (e.g. a custom silicone spatula) starts at $1,500–$6,000 and requires 1,000+ pcs to amortize. For 100 pcs, prefer stock products with sticker or sleeve branding.

4. Is 1688.com safe for small orders?

1688 is Alibaba's Chinese-language wholesale marketplace with lower MOQs (2–50 pcs) and lower unit prices, but it has limited buyer protection for foreign buyers. Use a Yiwu-based agent to verify suppliers, consolidate orders, run pre-shipment QC, and ship DDP — the agent fee (3–8%) is far cheaper than the loss from a defective stock lot.

5. How do I get freight cost down on a 50–100 pc order?

Consolidate. Ship 5–10 SKUs at 50–100 pcs each in one DDP shipment; this hits air-freight 45 kg breakpoints and sea-freight LCL minimums, cutting per-unit freight by 25–50%. A Yiwu consolidation warehouse can hold goods free for 15–30 days while other factory orders arrive.

6. Should I pay 100% upfront for a small order to save wire fees?

No. Even on a $500 order, the 50/50 rule applies: 30–50% deposit to start, 50–70% balance after pre-shipment QC photos clear. Low-MOQ trading companies are more likely to substitute stock-lot defects when full cash is in hand. The $25 wire fee is cheaper than losing $500 to a defective lot.

Conclusion

Low-MOQ sourcing from China is the standard entry path for first-time Amazon FBA, TikTok Shop, and small-retail buyers — but it always trades unit price for order flexibility. The 2026 floor is 1–10 pc at Yiwu, 2–50 pc on 1688.com, 50–200 pc stock lots, and 300–1,000 pc OEM. The keys to not overpaying are: pick the right channel for your order size, ask for split-MOQ across colors or SKUs to amortize setup, consolidate into one DDP shipment to cut freight 25–50%, and never skip the AQL 2.5 pre-shipment QC + 50/50 deposit rule.
