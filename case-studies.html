Amazon FBA Small Order

Case 1: Amazon FBA Small-Batch Sourcing

Buyer profile: First-time Amazon seller, US-based, sourcing a home & kitchen product with no existing China supplier relationship.

What the buyer needed

  • Identify 3–5 real manufacturers (not trading companies) for a kitchen gadget
  • Confirm MOQ under 500 pieces for first test order
  • Pre-shipment QC before goods leave China
  • FBA-ready packaging with FNSKU labels applied
  • DDP shipping to an Amazon US fulfillment center

Yeatru process

  1. Sourcing: Market visit in Yiwu + online factory outreach. Collected 8 quotes, filtered to 4 factories with valid business licenses (SAMR verified).
  2. Sample: Ordered samples from 3 factories. Tested for material, function, and packaging quality.
  3. Negotiation: Compared quotes; negotiated MOQ and unit price with the selected factory.
  4. Production follow-up: Scheduled in-line check and pre-shipment inspection at the factory.
  5. QC: AQL Level II inspection — checked product function, labeling, packaging, and count. Defects documented with photos.
  6. FBA prep: Applied FNSKU labels, bubble-wrapped fragile items, palletized per Amazon requirements.
  7. Shipping: DDP sea freight to Amazon US warehouse with customs clearance and duty prepaid.

Outcome

Buyer received FBA-ready inventory. Inspection report and shipping documents provided. Follow-up reorder placed after successful Amazon listing launch.

Quality Issue QC Inspection

Case 2: Pre-Shipment Quality Issue Resolution

Buyer profile: DTC brand owner who had already paid a deposit to a factory before engaging Yeatru for QC only.

The problem

The buyer ordered a branded consumer product. Before final payment and shipment, they requested a third-party pre-shipment inspection.

Inspection findings

  • AQL Level II sampling at the factory warehouse
  • Defects found: inconsistent logo printing (offset), color mismatch vs approved sample, and 5% of units missing accessories
  • Defect rate exceeded AQL 2.5 acceptance threshold

Resolution

  1. Detailed QC report with photos sent to buyer and factory
  2. Negotiated with factory: sorting of affected batch, re-printing of defective units, replacement of missing accessories
  3. Re-inspection after factory rework
  4. Release for shipment only after second inspection passed

Outcome

Buyer avoided receiving defective inventory. The cost of two inspection days was a fraction of what a return, rework, or Amazon suspension would have cost. Buyer now includes pre-shipment QC in every PO.

Consolidation Multi-Supplier

Case 3: Multi-Supplier Consolidation Shipping

Buyer profile: Wholesale importer building a mixed-SKU order from 6 different Yiwu suppliers.

The challenge

Sourcing from multiple suppliers means multiple shipments, higher per-unit freight cost, and customs complexity for each separate container or LCL shipment.

Yeatru solution

  1. Coordinated 6 suppliers to deliver goods to Yeatru's Yiwu consolidation warehouse
  2. 15-day free warehousing while all suppliers delivered
  3. Checked each supplier's goods against PO upon receipt (quantity, packaging, labels)
  4. Consolidated all goods into one LCL shipment
  5. Single customs declaration and DDP delivery to buyer's warehouse

Result

Buyer received one consolidated shipment with one set of documents instead of six separate shipments. Reduced shipping paperwork, customs fees, and last-mile delivery complexity.
