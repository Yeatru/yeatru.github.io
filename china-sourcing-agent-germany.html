Navigating EU Compliance from the First Sample

Germany and the EU have some of the strictest product safety and import rules in the world. CE marking WEEE registration for electrical goods REACH SVHC compliance for chemicals LFBG/LFGB food-contact testing and GPSR (General Product Safety Regulation) are often deal-breakers for new importers. We help German and DACH clients navigate every step: coordinating compliant factories securing test reports at SGS/TÜV/Intertek partner labs preparing EU Declaration of Conformity documentation and filing correct customs entries with your EORI.

For Amazon.de FBA sellers we also do full FNSKU labeling polybags “Erstickungsgefahr” German-language choking warning stickers required by Amazon Germany and case-ready palletization for FRA3 / HAM2 / STR1 / VIE2 or any other FBA code.

DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) — What Is & Is NOT Included

When we provide a written DDP quote specific to your product destination and weight the total price explicitly INCLUDES the following 6 items:

Included in DDP

  • International freight (air / sea / rail / express)

Frequently Asked Questions — Germany · Austria · Switzerland · DACH Region

How do you handle EU compliance (CE / REACH / WEEE) for my product?

Before production we review whether your product category is regulated (toys electronics cosmetics PPE food contact medical). If yes we only send you quotes from factories that already hold valid CE/LFGB/ROHS test reports. If certification must be freshly issued we coordinate TÜV/SGS/Intertek testing at cost — you see the original lab invoice no markup. DoC files are yours to keep.

Can you do DDP delivery to a residential address in Germany or Austria?

Yes. Our DDP DE/AT service includes: China export international freight EU customs clearance EU import duty 19% / 20% VAT and final-mile courier via DHL/DHL Parcel/DPD/GLS/Hermes to any PLZ in Germany and Austria. Switzerland requires separate customs paperwork (CH EORI) — just mention it upfront.

I am a private individual (Privatperson) in Germany no registered business. Can I still import?

Yes. For private individuals in the EU small sample orders under ~€22 are usually duty-free and VAT-free. Larger commercial shipments (≥ €150) are much simpler with a registered business + EU EORI but we can still ship DDP to your home address using an importer-of-record. We’ll explain the options before you pay anything.
