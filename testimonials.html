How We Collect & Use Client Feedback

1. Post-shipment survey

Within 14 days of goods arriving at the destination we email a 10-question survey (price, QC, communication, speed, accuracy, recommendation). Replies feed directly into the case studies above — we never use AI-generated content or paid reviews.

2. Quarterly business review (QBR)

For clients with 2+ orders per year, Neil Liu runs a 30-minute Zoom/QBR every quarter covering KPIs, defect trends, re-order forecast, and price-change expectations. Notes are shared in a written PDF.

3. Reference & verification calls

Any qualified prospect (with a registered business, company website, and target MOQ ≥ 50 pieces) can request an anonymised reference call with one of our existing clients. We introduce you via LinkedIn or email, not cold calls.
