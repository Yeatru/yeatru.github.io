Incoterms® 2020 & Payment Methods

Supported Trade Terms

  • EXW — Ex Works (supplier's factory)
  • FOB — Free On Board (port of loading)
  • FCA — Free Carrier (any named place)
  • CIF — Cost, Insurance & Freight
  • DAP — Delivered At Place (your warehouse)
  • DPU — Delivered at Place Unloaded
  • DDP — Recommended Delivered Duty Paid (landed)
