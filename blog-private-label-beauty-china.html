Private Label Beauty & Cosmetics from China: Compliance Guide

Private label beauty & cosmetics OEM from China: skincare, makeup, haircare factories in Guangzhou/Shanghai/Yiwu, MOQ 300-5000, FDA/CPNP compliance, formula dev & landed cost.

Key Takeaways

  • China is the world's largest beauty OEM hub — Guangzhou (1,000+ skincare/makeup factories), Shanghai (premium skincare R&D), and Yiwu (beauty tools, accessories, packaging) each specialize in different categories. Match the hub to your product.
  • MOQ by category: skincare 300–1,000 pcs/SKU, lipstick & color cosmetics 1,000–5,000, makeup brushes & tools 500–2,000. Total lead time runs 30–60 days.
  • Formula development: a factory's in-house formula is free with MOQ (3–7 days to sample); a custom formula from an independent lab costs $500–$2,000 and takes 2–4 weeks. Learn the full OEM flow in our OEM private label China guide.
  • Compliance is non-negotiable: FDA cosmetic registration (US), CPNP notification (EU), INCI labeling, stability + microbial testing, and heavy-metal limits (lead, arsenic, mercury) under EU Regulation 1223/2009. See FDA import from China for filing steps.
  • Packaging & branding: primary pack $0.30–$2.00/pc (custom mold $1,000–$5,000); logo via silk-screen $0.05–$0.15/pc, hot-stamp $0.10–$0.25/pc, or label $0.05–$0.10/pc. Read our private label packaging guide for the full cost breakdown.

What Is Private Label Beauty from China?

Private label beauty (also called cosmetics OEM or white-label skincare) means a Chinese factory manufactures a product — serum, lipstick, shampoo, makeup brush — and labels it with your brand. You choose the formula (or provide your own), packaging, color, scent, and logo; the factory handles production, filling, and assembly. China accounts for roughly 40% of global beauty OEM output, with clusters in Guangzhou, Shanghai, and Yiwu that cover every category from skincare to color cosmetics to beauty tools.

Formula Development: In-House vs Custom

Your biggest early decision is whether to use the factory's ready-made formula or develop a custom one.

  • In-house / stock formula — the factory's own R&D formula, usually free once you meet the MOQ. Sampling takes 3–7 days. Best for fast launches and price-sensitive buyers; the downside is that dozens of brands may sell an identical formula.
  • Custom formula — developed by an independent cosmetics lab to your spec (e.g., "20% vitamin C serum with hyaluronic acid"). Lab fee $500–$2,000, development 2–4 weeks, plus stability and compatibility testing. Best for brands that need a differentiated hero product.

Rule of thumb: use in-house formulas for your first 1–2 SKUs to validate the market, then invest in a custom formula once you have sales data. A custom formula is also easier to defend against copycats.

Compliance: FDA (US) & CPNP (EU)

Cosmetics are regulated in every major market, and non-compliance means seizure at customs or a forced recall. The core requirements are:

MarketKey RequirementTypical CostTimeline
USA (FDA)Facility registration + product listing (MoCRA), ingredient listing, responsible person$300 – $1,5002 – 4 weeks
EU (CPNP)CPNP notification, INCI labeling, safety report (CPSR), EU responsible person$800 – $3,0002 – 6 weeks
China domestic (NMPA)NMPA cosmetic registration (special cosmetics) or filing (general cosmetics)$2,000 – $8,0002 – 6 months

Every batch needs INCI labeling (International Nomenclature of Cosmetic Ingredients), stability testing (typically 3 months accelerated), microbial testing, and heavy-metal limits — lead ≤ 10 ppm, arsenic ≤ 2 ppm, mercury ≤ 1 ppm under EU Regulation 1223/2009. Worked Example: Private-Label Vitamin C Serum

Let's land a 1,000-unit private-label 20% vitamin C serum (30 ml) into the US.

  • Formula: factory in-house 20% vitamin C + hyaluronic acid serum — free with MOQ
  • Bulk fill + primary glass bottle with dropper: $3.50/pc × 1,000 = $3,500
  • Custom silk-screen logo on bottle: $0.10/pc × 1,000 = $100
  • Secondary box + label: $0.50/pc × 1,000 = $500
  • Stability + microbial testing batch: $400
  • FDA facility registration & product listing: $500
  • Sea freight DDP to US (≈ 150 kg): $1.50/kg × 150 = $225
  • US duty (cosmetics, ~3%): ≈ $120

Total landed ≈ $5,345 for 1,000 units → $5.35/unit. Selling at $24.99 on Amazon gives a gross margin of ~78% before Amazon fees and advertising. A pre-shipment inspection ($180) catches label or fill-weight issues before the container sails.

Packaging & Branding Costs

Packaging often equals or exceeds the product cost in beauty. Budget for both layers:

  • Primary packaging — bottle, jar, tube that touches the product. Stock shapes $0.30–$2.00/pc; a custom mold costs $1,000–$5,000 (amortized over 5,000–10,000 pcs).
  • Secondary packaging — outer box, insert, label. $0.20–$0.80/pc depending on material and printing.
  • Branding decoration:
    • Silk-screen printing: $0.05–$0.15/pc (best for 1–2 colors on glass/plastic)
    • Hot-stamp (foil): $0.10–$0.25/pc (premium metallic look)
    • Adhesive label: $0.05–$0.10/pc (cheapest, good for multi-color)

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can I private label beauty products from China?

Yes. China is the world's largest beauty OEM hub. Guangzhou alone has 1,000+ skincare and makeup factories, Shanghai leads premium skincare R&D, and Yiwu dominates beauty tools and packaging. Most factories offer full private-label service from formula to packaging to logo printing.

2. What is the typical MOQ for beauty OEM in China?

MOQ varies by category: skincare (serum, cream, mask, cleanser) 300–1,000 pcs/SKU; lipstick and color cosmetics 1,000–5,000 pcs; makeup brushes and beauty tools 500–2,000 pcs. Lower MOQs are possible for stock formulas or when combining SKUs.

3. How much does a custom skincare formula cost?

A factory's in-house formula is usually free once you meet the MOQ (3–7 days to sample). A fully custom formula from an independent lab costs $500–$2,000 in lab fees and takes 2–4 weeks, including stability and compatibility testing.

4. Do I need FDA registration to import cosmetics from China to the US?

Yes. US cosmetic facility registration and product listing are required under FDA MoCRA. You also need an INCI ingredient declaration, batch records, and a responsible person. For the EU, file CPNP notification and comply with Regulation 1223/2009.

5. How much does beauty packaging and logo printing cost?

Primary packaging (bottle, jar, tube) runs $0.30–$2.00/pc; a custom mold adds $1,000–$5,000. Logo decoration: silk-screen $0.05–$0.15/pc, hot-stamp $0.10–$0.25/pc, adhesive label $0.05–$0.10/pc. Secondary boxes add $0.20–$0.80/pc.

6. What is the lead time for private label beauty from China?

Total lead time is 30–60 days: formula development or sample approval 2–4 weeks, production 3–4 weeks, and packaging/assembly 1 week. Adding a custom mold extends it by another 2–3 weeks. Plan 8–12 weeks for a first custom order.

Private-label beauty from China gives brands access to the world's deepest cosmetics OEM supply chain — but success depends on matching the right hub to your category, locking MOQ and lead time early, and never skipping FDA/CPNP compliance. With skincare MOQs as low as 300 pcs and lead times of 30–60 days, a first launch is achievable on a modest budget.
