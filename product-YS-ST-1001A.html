Product Details

Overview

Our heavy-duty metal supermarket shopping trolley is a core retail supply designed for hypermarkets, grocery stores, convenience shops, wholesale markets and retail chain stores. As a professional trolley manufacturer, we provide a full range of global mainstream cart styles, complete size options, replaceable silent casters and custom logo service, one-stop solution for all retail store shopping cart demands.

About This Product

Material & Craft

The whole cart frame adopts thickened steel wire with full automatic welding process, then treated by hot-dip galvanization to achieve excellent rust & corrosion resistance, suitable for long-term daily commercial use. The base support frame is thick bending steel pipe, strong impact resistance, not easy to bend or break under frequent heavy loading.

Humanized Design

  1. Multi-color plastic grip handle, comfortable to push for long hours;
  2. Built-in child safety seat on the front basket to carry kids safely;
  3. Anti-collision plastic protective caps on four basket corners to protect store shelves & wall;
  4. Stackable structure, dozens of carts can be nested together to save storage space when not in use;
  5. Optional silent TPR/PU casters, low noise, floor-friendly, flexible rotation in narrow supermarket aisles.

Customization Service

  • Logo printing on the front cart panel;
  • Custom plastic parts color (red/green/blue/orange available);
  • Custom basket dimension & load capacity for special store needs;
  • Mixed style bulk order support for retail chain groups.

Application Scenarios

Supermarket, grocery store, mini mart, wholesale mall, hardware store, pet shop, retail boutique, warehouse storage, shopping mall retail zone.

Package & Shipment

All shopping carts are wrapped with bubble film separately before stacking, then loaded into containers neatly. We have large quantity ready stock for fast bulk delivery, stable production capacity to support long-term wholesale cooperation for Amazon resellers, offline retail distributors & supermarket project purchasers.
