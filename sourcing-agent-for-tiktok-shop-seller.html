Shipping & Rate Disclaimer

The freight rate tables and transit ETAs shown on this page are indicative planning ranges only, published for H2 2026 general reference. They are NOT a formal binding quote. DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) — What Is & Is NOT Included

When we provide a written DDP quote specific to your product, destination and weight, the total price explicitly INCLUDES the following 6 items:

Included in DDP
  1. International freight (air / sea / rail / express)
  2. China export customs clearance + export permits (per HS)
  3. Destination country import customs clearance filing
  4. Destination import duty (per applicable HS schedule)
  5. Destination VAT / GST / Sales tax
  6. Final‑mile delivery to the buyer's exact door address
EXCLUDED Unless Stated
  • Storage / warehousing fees after destination delivery
  • Demurrage beyond carrier free‑days
  • Product compliance certs (CE, UKCA, FCC, FDA, RoHS, SASO, SFDA, UKCA, CCC, Halal…)
  • Bonded‑warehouse deferment, VAT‑ID rep fees, importer‑of‑record setup
  • Returns, re‑shipments or reverse logistics
Specific Market Prep Capabilities
  • Bundle assembly + TikTok Shop product photography (optional upgrade)
  • TikTok Shop packaging: QR-code return label insertion, polybags
  • DDP to all TikTok Shop cross-border warehouses (FBT/FBP programs)
  • Emergency "viral hit" priority service (7-day LCL consolidation) at +10% freight
Destination Customs & Tax — TikTok Shop Global Markets · US / UK / DE / ES / IT / FR / SG / MY / TH / VN / PH / ID / AE / SA / MX

For TikTok Shop cross-border FBP (Fulfillment by Platform) and FBT, we ship DDP to the nearest TikTok-operated warehouse — so TikTok's marketplace de minimis or IOSS/VAT regime typically handles the destination taxes. For seller-fulfilled TikTok Shop orders, we prepare the full commercial invoice + HS-code declaration for you (or ship DDP if you prefer).
