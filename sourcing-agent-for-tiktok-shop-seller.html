DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) — What Is & Is NOT Included

When we provide a written DDP quote specific to your product destination and weight the total price explicitly INCLUDES the following 6 items:

Included in DDP

  • International freight (air / sea / rail / express)
  • China export customs clearance + export permits (per HS)
  • Destination country import customs clearance filing
  • Destination import duty (per applicable HS schedule)
  • Destination VAT / GST / Sales tax
  • Final‑mile delivery to the buyer’s exact door address

EXCLUDED Unless Stated

  • Storage / warehousing fees after destination delivery
  • Demurrage beyond carrier free‑days
  • Product compliance certs (CE UKCA FCC FDA RoHS SASO SFDA UKCA CCC Halal…)
  • Bonded‑warehouse deferment VAT‑ID rep fees importer‑of‑record setup
  • Returns re‑shipments or reverse logistics

Specific Market Prep Capabilities

  • Bundle assembly + TikTok Shop product photography (optional upgrade)
  • TikTok Shop packaging: QR-code return label insertion polybags
  • DDP to all TikTok Shop cross-border warehouses (FBT/FBP programs)
  • Emergency “viral hit” priority service (7-day LCL consolidation) at +10% freight

Frequently Asked Questions — TikTok Shop Global Markets · US / UK / DE / ES / IT / FR / SG / MY / TH / VN / PH / ID / AE / SA / MX

I have a viral video right now and need product in 10 days max. Can you do it?

If the item is in stock in Yiwu (most common TikTok viral products are) YES. We do “priority sourcing + priority air freight”: pull stock same day QC overnight ship next day via DHL/FedEx economy. Typical door-to-door: 5–9 working days. This costs +10% vs standard but saves your launch. If the product must be OEM made 12–20 days is the floor — not 10. We’ll tell you which applies BEFORE you pay.

I want to private label a viral trending product. What is the OEM MOQ and price?

For most TikTok viral goods (beauty tools kitchen gadgets organisers) OEM private label (custom logo printed on product + custom packaging box) MOQ is 500–1,000 pcs and the cost premium vs plain in-stock items is usually +15%–35% on ex-factory price. Send us the exact photo and we’ll get 3 factory quotes with comparison table in 48 hours.

Can you handle TikTok Shop FBP/FBT warehouse requirements?

Yes. TikTok Shop’s FBP (Fulfillment by Platform) and FBT have strict inbound rules (SKU barcodes polybag standards and per-case weight limits). We follow the official TikTok Shop Seller University guidelines for the relevant marketplace and label everything case-ready so your shipment won’t get rejected at the TikTok Shop warehouse receiving dock.
