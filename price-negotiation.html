FAQ

Price Negotiation FAQs

How much can you save on prices?

Savings vary by product, order size, and supplier relationship. We collect multiple quotes and negotiate factory-direct pricing, then share a transparent cost breakdown so you can compare.

Do suppliers pay your fee or do I?

For Plan B (full sourcing) the supplier pays a 0% commission fee to us. You pay nothing extra. For Plan C (you have a supplier) we charge a small flat fee per negotiation.

Can you negotiate with suppliers I already have?

Yes. We offer Plan C — we coordinate with your existing suppliers audit their pricing negotiate better terms and follow up on quality. 3% service fee.

Will the supplier know I’m using a negotiation agent?

Optionally. We can negotiate anonymously using our name or introduce ourselves as your buying partner. Many Chinese suppliers actually prefer working with agents for smoother communication.

What if I can’t reach my target price?

We’ll be transparent. If target is unrealistic we’ll explain why and suggest a workable alternative. We never promise prices we can’t deliver — that destroys long-term relationships.
