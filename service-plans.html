FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our service plans and pricing

Plan 1 (Order Management Service, 3-4%): You already have supplier links. We arrange orders, follow production, and conduct basic inspection. Minimum USD 150 per order.

Plan 2 (Full Sourcing Service, 4-8% tiered): You only have photos or model names. We source manufacturers from scratch, compare prices, verify specifications, and handle everything end-to-end. Minimum USD 300 per formal order.

Plan 2 uses a tiered commission structure based on order value:

  • 7% ~ 8% for order value below USD 5,000
  • 5% ~ 6% for orders between USD 5,000 – 20,000
  • 4% for orders over USD 20,000

The minimum service fee is USD 300 per formal order. Larger orders benefit from lower commission rates.

Continuous full-catalogue inquiry for dozens or hundreds of items consumes massive manpower. More importantly, factories will treat us as "price shoppers" without real orders, which means they won't offer their best pricing.

To provide you with accurate, competitive quotes, we need you to first select target items and confirm estimated order quantities for each model. This ensures factories take the inquiry seriously and offer their best rates.

To help us serve you efficiently, please prepare:

  1. Product styles/models — clear photos, reference links, or design files
  2. Estimated order quantity for each item (MOQ-related)
  3. Specification requirements — material, size, color, packaging
  4. Target market (affects certification requirements)
  5. Budget range and timeline

Check our detailed sourcing preparation checklist for more guidance.

Yes, you can switch between Plan 1 and Plan 2 based on your project needs. Some clients use Plan 2 for initial product sourcing, then switch to Plan 1 for repeat orders once the supplier is established. There are no lock-in contracts.

No hidden fees. The commission covers our core service. The only additional costs are third-party expenses passed through at cost, such as:

  • Lab testing fees (if certification is required)
  • Specialized third-party inspection (beyond standard AQL 2.5)
  • Express shipping for samples (if requested)

All third-party costs are transparently shared with you before any commitment.
