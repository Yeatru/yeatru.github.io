Air Freight from China: Rates, Transit & When to Use (2026)

Air freight costs, transit times, vs sea vs express.

Key Takeaways

  • Air freight from China costs $4–$9 per kg on standard routes (Hong Kong/PVG/PEK → US/EU) in 2026, vs $0.50–$1.50/kg for sea freight — but it ships in 3–7 days vs 25–40 days.
  • Chargeable weight uses the greater of actual weight or volumetric weight (length × width × height ÷ 6000 for IATA standard). A large, light box of plush toys can cost 4× its real weight.
  • Air freight beats express courier (DHL/FedEx) on shipments over 45–100 kg, and beats sea freight when you need stock in under 2 weeks or face a TikTok Shop trending-window deadline.
  • Use air freight for high-value-per-kg goods (electronics, jewelry, samples), seasonal launches, and first test orders — not for heavy, low-value cargo like ceramics or hardware.
  • Always quote DDP (door-to-door, duty included) vs FOB airport to compare apples-to-apples; the difference can be $1.50–$3/kg in customs/delivery on top of the airline rate.

What Is Air Freight from China?

Air freight (air cargo) is the shipment of goods via cargo aircraft — either belly cargo in the hold of passenger planes or freighter aircraft (dedicated cargo jets). From China, the main export gateways are Hong Kong (HKG), Shanghai Pudong (PVG), Guangzhou Baiyun (CAN), Shenzhen (SZX), and Beijing (PEK). Freight is sold by chargeable weight (the higher of actual gross weight or volumetric weight, L × W × H ÷ 6000), not by package or CBM like sea freight.

How to Calculate Chargeable Weight (With Example)

The single biggest pricing surprise for first-time buyers is volumetric (dimensional) weight. Airlines charge on whichever is greater — actual weight or volume weight.

Formula (IATA standard): Volumetric weight (kg) = (L × W × H in cm) ÷ 6000

Example: A box of plush toys measures 60 × 50 × 40 cm and weighs 8 kg actual.

  • Volumetric weight = (60 × 50 × 40) ÷ 6000 = 20 kg
  • Actual weight = 8 kg
  • Chargeable weight = max(20, 8) = 20 kg
  • At $6/kg to the US → $120 freight on an 8 kg box

This is why low-density goods (plush, pillows, hollow toys, lampshades) often cost more to air-ship than their unit price suggests. For these, consider sea freight LCL instead.

When Should You Use Air Freight from China?

Air freight is the right call in five scenarios:

  1. High value-per-kg goods — electronics, jewelry, branded apparel. A $40 smartphone costs $6 to air-ship but $1.50 by sea; the 30-day sea delay costs more in working capital than the $4.50 freight difference.
  2. Seasonal / trending launches — if a TikTok Shop trend has a 3-week window, 25-day sea freight means you miss it entirely.
  3. Urgent restock — Amazon FBA sellers whose bestseller drops to 0 units lose rank in 48 hours; air freight restocks before the Buy Box disappears.
  4. Samples and pre-production approvals — air is the only practical mode for 2–5 kg sample boxes from a new verified supplier.
  5. Trade-show / event stock — Canton Fair follow-up orders, holiday pop-up inventory, Kickstarter fulfillment deadlines.

Conversely, do not air-ship heavy, low-value-per-kg goods (ceramics, hardware, large furniture, bottled liquids). The volumetric penalty makes the unit landed cost uncompetitive.

Air Freight Process from China: 7 Steps

  1. Booking & space confirmation — your forwarder books space with the airline 3–5 days before cargo ready date; peak season (Sep–Dec) requires 7–10 days lead.
  2. Factory to airport trucking — inland haulage from Yiwu/Shenzhen/Guangzhou factory to PVG/HKG/CAN airport (0.5–1 day, $80–$250).
  3. Export customs clearance — China export declaration via the forwarder's broker (same day, $40–$80).
  4. Terminal handling & security X-ray — airport cargo terminal fee ($0.20–$0.40/kg) + mandatory security screening.
  5. Flight departure — main-deck or belly cargo on scheduled or charter flight.
  6. Import customs clearance at destination — duty/tax payment, HS-code declaration (1–2 days for DDP).
  7. Last-mile delivery — airport to your warehouse / Amazon FBA center (1–3 days).

Total door-to-door: 5–10 days on standard air, 3–6 days on express air.

How to Reduce Air Freight Costs from China

  • Consolidate to break the 45 kg / 100 kg breakpoints — airlines drop the per-kg rate at 45, 100, 300, and 500 kg thresholds. Combining two 30 kg boxes into one 60 kg shipment often cuts the rate by 15–25%.
  • Optimize packaging density — redesign carton dimensions to hit a volumetric weight closer to actual weight. Removing 5 cm of void space on a 50×40×30 box saves ~1.7 kg of chargeable weight.
  • Ship from the cheapest gateway — HKG and PVG have the most capacity and lowest rates; CAN is competitive for South China factories; PEK for North China.
  • Use a China-based consolidator — Yeatru's warehousing & consolidation service combines your orders from multiple factories into one air shipment, hitting the 100 kg+ discount tier.
  • Book 7+ days ahead in peak season — Q4 rates spike 30–60% and space sells out; early booking locks in general-cargo rates.
  • Compare DDP vs FOB-airport — if you have a destination broker, FOB-airport + your own clearance can save $1–$2/kg; if not, DDP is simpler and often net-cheaper.

Air Freight Restrictions & Dangerous Goods

Airlines enforce IATA DG (Dangerous Goods) rules strictly. Common China-export items that are restricted or require special handling:

  • Lithium batteries (power banks, Bluetooth speakers, toys with Li-ion) — UN3480/UN3481, requires battery shipping compliance docs, MSDS, and often a DG broker; many passenger-belly flights refuse them.
  • Magnets & magnetic materials — require magnetic testing certificate.
  • Liquids, aerosols, perfumes — restricted on passenger flights; ok on freighters with proper packing.
  • Counterfeit / IP-infringing goods — seized at customs; verify IP protection before shipping.

Non-compliance means cargo is offloaded, fined, and blacklisted — always declare DG correctly and work with a forwarder certified for your product class.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How much does air freight from China cost per kg?

In 2026, standard general-cargo air freight from China to the US runs $5.00–$7.50/kg; to the EU $4.00–$6.00/kg; to the Middle East $3.50–$5.00/kg. Express air adds $2–$3/kg. DDP door-to-door adds another $1–$3/kg for customs and last-mile.

2. How long does air freight from China take?

Airport-to-airport: 2–4 days to the US, 3–5 days to the EU, 2–4 days to Southeast Asia. Door-to-door DDP adds 2–3 days for customs and delivery, so plan 5–8 business days total from cargo-ready to your warehouse.

3. How is air freight chargeable weight calculated?

Airlines charge on the greater of actual gross weight or volumetric weight (L × W × H in cm ÷ 6000). A 60×50×40 cm box weighing 8 kg is charged at 20 kg. Always ask your forwarder for both numbers before booking.

4. Air freight vs DHL express — which is cheaper?

For shipments under 45 kg, DHL/FedEx/UPS express is usually cheaper and faster (2–5 days, door-to-door, no separate clearance). Above 45–100 kg, air freight's per-kg rate drops below express and becomes the better choice.

5. Can I air-ship lithium-battery products from China?

Yes, but only with full DG compliance: UN3480/UN3481 classification, MSDS, UN38.3 test report, and a DG-certified forwarder. Many passenger-belly flights refuse Li-ion; freighter aircraft accept it. Expect $0.50–$1.50/kg DG surcharge.

6. When should I choose air freight over sea freight?

Choose air when: (a) goods are high-value-per-kg, (b) you need stock within 7 days, (c) it's a seasonal/trending launch with a short window, or (d) the order is 45–500 kg. Choose sea freight for heavy, low-value-per-kg cargo with 25+ days of lead time.

Conclusion

Air freight from China is the fastest way to move 45–500 kg of high-value or time-sensitive cargo, costing $4–$9/kg and delivering in 3–7 days. The key to keeping it affordable is understanding chargeable weight, hitting consolidation breakpoints, and quoting DDP to compare real landed cost. If you're shipping samples, launching a trending product, or restocking a bestseller before it loses rank, air freight from PVG or HKG is almost always the right call.
