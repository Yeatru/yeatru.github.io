DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) — What Is & Is NOT Included

When we provide a written DDP quote specific to your product destination and weight the total price explicitly INCLUDES the following 6 items:

Included in DDP

  • International freight (air / sea / rail / express)
  • China export customs clearance + export permits (per HS)
  • Destination country import customs clearance filing
  • Destination import duty (per applicable HS schedule)
  • Destination VAT / GST / Sales tax
  • Final‑mile delivery to the buyer’s exact door address

EXCLUDED Unless Stated

  • Storage / warehousing fees after destination delivery
  • Demurrage beyond carrier free‑days
  • Product compliance certs (CE UKCA FCC FDA RoHS SASO SFDA UKCA CCC Halal…)
  • Bonded‑warehouse deferment VAT‑ID rep fees importer‑of‑record setup
  • Returns re‑shipments or reverse logistics

Specific Market Prep Capabilities

  • FNSKU barcode labeling included in service fee for Amazon FBA orders
  • Carton content labels suffocation warnings polybags at $0.25–$1.20 per unit
  • Palletization & stretch-wrap for LTL FBA warehouse delivery
  • Can send to Amazon LAX9 / ONT8 / LGB8 / JFK8 / MDW2 / ORD2 OR any 3PL

Frequently Asked Questions — United States of America

Can you handle all Amazon FBA prep rules (labeling polybags choking warnings)?

Yes. We follow Amazon’s latest US Seller Central FBA prep guidelines including FNSKU labels polybag self-sealing choking-hazard warning stickers for children’s products carton weights ≤50 lbs and palletization for LTL. Prep pricing: $0.25–$1.20 per unit fully itemized on your invoice.

I don’t have a US LLC or EIN yet. Can you ship my first Amazon FBA order?

Yes. For small trial orders under $800 you can ship directly to your home address via Section 321 de minimis entry or we can work with your FBA importer-of-record. If you need a formal EIN-based importer we can also refer you to a US-based customs broker at no extra cost.

What is your QC process before shipping to my FBA warehouse?

Every batch goes through AQL 2.5/4.0 inspection: visual functional packaging quantity. You receive photos + short video BEFORE the balance is released. If the batch fails rework or replacement is at the factory’s cost — never yours.
