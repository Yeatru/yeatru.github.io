FAQ

Supplier Verification FAQs

How long does a supplier verification take?

Standard verification takes 3-5 business days. Expedited 24-48 hour service is available for urgent cases at an additional fee.

Do you visit the factory in person?

Yes. For premium and standard verification tiers we send a local inspector to physically visit the factory and capture photos video and on-site observations.

What if the supplier is fake or a trading company?

Our report will clearly state the supplier’s true nature. If the supplier misrepresented themselves we can recommend 2-3 verified alternative factories at no extra cost.

Can you verify suppliers outside Yiwu?

Yes. Our verification network covers all major Chinese manufacturing hubs including Guangzhou Shenzhen Yiwu Ningbo Shanghai Hangzhou and Qingdao.

What does the final report include?

A 15-30 page PDF with: company overview license verification on-site photos capacity analysis QC system assessment risk score and our go/no-go recommendation.
