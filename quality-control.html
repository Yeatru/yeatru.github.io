Inside a Real Inspection

What We Actually Check — Our Own Photos

Taken during pre-shipment inspections on live orders. No stock photography, no staged studio shots. Dimensions, weight, colour consistency and carton labelling are checked by hand.

On-site factory check Measuring a finished mat against the approved size before the lot is packed. Tape measure and digital scale — not a visual glance.

Carton dimensions Outer cartons are measured and recorded so freight CBM and volume weight come from the actual carton, not the supplier’s estimate.

Style & colour check A production lot laid out box by box. Styles, colours and heel heights are compared against the approved sample.

Pair-by-pair review Every sampled pair is opened and checked — stitching, hardware, sole alignment — before the carton is passed.

Finish & colour consistency Colour variants checked side by side on protective paper to catch shade drift between batches.

Unit weight Units are weighed on a bench scale to confirm the weight stated on the spec sheet, which also drives freight cost.

Passed & labelled Cartons are sealed, labelled and staged for consolidation once the lot passes inspection.

FAQ

Quality Control FAQs

What is AQL 2.5?

AQL (Acceptable Quality Level) 2.5 is the industry standard for consumer products. It means the buyer accepts no more than 2.5% defective items in the sample. We offer AQL 1.0 1.5 2.5 and 4.0 depending on your standards.

How much does a QC inspection cost?

Standard PSI starts at $200 per man-day. Most inspections require 1-2 inspectors for 1 day. AQL 2.5 PSI on a 5,000-unit order typically costs $200-300.

Can you inspect Amazon FBA shipments?

Yes. We offer 100% piece-by-piece inspection for Amazon FBA plus FNSKU labeling poly bagging and box content verification to Amazon’s strict requirements.

What if my inspection fails?

We’ll issue a fail report and provide specific defect details. The factory must rework or replace defective units. We then re-inspect at no extra charge within 7 days.

Do you have certified inspectors?

Yes. All our inspectors are AQSIQ-trained with 5+ years experience. We use calibrated measuring tools and follow ISO 2859-1 sampling standards.
