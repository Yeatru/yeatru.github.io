Product Description

Multi-Pocket High Visibility Reflective Safety Work Vest

Our professional hi-vis reflective safety vest meets daily industrial safety demands, designed for all outdoor & indoor low-light working scenarios, bringing reliable visibility protection for workers.

Superior 360° Night Visibility

The vest adopts wide high-gloss reflective tapes around torso and shoulders, forming full 360° reflective coverage. Vehicle headlights can catch the reflective signal from 300 meters away, greatly reducing collision risks during night construction, traffic control and warehouse night shift work, performs stably on rainy, foggy and dim light days.

Humanized Multi-Pocket Storage System

No more messy scattered small tools! This reflective vest comes with a full set of functional pockets:
  1. Transparent PVC ID badge pocket on left chest for quick identity check
  2. Dedicated pen slot for marking pens, markers
  3. 3 large deep side pockets to hold tape measure, walkie talkie, blueprint, phone and small hardware tools
  4. Smooth central zipper closure enables fast wearing and taking off, avoid accidental opening during work movement.

Comfortable & Wear Resistant Material

Made of lightweight breathable polyester fabric, the loose sleeveless cut keeps air circulation without overheating even in summer long-hour work. All edges are finely wrapped with tight stitching, the reflective strips are firmly sewn and not easy to fall off after repeated washing, durable for long-term frequent use.

Custom Logo & Abundant Color Selection

Large blank printing area on front chest and back supports custom screen printing or heat transfer logo, company name, safety slogans, perfect for team uniform matching. We provide over 15 color options: fluorescent yellow, orange, red, light blue, dark blue, gold, dark green, black, pink, purple and color splicing styles, also supply mesh breathable lightweight styles for summer hot weather.

Universal Working Scenarios

Perfect safety apparel for construction sites, highway traffic police, sanitation workers, warehouse forklift operators, airport logistics staff, surveyors, road maintenance crews, cycling riders, event volunteers, parking attendants and outdoor night patrol staff.
