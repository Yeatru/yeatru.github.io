Kuwait: the compliance checks that actually bite

KUCAS conformity

Kuwait runs a conformity assurance scheme: regulated products need a certificate of conformity issued through an accredited inspection body, usually arranged before shipment. Arranging it after the goods arrive is not an option, and the product categories covered are defined rather than universal.

G-Mark for electricals

Low-voltage electrical products sold in GCC states carry the G-Mark under the Gulf technical regulation, and toys fall under a GCC toy regulation of their own. Apparel is simpler: fibre composition and care labelling, with Arabic on the label.

Arabic labelling

Consumer information has to be in Arabic, commonly Arabic and English together. For apparel that means composition, care and origin; for anything with instructions, a translated leaflet. Labels are printed before production, not applied on arrival.

Frequently asked questions

Can you handle orders in Arabic?

Yes. The verified order on this page was placed by a buyer who writes in Arabic and also works in English, and the specification, quotation and label questions were handled bilingually. Arabic is also the language the product label has to carry for the Kuwaiti market.

Is there VAT in Kuwait?

Kuwait currently applies no VAT, which is one reason landed cost is simpler than in the EU or Brazil. The GCC common tariff usually puts customs duty at 5% on most goods, with exemptions for some categories. The duty rate for your HS code is confirmed at quotation stage rather than assumed.

Do I need a conformity certificate before shipping?

For regulated products, yes. Kuwait’s conformity scheme requires a certificate of conformity from an accredited body, usually obtained before shipment. Low-voltage electrical goods additionally carry the GCC G-Mark, and toys fall under the Gulf toy regulation. We confirm which scheme applies before the factory is booked.

Can I mix models in one 100-piece order?

Yes — that was exactly the arrangement on the order here: 100 pieces split 50 + 50 across models, with no extra charge for mixing colours and sizes. A mixed run is a normal first order, not a special favour.

What does DDP to Kuwait include?

Freight, customs duty, clearance and last-mile delivery to the address you give, for one price. If you have your own clearing agent or want to control the entry, FOB or CIF is quoted as well, and we explain what each option leaves in your hands.
