**Frequently Asked Questions

Can I import from China with a small business budget?

Yes. Most sourcing agents now support orders as small as $500-$1,000. With 1688.com in-stock suppliers, MOQs can be as low as 2-5 pieces for standard items. Budget $800-$1,500 total for a first trial order including samples, production, freight, and a small QC check.

What is the cheapest sourcing agent for small orders?

Flat-fee agents charging $90-$200 per order are cheapest for orders under $3,000. Above that, a 3-5% commission agent becomes cheaper because the commission scales with value. Break-even for a $90 flat fee vs 5% commission is around $1,800.

Do I need a registered business to import?

No. You can import as a private individual. Small orders under €22 / $25 are usually duty-free and tax-free. Larger commercial shipments need EORI (EU) or ISF filing (US), but agents can handle this via importer-of-record.
