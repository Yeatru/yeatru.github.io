China Wholesale Products Catalog

Yeatru Sourcing offers 675 wholesale products from China at factory-direct prices. As a Yiwu-based China wholesale supplier, we connect global buyers with verified Chinese manufacturers across 50+ product categories. Whether you need wholesale apparel from China, wholesale kitchenware, wholesale electronics, wholesale toys, or wholesale home goods, our catalog covers every major category for importers, Amazon FBA sellers, TikTok Shop merchants, and brick-and-mortar retailers worldwide.

Our China wholesale product catalog includes detailed SKU information, MOQ (minimum order quantity), ex-factory USD pricing, materials, and specifications for every item. Prices are calculated transparently: CNY factory cost divided by 6.7 exchange rate, plus a 15% wholesale markup that includes sourcing service. Low MOQ wholesale from China starts from just 1 piece for many items, making us ideal for small batch wholesale and bulk product purchase from China.
