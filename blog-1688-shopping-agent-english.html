1688.com English Shopping Agent Guide 2026

Aug 13, 2026 Yeatru Sourcing 9 min read

Key Takeaways

  • 1688.com is Alibaba's domestic Chinese B2B platform; prices are typically lower than Alibaba.com because they target Chinese buyers, not export.
  • Foreign buyers cannot easily pay on 1688 with a foreign credit card or communicate in English — a 1688 shopping agent solves the language, payment, and logistics gap.
  • An agent translates listings, negotiates price and MOQ, arranges domestic pickup, inspects goods, and consolidates multiple suppliers into one export shipment.
  • 1688 MOQs are usually lower and aimed at domestic wholesale, but quality varies, so pre-shipment inspection matters even more.
  • Common pitfalls: misleading photos, hidden domestic shipping fees, and sellers who refuse export — an agent filters these out before you pay.

What Is 1688.com and Why Use an Agent?

1688.com is China's largest domestic B2B wholesale platform (Alibaba Group's Chinese-language site), offering factory-direct prices 15-30% lower than Alibaba.com's export prices. However, 1688 is entirely in Chinese, requires Chinese payment methods (Alipay/WeChat Pay), and most sellers do not ship internationally. A 1688 shopping agent provides English translation, international payment processing, quality inspection, and global shipping — making 1688's lower prices accessible to foreign buyers.

If you have browsed Alibaba.com and been shocked by the price gap between similar products, the reason is often simple: the same factory lists its real domestic price on 1688.com, Alibaba's China-only B2B platform, and a higher export price on Alibaba.com. A 1688 shopping agent in English is the bridge that lets a foreign buyer access those domestic prices, in English, without a Chinese bank account or Mandarin skills. This guide explains what 1688 is, why it is cheaper, and exactly how an agent helps you buy from it.

What Is 1688.com?

1688.com is Alibaba Group's domestic wholesale marketplace, launched in 1999 and named after a lucky number in Chinese business culture. Unlike Alibaba.com, which is built for international export, 1688 is built for Chinese buyers — domestic distributors, Taobao sellers, physical shops, and small manufacturers sourcing components. Listings are entirely in Mandarin, prices are quoted in RMB, and payment is settled through Chinese bank transfer, Alipay, or WeChat Pay. The product catalog is enormous and covers almost every manufactured category: home goods, electronics accessories, apparel, packaging, toys, beauty tools, hardware, and more.

Why 1688 Is Cheaper Than Alibaba.com

The price difference between 1688 and Alibaba.com is not random. It reflects three structural factors. First, 1688 prices are domestic ex-works prices with no export markup, no English-speaking sales overhead, and no commission built in for an export trading layer. Second, 1688 sellers expect domestic wholesale volumes and quote accordingly, while Alibaba sellers inflate prices to absorb the risk and cost of serving unknown foreign buyers. Third, 1688 has far more small factories and direct producers competing, which keeps prices aggressive. The result is that the same product is frequently 20% to 40% cheaper on 1688 than on Alibaba.com — but only if you can actually buy it.

The Three Barriers a 1688 Agent Solves

For a foreign buyer, 1688 has three hard barriers: language, payment, and logistics. A 1688 shopping agent removes all three.

1. Language

Listings, specifications, and seller chat are all in Mandarin. Machine translation misses trade-specific terms, MOQ conditions, and customization options. An agent reads the listing accurately, asks the seller the right questions, and confirms details such as material, size, and packaging before you commit.

2. Payment

1688 does not accept foreign credit cards or PayPal. Buyers pay through Alipay or a Chinese bank account, both of which require Chinese identity or a Chinese business entity. Your agent pays the 1688 seller in RMB and invoices you in USD (or your currency) using safe international payment methods. See our guide on how to pay Chinese suppliers safely.

3. Logistics

1688 sellers ship domestically only — to a Chinese address. Your agent receives the goods at a consolidation warehouse, inspects them, repacks if needed, and arranges export shipping to your country. Without an agent, you cannot receive 1688 goods overseas at all.

Step-by-Step: From Finding a Product to Delivery

  1. Send a product link or photo. Share a 1688 link, an Alibaba link, a competitor ASIN, or just a photo. The agent confirms whether the 1688 seller is real and matches your spec.
  2. Get a translated quote. The agent returns unit price, MOQ, lead time, and domestic shipping cost in English.
  3. Order samples (optional). Sample fees typically run USD 30-80 per SKU, often refundable on bulk orders from USD 500.
  4. Place the bulk order. Pay 50% deposit; the agent pays the 1688 seller in RMB and tracks production.
  5. Inspection at the warehouse. Goods arrive at the agent's consolidation center and are checked against your spec. AQL 2.5 inspection costs about USD 120 per man-day.
  6. Consolidate and export. Multiple 1688 suppliers are merged into one shipment. Free consolidation storage runs around 60 days.
  7. Ship and receive. Choose express, air, or sea. The agent handles export customs and hands off to your destination carrier or DDP service.

MOQ Differences on 1688

1688 MOQs are generally lower and more flexible than Alibaba export MOQs because the platform serves domestic wholesale. A product with a 500-piece MOQ on Alibaba may show 100-piece or even 1-lot (often 10-50 piece) MOQs on 1688. This makes 1688 attractive for small-batch testing, TikTok Shop launches, and Amazon FBA trial orders. Our low MOQ sourcing guide covers how agents negotiate MOQ down further using factory relationships.

Payment Methods: Why You Need an Agent

You cannot use a foreign Visa, Mastercard, or PayPal directly on 1688. Domestic payment requires Alipay (linked to a Chinese bank account) or a Chinese bank transfer. Some 1688 sellers accept WeChat Pay. For a foreign buyer, the only practical path is to pay your agent in USD by bank transfer or Trade Assurance, and let the agent settle the seller in RMB. This also gives you a single point of accountability if something goes wrong.

Common Pitfalls When Buying from 1688

  • Misleading photos: stock images that do not match the actual product. An agent requests real factory photos or a sample first.
  • Hidden domestic shipping: the unit price looks cheap but excludes domestic courier to the warehouse. The agent quotes landed-at-warehouse cost.
  • Sellers who refuse export: some 1688 sellers will not sell to anyone exporting, fearing IP or warranty issues. The agent filters these out.
  • Quality variance: 1688 has many small factories; quality control is essential before export.
  • No English after-sales: if a defect appears later, you have no direct channel. The agent handles warranty claims on your behalf.

Is a 1688 Agent Worth It?

For any order above a few hundred dollars, yes. The domestic price savings usually exceed the agent's 3-8% commission many times over, and you gain inspection, consolidation, and English support that you simply cannot get by buying 1688 direct. For very small orders under USD 50, the minimum order fee may eat into savings, so consolidate several SKUs into one shipment.
