One Agent for Every European Market

Europe is diverse: EU 27 (harmonised CE, 19%–25% VAT), UK (post-Brexit GB-EORI, 20% VAT), Nordics (Norway NO, Sweden SE VAT thresholds), Switzerland CH (separate CH-EORI and customs), Türkiye, the Balkans (RS, HR, BA, ME, MK, AL), and CIS countries (KZ, RU, UZ, AM, GE, AZ). Each has its own customs rules, VAT rate, currency, and product certification. We know them all, because we ship to 40+ European countries every month.

Whichever European country you are importing into, we: (1) find the best genuine Chinese source factory at the best price, (2) do AQL 2.5/4.0 pre-shipment QC, (3) arrange the optimal shipping mode — sea freight, China-Europe block-train railway (Horgos/Alashankou border), or air freight — and (4) deliver full DDP to your door or warehouse, with every cost item visible in writing BEFORE you pay anything.

DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) — What Is & Is NOT Included

When we provide a written DDP quote specific to your product, destination and weight, the total price explicitly INCLUDES the following 6 items:

Included in DDP
  1. International freight (air / sea / rail / express)
  2. China export customs clearance + export permits (per HS)
  3. Destination country import customs clearance filing
  4. Destination import duty (per applicable HS schedule)
  5. Destination VAT / GST / Sales tax
  6. Final‑mile delivery to the buyer's exact door address
EXCLUDED Unless Stated
  • Storage / warehousing fees after destination delivery
  • Demurrage beyond carrier free‑days
  • Product compliance certs (CE, UKCA, FCC, FDA, RoHS, SASO, SFDA, UKCA, CCC, Halal…)
  • Bonded‑warehouse deferment, VAT‑ID rep fees, importer‑of‑record setup
  • Returns, re‑shipments or reverse logistics
Specific Market Prep Capabilities
  • Amazon EU Pan-EU FBA prep (all 9 FBA marketplaces supported)
  • CE / UKCA / RoHS / REACH / WEEE coordination at cost
  • China-Europe Railway (Yiwu → Duisburg / Hamburg / Łódź / Budapest)
  • DDP to home, shop, or 3PL in all 40+ European countries
Destination Customs & Tax — Europe (EU 27 + UK + Norway + Switzerland + Türkiye + Balkans + Caucasus + Central Asia)

For EU DDP: duties and VAT rates vary by destination member state (VAT 17%–27%). DDP pricing includes Customs Duty + Import VAT based on the declared CIF value, local clearance, and final-mile courier. For non-EU Europe (CH, NO, TR, BA, RS, CIS, etc.) we prepare the specific local customs regime (CH EORI, NO VAT OSS, TR TÜRKVAT, RU EAEU CU Declaration) separately — always confirmed in writing with your DDP quote.
