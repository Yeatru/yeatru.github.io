DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) — What Is & Is NOT Included

When we provide a written DDP quote specific to your product destination and weight the total price explicitly INCLUDES the following 6 items:

Included in DDP

  • International freight (air / sea / rail / express)
  • China export customs clearance + export permits (per HS)
  • Destination country import customs clearance filing
  • Destination import duty (per applicable HS schedule)
  • Destination VAT / GST / Sales tax
  • Final‑mile delivery to the buyer’s exact door address

EXCLUDED Unless Stated

  • Storage / warehousing fees after destination delivery
  • Demurrage beyond carrier free‑days
  • Product compliance certs (CE UKCA FCC FDA RoHS SASO SFDA UKCA CCC Halal…)
  • Bonded‑warehouse deferment VAT‑ID rep fees importer‑of‑record setup
  • Returns re‑shipments or reverse logistics

  • Amazon EU Pan-EU FBA prep (all 9 FBA marketplaces supported)

Comparison Q&A — the questions EU buyers ask most

Is a China sourcing agent cheaper than a trading company?

Yes, in most cases. A trading company bakes a 15%–30% margin into the unit price you see, so you never know the true factory cost. A sourcing agent charges a transparent 3%–8% success fee on the ex-factory price, so the same SKU typically lands 10%–25% cheaper. Real example: a Swedish brand paid €12/unit through a distributor; the identical LED desk lamp was €3.10/unit ex-factory through our Yiwu team.

China sourcing agent vs Alibaba.com — which should I use?

Use both, for different jobs. Alibaba is a supplier directory; a sourcing agent is your buyer on the ground in China. Use Alibaba to discover product ideas, then let the agent negotiate the real ex-factory price, audit the factory, run AQL 2.5 QC, consolidate the cargo and ship DDP. Alibaba’s listed prices usually already include a 15%–30% trading-company margin.

Should I buy directly on 1688.com to save money?

1688 is China-domestic wholesale: prices run roughly 10%–20% below Alibaba, but listings are in Chinese only, need Alipay/CNY payment and domestic logistics, and come with no export documents. An agent buys on 1688 for you at those prices while handling translation, payment, QC, consolidation and export clearance.

China sourcing agent vs freight forwarder — do I need both?

They do different jobs. A forwarder only moves cargo once you already own the goods; an agent finds, negotiates, audits and QCs the factory and can also book freight. Most EU importers need the agent first and can keep their own forwarder — or use the agent’s DDP service that bundles customs, EU VAT and final-mile delivery.

Is a sourcing agent cheaper than hiring my own staff in China?

Usually yes. A China-based sourcing employee costs €40,000–€90,000 per year plus office, visa and benefits. A 3%–8% success fee on a €200,000 annual import budget is €6,000–€16,000 — and only when you actually place an order. You also get an existing QC and logistics team instead of a single new hire.

Do I still need Alibaba if I already work with a sourcing agent?

No. With an agent you receive quotes from multiple vetted factories — including 1688 and Yiwu wholesale sources that are not listed on Alibaba — so you do not depend on self-reported trade-assurance badges or pay an extra platform margin.

China sourcing agent vs Amazon FBA prep company?

A prep company only inspects, labels and ships; it does not source. A sourcing agent covers sourcing, CE/REACH/GPSR compliance, AQL QC and FBA prep together. If you already have a reliable supplier you are happy with, a prep-only provider can be cheaper for that single step.

China sourcing agent vs a dropshipping supplier?

A dropshipping supplier ships single units at a high per-unit cost with no brand or QC control. An agent buys 50–500 pcs at factory price with your own branding, so unit costs usually fall 40%–70% and you control packaging, manuals and compliance.

What does a China sourcing agent charge in 2026?

Typically 3%–8% of the ex-factory order value, or €500–€2,500 per month for continuous work. Inspection is pass-through at cost (US$99–US$299 per man-day) and freight is billed at carrier cost, with zero markup on the factory invoice.

Can a sourcing agent get a lower MOQ than Alibaba?

Yes. Alibaba listings frequently show 1,000+ MOQ. Through Yiwu wholesale channels and direct factory negotiation we regularly place 50–500 pcs trial orders, and first-time private-label runs can start even lower on stock items.

China sourcing agent vs buying from a European importer or distributor?

A European distributor typically adds 40%–120% margin. Buying direct from the Chinese factory through an agent removes that layer, so the landed DDP cost to a European address is usually 15%–25% lower for the same product.

Does a sourcing agent handle CE / REACH / GPSR compliance?

Yes. We verify the factory’s certificates (CE, LVD, EMC, RoHS, REACH, GPSR) and issue the Declaration of Conformity in your brand’s name. Trading companies and raw Alibaba listings normally leave that compliance risk with you.

Is a sourcing agent worth it for small orders?

Above roughly €1,500 it usually is. A 3% fee on €1,500 is €45, and that is more than offset by a 10%–25% lower unit cost plus QC that prevents a full-batch defect and costly rework.

China sourcing agent vs doing it yourself (DIY sourcing)?

DIY saves the 3%–8% fee but costs 40–120 hours per SKU, exposes you to factory-vetting fraud, and rarely gets you below public Alibaba pricing — so your first order often ends up more expensive, not less.

China sourcing agent vs a Yiwu market buying agent?

Yiwu buying agents specialise in the Yiwu wholesale market (small commodities, low MOQ, fast turnaround) but rarely handle factory audits, CE compliance or international DDP. We combine Yiwu market access with factory-level sourcing, QC and door-to-door delivery to Europe.

China sourcing agent vs a local sourcing consultant in Europe?

A European consultant advises but does not buy, inspect or ship. An agent in China negotiates in Mandarin on the ground, attends the factory, runs the inspection and consolidates the shipment — the execution layer a consultant cannot provide.

China sourcing agent vs AliExpress or Temu wholesale?

AliExpress and Temu are retail or B2B-lite channels with the platform margin built in and no factory relationship. Per-unit costs are typically 2–4 times the ex-factory price; an agent is the route to true wholesale pricing and your own brand.

Does an agent give me ownership of the tooling, moulds and supplier relationship?

Yes. We negotiate so moulds, tooling and IP stay with you, buy in your company’s name where required, and can hand the factory relationship over if you later build your own team. Trading companies usually keep that ownership themselves.

China sourcing agent vs Made-in-China.com or Global Sources?

Both are directories that surface factory listings, but a listed price is a starting point, not a negotiated price. An agent quotes the same factories at a 5%–15% lower effective price after negotiation and adds audit, AQL QC and DDP shipping.

How do I know the agent is not secretly marking up the factory price?

Ask for the factory invoice or a signed quotation on the factory’s letterhead. A reputable agent shows the ex-factory price and bills the success fee separately, so there is no hidden margin — and you can request the factory contact for verification.

China sourcing agent vs asking a Chinese friend or relative to buy for you?

Goodwill buys the product, not the process: no factory vetting, no AQL QC, no export documentation and no recourse if the batch is defective. An agent provides contracts, inspection reports and compliance documents you can enforce.

Frequently Asked Questions — Europe (EU 27 + UK + Norway + Switzerland + Türkiye + Balkans + Caucasus + Central Asia)

Can you use China-Europe Railway (block train) to ship to Europe?

Yes. The China–Europe Railway corridor (Yiwu → Horgos/Alashankou border → Duisburg/Hamburg/Łódź/Budapest/Málaga) is often 30%–50% cheaper than air freight and 10–15 days faster than sea freight for European destinations. Typical door-to-door: 18–28 calendar days. We book with Maersk COSCO and DB cargo rail partners. Full DDP service available on rail: customs clearance + EU VAT + duty + final-mile all included.

I need CE marking and DoC for the EU market. Do you handle it?

Yes. We only quote factories that hold the relevant compliance certificates (CE LVD EMC RoHS REACH GPSR as required). If a fresh certificate is required for your SKU (e.g. first-time private label) we coordinate with SGS/TÜV/Intertek partner labs at cost — original lab invoice zero markup. The Declaration of Conformity (DoC) is issued in your brand’s name kept on file permanently and copies sent with every shipment.

Do you ship to residential addresses in Europe (private individuals)?

Yes. Individual / private-person buyers across Europe can start with 50–200 pcs trial order shipped DDP door-to-door to their home address via DHL DPD GLS DHL Parcel PostNL or local courier. We do not require you to have a registered business or EU EORI for small sample orders — we explain importer-of-record options before you commit.
