What one-stop procurement removes from your desk

Buying from China as a Ghanaian importer usually means chasing a factory in a different time zone, trusting photos, and finding out at Tema that a document is missing. A procurement agency exists to close those gaps before the container leaves.

Supplier shortlist

Factories and market stalls compared on specification, capacity and export record; no supplier is proposed on price alone.

Negotiation in Chinese

Price, MOQ and payment terms argued with the factory directly. Our fee is a separate line, not a hidden margin inside the unit cost.

Sampling with evidence

A physical sample or a documented pre-production sample, photographed at the factory, so approval is based on the goods and not on a catalogue picture.

Inspection before balance

AQL 2.5 at the factory, dated photos and video, defects agreed with the supplier while the goods are still in China.

Consolidation

Several suppliers, one shipment. Cartons received and checked at the Yiwu warehouse, stored free for 15 days, packed for the Tema lane.

Documents that match the entry

Invoice, packing list and certificates prepared to the format Ghanaian clearance expects, so demurrage is not caused by a missing sheet.

Ghana: what decides the landed cost

The levy stack

Ghana's VAT is 15%, but NHIL, GETFund and the COVID-19 health recovery levy are charged alongside it, so the effective rate on the CIF value is around 21%. A quote built on 15% alone understates the cost by roughly six points before duty is counted.

Standards and conformity

The Ghana Standards Authority sets requirements for many consumer and electrical goods, and some categories need registration or a conformity certificate before release. Food, cosmetics and medical items additionally fall under FDA Ghana. Which route applies depends on the product, so it is decided at quotation stage.

Port and inland costs

Tema handles most container traffic; Takoradi serves the west. Port charges, terminal handling and the inland haulage to Kumasi or Tamale are frequently underestimated, and they are quoted as their own lines rather than folded into a single vague freight number.

How an order runs

  1. Brief — product, quantity, target landed cost in Accra, not just the FOB price.
  2. Quote — supplier comparison with the product, the freight, the fee and the tax estimate on separate lines.
  3. Sample — approved before production; label and packaging confirmed at the same time.
  4. Production and AQL 2.5 — dated evidence, rework agreed before balance.
  5. Shipment — consolidation at Yiwu, documents prepared, sea or air to Ghana.
