Product Overview

This 70L rolling plastic shopping basket is an all-purpose storage solution designed for commercial retail and household daily use. Made of heavy-duty impact-resistant plastic with full ventilated mesh walls, it combines large storage capacity, stackable space-saving design and smooth silent rolling PU wheels. The colorful interchangeable rim trims and custom logo printing service make it ideal branding equipment for supermarkets, convenience stores, laundromats and warehouse storage.

Multi-Scenario Usage
Commercial Retail: Supermarkets, grocery stores, liquor shops, cosmetic boutiques for customer shopping
Household Storage: Laundry basket for dirty clothes, garage tool bin, toy storage cart
Industrial & Service: Warehouse goods sorting, hotel linen transport, restaurant kitchen supply storage

Product Advantages
