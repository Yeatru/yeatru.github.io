Product Details

Product Introduction

Our cloud-shaped sink filter holder is a revolutionary kitchen drain tool to stop sink clogging. Adopting foldable 90° adjustable structure, it can be firmly fixed on sink corner with self-adhesive sticker, matching all standard kitchen sinks. Paired with disposable drain mesh bags, it separates food waste and wastewater instantly, freeing your hands from messy sink cleaning.


Wide Application Scenarios

Perfect for draining leftover soup, washing fruits & vegetables, filtering noodle waste, catching food residues after dish washing. Great accessory for family kitchen, dormitory, rental housing, catering kitchen. Replace bulky metal sink strainers, more hygienic and convenient.

Bullet Points

Foldable 90° Corner Design, Perfect Fit Sink Corner

Foldable 90° angle design matches most kitchen sink corners perfectly. Just press both arms to open and stick on sink wall, instantly form a suspended filter frame. No occupying sink space, drain water quickly while catching all food scraps. Freely fold flat when not in use, won’t block sink movement.

Safe Durable ABS Material & Multi Hook Design

Made of premium food-grade ABS plastic, sturdy, drop-resistant, non-toxic, long service life. Each arm equipped with multiple tear-drop hooks to fix disposable mesh bags tightly, prevent slipping or falling during draining. Smooth surface easy to wipe clean, no food residue hidden.

Solve Sink Clogging Trouble, Widely Used for All Food Waste

Comes with disposable 10*15cm mesh filter bags, catch noodles, vegetable scraps, leftover food, eggshells, fruit peels efficiently. No more manually picking waste from sink drain, avoid sewer blockage, reduce pipeline maintenance trouble. Ideal for kitchen washing, salad rinsing, noodle soup draining, fruit cleaning.

Self-Adhesive Installation, No Drilling & No Damage

Strong self-adhesive back on cloud top, just tear off sticker and paste on clean dry sink wall. No drill, no nail, no damage to stainless steel sink or countertop. Firm stickiness supports long-term use, easy to remove without residual glue. Compact size: 15.6cm arm length, 9.8cm cloud width, 3 stylish colors (Dark Green/Orange/Gray) to match kitchen decor.

Practical Kitchen Helper, Easy Storage & Wide Application

Lightweight foldable design, fold flat to save cabinet space when idle. Complete set includes 1pcs sink filter holder + multiple disposable mesh bags. Suitable for home kitchen, apartment, rental house, restaurant back kitchen. Replace metal sink strainer, cleaner and more hygienic, one-time mesh bag directly discard waste without touching dirty scraps.
