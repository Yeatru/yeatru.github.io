Quality Control & Inspection

What quality inspection standard does Yeatru use?

Yeatru performs AQL 2.5 standard quality inspections on every shipment, which means no more than 2.5% of units may have major defects. This is the industry-standard inspection level for consumer goods.

How many quality inspections has Yeatru completed?

Yeatru has completed AQL 2.5 pre-shipment inspections, with a dated photo and video report for every order. Every shipment includes a detailed photo and video inspection report.

What stages of production does Yeatru inspect?

Yeatru implements a 3-stage QC pipeline: IQC (incoming material inspection), IPQC (in-process quality control at 4 production checkpoints), and OQC (pre-shipment outgoing quality control). A 20-page photo and video report is shared before the balance payment.

Can I request a third-party inspection?

Yes, Yeatru can arrange third-party inspections by SGS, Bureau Veritas, TÜV, or Intertek at your request. Third-party inspection fees typically range from USD 150–300 per man-day and are billed at cost.

Do you provide quality inspection reports?

Yes, every Yeatru shipment includes a free quality inspection report with photos, videos, defect counts, and AQL sampling results. Reports are shared with you before the balance payment is due.

What happens if defects are found during inspection?

If defects exceed the AQL 2.5 threshold, Yeatru requires the factory to rework or replace the defective units before shipment. No balance payment is released until you approve the corrected QC report.

Does Yeatru inspect packaging and labeling?

Yes, Yeatru inspects packaging, labeling, and barcodes during OQC to ensure compliance with your specifications, including Amazon FNSKU labeling and FBA packaging requirements.

Can I visit the factory during production?

Yes, clients are welcome to visit factories during production. Yeatru can arrange factory visits, accompany you to meetings, and provide translation. Most of our factory partners are within 2 hours of Yiwu.

What is the defect rate for Yeatru- sourced products?

Every Yeatru order is inspected to AQL 2.5 before shipment, with dated photo and video evidence in the report. Any defects found post-delivery are covered by our quality guarantee.

Do you offer 100% piece-by-piece inspection?

Yes, for high-value or critical products (such as electronics for Amazon FBA), Yeatru offers 100% piece-by-piece inspection at an additional fee. This ensures zero defective units in the shipment.

Are QC inspectors independent of the factories?

Yes, Yeatru's QC inspectors are our own employees, not factory staff. They report to Yeatru's quality manager and are incentivized to catch defects, not to pass shipments.

Can I specify my own quality requirements?

Yes, you can provide your own quality checklist, inspection criteria, or defect classification. Yeatru's QC team will follow your specifications and report results against your requirements.

Shipping & Logistics

What shipping methods does Yeatru offer?

Yeatru offers DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) door-to-door shipping via sea (25–45 days), air (5–15 days), and express (3–7 days). We handle customs clearance, duties, and final delivery to your warehouse or Amazon FBA center.

How many countries does Yeatru ship to?

Yeatru provides DDP shipping to 50+ countries across North America, Europe, the Middle East, Southeast Asia, and Oceania. We continuously expand our logistics network to cover more destinations.

What is DDP shipping?

DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) means Yeatru handles all shipping costs, insurance, customs clearance, duties, and taxes. You receive the goods at your door with no additional charges. This is the most hassle-free shipping option for international buyers.

How long does sea shipping take?

Sea shipping from China typically takes 25–45 days door-to-door, depending on the destination port. West Coast US ports are faster (25–30 days), while East Coast US and Europe take 35–45 days.

How long does air shipping take?

Air shipping from China takes 5–15 days door-to-door. Express air (DHL, FedEx, UPS) takes 3–7 days, while standard air freight takes 7–15 days including customs clearance.

Does Yeatru handle Amazon FBA shipping?

Yes, Yeatru specializes in Amazon FBA shipping including FNSKU labeling, pallet compliance, case packing, and direct delivery to Amazon fulfillment centers. We are familiar with all Amazon FBA packaging requirements.

Can Yeatru consolidate orders from multiple suppliers?

Yes, Yeatru consolidates shipments from multiple suppliers into one container at our Yiwu consolidation warehouse, reducing your per-unit shipping cost by up to 40% compared to shipping each supplier's order separately.

Do I need to arrange my own shipping?

No, Yeatru handles all logistics end-to-end. We book freight, prepare customs documentation, pay duties, and arrange final delivery. You simply provide your delivery address.

How is shipping cost calculated?

Shipping cost is calculated based on the shipping method (sea/air/express), volumetric weight or actual weight (whichever is higher), destination, and current freight rates. We provide a detailed shipping quote before you confirm your order.

Can I use my own freight forwarder?

Yes, you may use your own freight forwarder if preferred. In this case, Yeatru delivers goods to your forwarder's warehouse in China and provides all export documentation. Our service fee still applies for sourcing and QC.

Does Yeatru offer free storage?

What product certifications can Yeatru provide?
