7-Step Workflow

Step-by-Step China Sourcing Process

From initial inquiry to delivered goods — here’s exactly how it works.

1

Demand Analysis

We review your product specs target price MOQ certification needs and timeline. Consultation call within 24 hours.

2

Supplier Search

We shortlist 3-5 verified factories matching your requirements. You compare quotes capabilities and reviews side by side.

3

Sample Order

We arrange sample production handle payment and coordinate shipping. Sample typically arrives in 7-15 days. Revisions until perfect.

4

Mass Production

PO placement with 50% deposit. We monitor production progress conduct in-line QC and keep you updated with photos and reports.

5

QC Inspection

Pre-shipment inspection using AQL 2.5 standard. Full photo/video report. Defects tracked and re-inspected for free if needed.

6

Logistics & Shipping

We handle warehousing freight forwarding customs clearance and door-to-door delivery. Real-time tracking from factory to your door.

7

After-Sales Support

Warranty handling spare parts coordination and reorder management. We’re your long-term sourcing partner not just a one-time agent.

FAQ

Sourcing Process FAQs

How long does the entire sourcing process take?

For standard products the full process from inquiry to delivery typically takes 8-12 weeks. Custom/OEM products may take 12-16 weeks due to tooling and prototyping. We provide a detailed timeline during the demand analysis phase.

Can you handle rush orders or urgent timelines?

Yes. We offer expedited processing for urgent orders. Rush samples can be ready in 3-5 days and we can prioritize production with our partner factories. Additional rush fees may apply but we’ll always try to match your deadline.

What happens if I’m not satisfied with the sample?

We offer unlimited sample revisions within reasonable scope. If the factory cannot meet your requirements after 3 rounds of revisions we’ll refund your sample fee or switch to an alternative supplier at no extra sourcing cost.

Do you work with Amazon FBA specifically?

Absolutely. We’re deeply experienced with Amazon FBA requirements including FNSKU labeling poly bagging box content verification and prep services. Many of our clients are Amazon FBA sellers from small private label to wholesale arbitrage.

How much do your sourcing services cost?

Our sourcing fee is 5-8% of the order value for full sourcing, or a flat 5% if you already have supplier links. For small orders under $2,000 we offer a fixed fee arrangement. We provide a transparent quote before you commit — no hidden charges.
