About This Product

This wireless lavalier microphone interview handle is a practical creator accessory, specially designed to convert your existing clip-on wireless lapel mic into a professional handheld interview microphone. Equipped with matching foam windscreen, it solves outdoor wind noise trouble, perfect for reporters, vloggers, live streamers and content creators.

Package Options

  1. Interview Handheld Stick Only
  2. Interview Stick + Desktop Base
  3. Interview Stick + Extra Foam Windshield
  4. Interview Stick + Base + 2 Pcs Foam Windscreens

Warm Tips

  1. This product is only microphone handle accessory, wireless lavalier microphone transmitter is NOT included.
  2. The foam windscreen can be detached and cleaned to extend service life.
  3. Make sure the cold shoe transmitter fully inserted into the top slot to avoid audio shaking noise.

Bullet Points

Convert Wireless Lavalier Transmitter into Professional Handheld Interview Mic

Built-in standard cold shoe mount perfectly fits most wireless lapel microphone transmitters. Effortlessly turn your clip-on lavalier mic into a full handheld reporter microphone for news interviews, street vlogs, live broadcast and podcast recording.

Matching Foam Windshield for Clear Noise-Free Recording

Comes with dedicated foam windscreen cover, effectively blocks wind noise, background air friction and ambient clutter. Delivers crisp, clear vocal audio during outdoor shooting, street interviews and windy environment recording, greatly improve SNR ≥75dB.

Ergonomic Lightweight & Durable Handheld Grip

Made of smooth matte plastic, ergonomic long handle design for comfortable long-time holding. Ultra-light only 75g, no hand fatigue during all-day shooting. Solid integrated structure, wear-resistant and not easy to break, long service life.

Wide Compatibility & Multi Scene Application

Universal cold shoe interface fits most mainstream wireless lavalier mics. Ideal for street interviews, news reporting, vlog shooting, live streaming, podcast, short video creation, outdoor recording, media interview, creator content production.

Complete Optional Package Kits & Portable Size

Multiple package options available: single handle / handle + base / handle + extra foam windshields. Compact size easy to store in camera bag, backpack or storage box. Dimension: total length 237mm, windscreen 70*68mm, portable for travel shooting.
