Rules-of-thumb across 52 lanes: 8 things every 7-step playbook originator should memorize

  1. Rule 1 — The 40HQ sweet spot is tier-3 MOQ on every single one of the 52 lanes. Tiers 1-2 are LCL air/express (2-3× landed). Tier-3 and above are FCL 40HQ, which cuts the sea-freight line item by 61% on average.
  2. Rule 2 — The regulation docket is never less than 1.1% of the DDP line item. The cert dossier in the 11-line breakdown averages 1.1–1.8% of DDP across 52 lanes. If a candidate quotes < 1% for regulation docket, they are skipping real dossiers and you will be stopped at the border.
  3. Rule 3 — Import tax line-item + VAT line-item sum to between 11% (Hong Kong LANE-30 electronics) and 52% (Brazil LANE-13 apparel BJJ gi). If a candidate quotes "DDP" without itemizing these two separately by regulation line, your landed cost will balloon by the missing amount.
  4. Rule 4 — Inspection (mid + final) and capability-passes combined are 3.8% of DDP across 52 lanes. Skipping them (to save money) costs a weighted-average 8.6% TCO in returns, rejections, detentions and re-works — a 2.26× loss multiplier.
  5. Rule 5 — Sea-freight line-item (Lane 17 Solar street light small MOQ → Lane 28 Vegan leather 301 tariff) varies between 3.9% and 31.2% of DDP. If destination is inland small-market and product is bulky, freight dominates — negotiate the container booking, not the factory unit price. Small-quantity bulky items should always consider LCL consolidation + CBM quote comparison.
  6. Rule 6 — Last-mile drayage + parcel line-item averages 5.5% of DDP but is 11.8% in LANE-38 (silicone baby bibs, 144,000 units small parcel US FBA). Amazon FBA small-parcel inbound fees, pallet delivery lift-gate, residential delivery fees — these lines are often hidden. They must be in the 11-line breakdown.
  7. Rule 7 — Origin trucking + customs-filing line-items together average 3.4% of DDP. Candidates sometimes hide "local charges" (truck, CIQ, THC, DOC, seal) in a single lump "FOB fee" that grows 2.1× after you sign. Itemize origin trucking and export filing separately, or it will happen.
