Post-Brexit China → UK Import Simplified

After Brexit the rules for importing goods from China into the United Kingdom changed significantly. Every commercial shipment now requires an EORI number starting with GB (or XI for Northern Ireland) formal customs declaration VAT (usually 20% on CIF + duty) and may require UKCA marking for regulated products. We handle all of this for you including preparing the commercial invoice UK customs entry and VAT-deferment-friendly shipping where your EORI allows.

Send us your product photos links or sketches — we search 1688 Yiwu International Trade City and our factory network in Yiwu, Guangzhou and Shenzhen to find the best source at the best price. MOQs start from 1 pc–200 pcs for off-the-shelf consumer goods.

DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) — What Is & Is NOT Included

When we provide a written DDP quote specific to your product destination and weight the total price explicitly INCLUDES the following 6 items:

Included in DDP

For UK imports: You will need a GB EORI number (freely obtainable from HMRC in ~1 week). Shipments valued ≤ £135 are handled via IOSS (we can advise). Frequently Asked Questions — United Kingdom (England Scotland Wales Northern Ireland)

Do I need a GB EORI number before placing my first order?

For small test orders (≤ £135 shipped via IOSS DDP) an EORI is not strictly required. For larger orders shipped via sea freight or standard air freight a GB EORI from HMRC is required. It is free and typically issued in 1–3 working days. We can send you the step-by-step application guide before you order.

Are UKCA CE or RoHS marks handled?

For regulated products (toys electronics personal protective equipment cosmetics food contact) we coordinate with factories to apply the correct UKCA / CE marking and keep the technical file on record. We recommend registering the product with OPSS for UK sale if required. Certificates are sent to you before shipping.

Can you ship DDP (all duty + VAT + final-mile paid) to my home address in the UK?

Yes. Our standard DDP UK service includes: international freight UK customs clearance import duty 20% VAT and final-mile courier to any postcode in England Wales Scotland and Channel Islands. NI requires separate paperwork via XI EORI — please mention it upfront.
