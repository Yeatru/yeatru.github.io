Post-Brexit China → UK Import Simplified

After Brexit the rules for importing goods from China into the United Kingdom changed significantly. Every commercial shipment now requires an EORI number starting with GB (or XI for Northern Ireland), formal customs declaration, VAT (usually 20% on CIF + duty), and may require UKCA marking for regulated products. We handle all of this for you, including preparing the commercial invoice, UK customs entry, and VAT-deferment-friendly shipping where your EORI allows.

Send us your product photos, links, or sketches — we search 1688, Yiwu International Trade City, and our 75,000+ audited factory network to find the best source at the best price. MOQs start from just 50–200 pcs for off-the-shelf consumer goods.

DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) — What Is & Is NOT Included

When we provide a written DDP quote specific to your product, destination and weight, the total price explicitly INCLUDES the following 6 items:

Included in DDP
  1. International freight (air / sea / rail / express)
  2. China export customs clearance + export permits (per HS)
  3. Destination country import customs clearance filing
  4. Destination import duty (per applicable HS schedule)
  5. Destination VAT / GST / Sales tax
  6. Final‑mile delivery to the buyer's exact door address
EXCLUDED Unless Stated
  • Storage / warehousing fees after destination delivery
  • Demurrage beyond carrier free‑days
  • Product compliance certs (CE, UKCA, FCC, FDA, RoHS, SASO, SFDA, UKCA, CCC, Halal…)
  • Bonded‑warehouse deferment, VAT‑ID rep fees, importer‑of‑record setup
  • Returns, re‑shipments or reverse logistics
Specific Market Prep Capabilities
  • Amazon UK FBA prep: FNSKU labels + polybags + suffocation warnings
  • Can ship LTL to LTN4 / LBA4 / BHX2 / MAN1 / MMU1 or any 3PL
  • UKCA marking coordination available for electrical, toys, PPE, cosmetics
  • Full DDP to home address, shop, or warehouse — no surprise bills
Destination Customs & Tax — United Kingdom (England, Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland)

For UK imports: You will need a GB EORI number (freely obtainable from HMRC in ~1 week). Shipments valued ≤ £135 are handled via IOSS (we can advise). Shipments > £135 require formal customs entry; VAT at 20% is payable at import unless you use a deferment account. We will itemise VAT, Duty, Customs Clearance, and UK final-mile in your written DDP quote.
