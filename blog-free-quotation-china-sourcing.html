1. Why a Free Quotation Model? The Hidden Cost of Per-Quote Sourcing Fees

Most "China sourcing agents" fall into one of three paid models:

  1. $50 to $300 per product quotation. You email a product photo, they send a quote. But if you need 15 products? That is $750 to $4,500 before you have even seen real factory quality.
  2. $250 to $1,000 monthly retainer. Typical for boutique agents. Works for Fortune 500 procurement teams, but kills small and mid-sized importers who only source 2 or 3 times per year.
  3. 10-15% factory-side kickback (hidden). Agents claim "free quotation" but accept 8 to 12% undisclosed kickback from the factory — effectively inflating your price 15 to 25% with zero service delivered for the extra margin.

Yeatru was founded in 2022 to fix this. We believe buyers should never pay for pricing information. Pricing is a market commodity — not a premium service. A great quotation is simply the first page of a good relationship.

Why does "free" produce better quotations?

Simple: incentives are aligned. Every other paid-quotation agent is motivated to deliver a quotation quickly and move on — that is how they earn. We are motivated to deliver a quotation that wins your business for the full order — because that is when we earn.

This creates a critical quality difference:

  • Paid-quotation agent: 1 factory, 1 price, delivered in 48h. You pay.
  • Yeatru: 3 factories, price + incoterm + shipping + MOQ + lead time + photos + certs in 24h. You pay zero.

In 2025, our internal analysis showed Yeatru quotations result in 4.3 times more approved orders per RFQ than the industry average. Why? Because we only profit if the quotation is accurate, honest, and compelling enough for you to commit.

9. 12 Common Objections (and Why They Are Less Important Than You Think)

We track every sales objection for training purposes. Here are the top 12 from a sample of 1,847 RFQs from January to June 2026, with fully honest answers.

Objection 1: "If it is free, the quality must be low." (21% of objections)

Truth: The quotation quality is actually higher than paid models because we only earn if you approve the quotation and place the order. A paid agent has no incentive beyond delivery speed. Counter-evidence: 4.3 times higher approval ratio than industry average.

Objection 2: "What if you add hidden kickbacks from the factory anyway?" (17%)

Any Yeatru specialist found accepting factory kickbacks is terminated immediately (2 cases in 3.5 years, both reported within 30 days by their colleagues). Additionally, you can always (a) ask for factory contact and call them independently to confirm the unit price; (b) request third-party payment verification via Alibaba Trade Assurance; (c) order a 100pc spot check to verify per-unit pricing at customs.

Objection 3: "I already have a good factory contact. Why use you?" (15%)

Great — Order Management Service is exactly 3-4% for clients who want us to run QC, consolidate, and handle forms. But we can usually find a second factory with 5 to 20% better pricing or 2 to 3 week faster lead time on the free quotation, no cost to check.

Objection 4: "Minimum commission $100 is expensive for my $800 order." (12%)

Correct. If your order is consistently under $1,000 it is cheaper to use AliExpress or DHgate. But for any client expecting to reorder, the $100 minimum pays for itself on the second order alone because we beat AliExpress unit pricing 30-65% on identical items once you clear $2,500 or more volume.

Objection 5: "Commission tiers seem arbitrary — can they change later?" (10%)

Tiers are hard-coded into the PDF quotation we send you. Once you sign the service agreement referencing the PDF, the tier rate is fixed for that entire order and all repeat orders within the same product family for 12 months.

Objection 6: "My product is custom OEM with private label mold. Can you still quote free?" (9%)

Yes. 38% of our 2025 orders included OEM. The free quotation includes NRE (mold and tooling) cost from 3 factories plus estimated per-unit cost at 4 volume tiers. Mold ownership transfer documents are provided free of charge as part of the paperwork package.

Objection 7: "Paying 30% deposit to a China company sounds risky." (8%)

Totally understandable. First-time clients may use Alibaba Trade Assurance which holds the deposit in escrow and releases it only after QC stage number 1 passes. For repeat clients, 30/70 T/T with our licensed trading company (11-year track record, audited accounts, SGS verified) is the standard.

Objection 8: "DDP always surprises me with extra fees at customs." (7%)

That happens because agents under-quote DDP to win the job, then charge surcharges. Yeatru DDP quote includes: FOB, ocean/air freight, terminal handling, import duty, VAT/GST, customs brokerage, ISF bond (US) or IOSS (EU), and last-mile — all itemized. No line item appears after your balance payment.

Objection 9: "AQL 2.5 defect guarantee is vague." (5%)

Fair. Our written QC guarantee states: "If QC stage number 3 (pre-shipment) inspection shows more than 2.5% critical or major defects, we ship zero units until the factory reworks or replaces the entire lot at their cost. If 45 days after delivery more than 2.5% manufacturing defects are documented via photos, Yeatru refunds or re-ships at its election."

Objection 10: "Consolidating 5 factories sounds like it adds delays." (4%)

Done well, consolidation saves 7 to 14 days because we schedule all factories to finish within the same 3-day window and ship to Yiwu warehouse in parallel. Done poorly, it adds 3+ weeks. We have a 92% on-time completion rate across multi-factory consolidations (versus industry 63%).

Objection 11: "UK/EU IOSS VAT and US customs bonds are too complicated." (3%)

Yes they are — which is why we handle it end-to-end. We will either (a) file IOSS under Yeatru EU IOSS number on your behalf with full VAT credit documentation, or (b) provide you the standard forms to file under your own number. US ISF entry and continuous bond quotes are included free in DDP orders.

Objection 12: "One month turnaround for my order sounds slow." (3%)

We can usually deliver 15% faster than the stated timeline if OEM is not required. The average full order timeline (RFQ to doorstep) for a 20ft container USA is: 24h quotation, 24h approval, 7-10 days sample QC, 18-25 days mass production, 4-7 days QC + consolidation, 14-21 days ocean + customs = 45-65 days total. Express air for small shipments: 14-21 days total.
