About This Product

Our rolling plastic trolley shopping basket comes with 35L medium and 45L large two capacity options, designed for both commercial retail and household storage. Adopting full ventilated grid plastic body, combined with foldable long pull handle and 4 mixed swivel & fixed silent PU wheels, this shopping trolley integrates large storage capacity, space-saving stackable structure and easy maneuverability. Multiple color options and custom logo printing service make it the ideal storage tool for supermarkets, convenience stores, laundries and family daily organization.

Bullet 1｜Various capacities, lightweight breathable mesh design

Two sizes available: 35L Small & 45L medium & 50L large, match small convenience stores and big supermarkets. Full hollow grid basket body keeps air circulating, prevent mildew for fresh produce and dirty laundry. Thickened impact-resistant PP plastic ensures long service life without extra weight burden.

Bullet 2｜Smooth 4-wheel silent rolling, floor-friendly

Equipped with 2 swivel casters + 2 fixed directional PU wheels. Silent wheel rolling reduces noise in shopping malls; the metal wheel bracket bears heavy loads stably, no scratches on tile, concrete and epoxy floors. Flexible wheel design makes sharp turns easily in narrow aisles.

Bullet 3｜Ergonomic Foldable Pull Handle & Stackable Design

Adjustable foldable long pull handle lets users pull upright without bending down to relieve waist fatigue. Interlocking stackable structure allows dozens of baskets stacked together when idle, cut 70% storage space for backroom, warehouse and stock rooms.

Bullet 4｜Multi-color Selection & Custom Logo Support

5 vibrant solid colors to choose: Red, Pink, Yellow, Blue, Gray. Blank flat panel on basket front supports custom silk-screen brand logo printing, perfect for supermarket, boutique, pharmacy, laundromat to build brand recognition and zone classification.

Bullet 5｜Wide Commercial & Household Application

Ideal for retail grocery shopping, convenience store goods sorting, laundry room clothes storage, garage toy & tool organizer, hotel linen transport, restaurant kitchen supplies carrying. Stable bulk supply for wholesale orders, perfect for B2B store owners and individual family daily use.

Warm Tips

  • Do not overload the basket beyond the weight limit to protect wheel brackets and plastic body
  • Clean the grid surface with mild soapy water; avoid strong corrosive cleaning agents
  • Stack baskets gently to prevent edge deformation
