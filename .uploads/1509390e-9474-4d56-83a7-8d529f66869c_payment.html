How It Works

4-Step Payment Process

A clear, transparent flow from invoice confirmation to fund release — every step documented and traceable.

01

Confirm Invoice

Review the Proforma Invoice (PI) with your agent. Confirm product specs, quantities, unit price, and total amount before any payment.

02

Pay Deposit (50%)

Send 50% deposit via your chosen method (XTransfer recommended). We confirm receipt and start production immediately.

03

QC & Inspection

Before shipping, we conduct a pre-shipment quality inspection (AQL 2.5) and share photo/video evidence for your approval.

04

Pay Balance & Ship

After QC approval, pay the remaining 50% balance. We release the goods for shipping and provide tracking details.

Standard Terms

Payment Terms That Protect Both Sides

Full Payment Before Shipping
