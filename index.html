FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is your minimum order quantity?

Our MOQ starts at 50 pieces for most products, and we accept sample orders from 1 piece. We are flexible for trial orders.

How long does sourcing take?

Quotes within 24 hours. Sample delivery 3–7 days. Production 15–30 days. International shipping 5–14 days.

What payment methods do you accept?

XTransfer, LianLian, Airwallex, T/T and PayPal. 50% deposit to start, 50% balance before shipment.

Do you provide quality inspection?

Yes. Every shipment undergoes AQL 2.5 pre-shipment inspection with photo and video evidence. Third-party inspection (SGS, Bureau Veritas) also available.

Can you handle OEM and private label?

Yes. We manage custom molds, tooling, sampling, revisions and compliance (CE, FCC, REACH, FDA) from prototype to mass production.
