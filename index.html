What Is a China Sourcing Agent?

Everything you need to know about working with a sourcing agent in China — definitions, costs, comparisons, and FAQs

A China sourcing agent is a professional intermediary that helps international buyers find reliable suppliers, negotiate factory-direct prices, verify product quality, manage production, and handle logistics — all without the buyer needing to travel to China or speak Chinese. Unlike a trading company that buys and resells products, a sourcing agent works on your behalf, representing your interests at every step of the procurement process.

Yeatru Sourcing has served 200+ global clients across 50+ countries with a network of 75,000+ verified suppliers. Our management team brings 14+ years of combined experience in supplier management, procurement, quality control, and international logistics. Whether you're an Amazon FBA seller, TikTok Shop owner, or wholesale distributor, a sourcing agent is your boots-on-the-ground partner in China.

What Does Yeatru Do as Your Sourcing Agent?

As your China sourcing agent, Yeatru handles the entire procurement lifecycle — from finding the right factory to delivering finished goods to your warehouse. Here's what we do:

  1. Supplier Sourcing & Verification: We search our database of 75,000+ factories to find the best match for your product, MOQ, and budget. Every supplier is vetted for business license, production capacity, and export experience.
  2. Price Negotiation: We negotiate factory-direct prices on your behalf — typically saving clients 15-30% versus Alibaba-listed prices. You see the real factory quote, not a marked-up trading company price.
  3. Sample Arrangement & Evaluation: We order samples from 2-3 shortlisted factories, evaluate quality, and send you a comparison report with photos and recommendations.
  4. Order Follow-up & Production Monitoring: We track production milestones, send weekly progress updates with factory photos, and flag delays before they become problems.
  5. Quality Control & Inspection: Our QC team conducts AQL 2.5 inspections before shipment — checking dimensions, function, packaging, labeling, and barcodes. You get a detailed inspection report with pass/fail results.
  6. OEM/ODM Customization: Need custom logo, private label, custom packaging, or product modifications? We coordinate with the factory to make it happen — from design files to finished samples.
  7. Consolidation & Warehousing: Multiple suppliers? We consolidate goods at our Yiwu warehouse, re-pack as needed, and prepare for export in one shipment — saving you hundreds in shipping costs.
  8. Export Documentation & Customs: We handle commercial invoices, packing lists, certificates of origin, and all export documentation required by Chinese customs.
  9. Shipping & Logistics: Air freight, sea freight (FCL/LCL), express courier (DHL/FedEx/UPS), or door-to-door delivery — we arrange the most cost-effective shipping method and track your cargo to destination.
  10. After-Sales Support: Defective products? Missing items? We handle claims with the factory and arrange replacements or refunds on your behalf.

How to Choose the Right Sourcing Agent

Not all sourcing agents are created equal. Here's our 10-point checklist for evaluating any China sourcing agent — including us:

  1. Verify their legal entity: Do they have a registered Chinese company with an export license? Ask for their business license.
  2. Check their physical presence: Do they have a real office in China (not just a virtual address)? Can they video-call you from their warehouse?
  3. Assess supplier network: How many verified suppliers do they have? Can they source outside Alibaba (direct factory relationships)?
  4. Review pricing transparency: Do they show you the factory's original quote? Or do they just give you a "final price" (hiding their markup)?
  5. Inspect QC process: Do they follow AQL standards? Can they send you a sample inspection report before you commit?
  6. Test communication speed: Send them a product inquiry. Do they respond within 24 hours with specific, useful information — or generic templates?
  7. Ask for client references: Can they connect you with 2-3 existing clients in your industry/platform (Amazon, TikTok, wholesale)?
  8. Review contract & payment terms: Is there a written service agreement? Are payment terms clear (factory deposit, balance after QC)?
  9. Evaluate after-sales policy: What happens if products arrive defective? Do they handle claims, or disappear?
  10. Assess cultural & language fit: Can they communicate in your language? Do they understand your market's compliance requirements (CE, FCC, CPC, FDA)?
