One agency from enquiry to unloading

Brazil rewards preparation. An order that reaches Santos with the wrong NCM, a missing licence or a label without Portuguese is not delayed by a week — it is stored, fined or rejected. The work that prevents that sits upstream of the shipment.

  1. Supplier search — factories compared against the specification, with capacity and export history checked.
  2. Costing before sampling — NCM classification first, because the tax rate decides whether the product is viable at all.
  3. Sampling and localisation — Portuguese label, INMETRO or ANATEL requirements identified, manual and packaging adapted.
  4. Production and inspection — AQL 2.5 at the factory, defects settled before the balance is paid.
  5. Export and shipping — documents issued, cartons consolidated, booked on the lane that suits the volume.

Brazil: the four lines that move the landed cost

The tax stack

Import duty (II) is set by NCM and can run from zero to 35%. IPI is added by product type, PIS/COFINS-Importação applies to the import, ICMS is charged by the state at 17–20%, AFRMM adds 8% on the ocean freight, and Siscomex charges a transaction fee. Together these can add 60–100% to the FOB value, which is why a Brazilian landed-cost sheet looks nothing like a European one.

Certification

ANATEL homologation for anything that transmits or receives radio, INMETRO certification for toys and a defined list of electrical and safety products, ANVISA for cosmetics, food-contact and health items. Wood packaging must be ISPM 15 treated. The certificate is a condition of sale, not an optional extra.

Importer registration

The Brazilian importer needs a Siscomex/Radar profile to import commercially, and some NCM codes require a prior import licence. A buyer without an active Radar can still trade, but the goods need a licensed importer of record — a decision to make before production, not at the port.

Portuguese labelling

Consumer information, warnings, ingredients and instructions have to be in Portuguese, and the importer's details usually appear on the label. Reprinting a label after the goods are made is the single most expensive correction in a Brazilian order.

How an order runs

  1. Brief and NCM — product, quantity, and the classification that determines the tax burden.
  2. Quote with landed cost — product, freight, fee and a tax estimate separated, so the comparison is honest.
  3. Sample and certification plan — INMETRO, ANATEL or ANVISA route confirmed before production.
  4. Production and AQL 2.5 — inspection report and dated evidence before the balance.
  5. Export and shipping — Portuguese label checked, documents issued, consolidated shipment booked.
