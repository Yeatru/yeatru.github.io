DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) — What Is & Is NOT Included

When we provide a written DDP quote specific to your product destination and weight the total price explicitly INCLUDES the following 6 items:

Included in DDP

  • International freight (air / sea / rail / express)
  • China export customs clearance + export permits (per HS)
  • Destination country import customs clearance filing
  • Destination import duty (per applicable HS schedule)
  • Destination VAT / GST / Sales tax
  • Final‑mile delivery to the buyer’s exact door address

EXCLUDED Unless Stated

  • Storage / warehousing fees after destination delivery
  • Demurrage beyond carrier free‑days
  • Product compliance certs (CE UKCA FCC FDA RoHS SASO SFDA UKCA CCC Halal…)
  • Bonded‑warehouse deferment VAT‑ID rep fees importer‑of‑record setup
  • Returns re‑shipments or reverse logistics

Specific Market Prep Capabilities

  • Amazon AE / Noon / Carrefour / Jarir marketplace prep
  • GCC G-Mark / SASO IECEE / ESMA conformity certificates coordination
  • Halal certification coordination for cosmetics food contact and products consumed by Muslim consumers
  • Saudi NLP (SABER) platform registration & SFDA product registration

Destination Customs & Tax — GCC Countries · UAE (Dubai Abu Dhabi Sharjah) · Saudi Arabia (Riyadh Jeddah Dammam Makkah Madinah) · Qatar · Kuwait · Oman · Bahrain

For GCC imports: UAE VAT is 5% on CIF + customs duty; Saudi VAT is 15%. Customs duty is typically 4.5%–15% ad valorem based on HS code with 0% duty on 400+ items under GCC FTA. For Saudi Arabia SABER e-platform conformity certificate (TIRA/SFOR) is mandatory for many consumer goods BEFORE shipment to KSA arrives at port. For UAE ESMA ECAS registration is required for regulated products (electrical toys PPE cosmetics food-contact). Our DDP quotes INCLUDE duty + VAT + customs clearance + conformity certificate fees (if applicable).

Yes. For products subject to SFDA (cosmetics skincare food-contact items perfumes) we coordinate certification via halal-accredited bodies in China (cited by SFDA) and issue the SABER e-platform Product Certificate + Shipment Certificate before your container arrives. Paperwork is always filed BEFORE goods leave China — so there is no port delay or customs storage fee. SABER fee is included in DDP price for Saudi-bound shipments.

Can you ship full DDP (all-in) to a private villa or home address in Riyadh?

Yes. Full DDP delivery covers: sea/air freight Saudi customs clearance 5% UAE or 15% Saudi VAT customs duty SABER/SFDA conformity fees if applicable and final-mile trucking to any emirate (UAE) or city in KSA. For large items (kitchen cabinets furniture home decor) we offer white-glove delivery and staircase carry for a small additional fee itemised in your quote.

Absolutely. Most off-the-shelf perfume bottles abayas incense burners Ramadan decorations and kitchenware found in Yiwu International Trade City District 1–4 can be ordered from just 20–200 pieces. Test batches are consolidated at our Yiwu warehouse and shipped via DHL/DDP economy to your shop office or home address in any UAE emirate within 7–10 business days.
