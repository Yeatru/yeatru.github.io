Pricing & Transparency (30%)

Compliance & QC (25%)

Shipping & Support (20%)

**Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a sourcing agent good for Amazon FBA?

A strong FBA agent handles five things well: (1) FNSKU labeling and polybag prep that matches Amazon’s exact requirements, (2) category-specific compliance (CPSIA for US toys, CE for EU electronics, FDA for food contact), (3) DDP shipping with FBA destination codes, (4) product photography optimized for Amazon listings, and (5) rapid reorder management to avoid stockouts.

How much does a sourcing agent cost for Amazon FBA?

Most agents charge 5-8% commission on order value. For Amazon FBA sellers placing $10,000-$50,000 orders, expect 4-6%. A $50k FBA order at 5% commission ($2,500) typically returns $6,500-$12,000 in net savings through factory-direct pricing and defect prevention.

Should I use a sourcing agent or a trading company for FBA?

Use a sourcing agent. Trading companies markup prices 15-35% and won’t introduce you to the actual factory. Agents work for you, not the factory, and earn their fee by negotiating the lowest possible ex-factory price. On a $50k FBA order, that difference is $7,500-$17,500 in your pocket.
