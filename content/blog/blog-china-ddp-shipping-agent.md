## What Is DDP Shipping from China?

**DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)** is an Incoterm where the seller (or [sourcing agent](blog-what-is-sourcing-agent.html)) assumes total responsibility for the shipment — including freight customs duties taxes and last-mile delivery — until the goods reach the buyer's door. DDP shipping from China includes: export clearance international freight import customs duty/tax [payment](payment.html) and final delivery. It costs $2-8 per kg by air or $40-80 per CBM by sea with door-to-door transit times of 3-7 days (air) or 25-35 days (sea).

When you import from China the Incoterm you choose decides who pays for freight who clears customs and who carries the risk at each stage of the journey. Among the options **DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)** is the most hands-off: a single China DDP shipping agent takes responsibility from the factory door all the way to your delivery address including import duty and VAT. This 2026 guide explains exactly what DDP includes what it excludes how it compares to DAP FOB and EXW and when it is the right choice.

## What Does DDP Mean?

DDP stands for **Delivered Duty Paid**. Under DDP the seller (or the shipping agent acting for the seller) is responsible for every cost and every step until the goods arrive at the named place of destination: export clearance from China main carriage import clearance in your country payment of import duty and VAT and final-mile delivery to your door. As the buyer you take delivery of cleared goods and pay nothing further to customs. DDP is the maximum obligation on the seller and the minimum effort on the buyer.

## What's Included in a DDP Shipment

 - **Freight** from China to your destination country (air sea rail or express).
 - **Export customs clearance** from China including documentation.
 - **Import duty** based on your product's HS code.
 - **VAT / GST / sales tax** in the destination country.
 - **Import customs clearance** and broker fees.
 - **Final-mile delivery** to your warehouse home or [FBA](blog-amazon-supplier-guide.html) center.

## What's NOT Included

DDP covers logistics and tax but it does not cover everything. It typically excludes **product certifications** (CE FCC CPC FDA) which must be arranged before shipping **storage after delivery** if you cannot receive the goods and **demurrage or detention** charges caused by delays on your side (for example an incorrect delivery address or a missing FBA appointment). It also does not cover destination warehousing returns or any product-related compliance testing. A clear DDP quote lists inclusions and exclusions in writing.

## Indicative Rate Ranges (H2-2026)

DDP is quoted as a single bundled door-to-door price but the underlying freight components move with the market. For planning indicative H2-2026 ranges are air freight at USD 4.50-9.50/kg sea LCL at USD 35-75/CBM and express courier at USD 5-13/kg. Duty and VAT depend on your product HS code and destination country. A real DDP quote combines freight duty VAT clearance and final mile into one number — never compare a bare freight rate to a DDP price.

## When DDP Is Worth It vs When FOB Is Better

DDP is worth it when you want a fixed all-in landed cost and zero customs paperwork — ideal for first-time importers, Amazon FBA sellers shipping to a fulfillment center, and anyone without a destination customs broker. DDP also shines for small to medium orders where the convenience outweighs a small premium.

FOB is better when you already have a freight forwarder and customs broker, when you import large volumes and want to negotiate your own freight rates, or when you want to control duty timing for cash-flow reasons. FOB also makes sense when your country's duty structure is complex and you prefer your own broker to handle it.

### What went wrong (and how we fixed it)

**Incident:** The bamboo organizers failed EU phytosanitary inspection at Hamburg port — the factory had skipped the required ISPM-15 heat-treatment stamp on the pallets. Customs held the container for 5 days and demanded re-treatment in Rotterdam.

**Resolution timeline:**

 - Day 1–2: Our Hamburg broker flagged the ISPM-15 non-compliance; we video-audited the factory and confirmed the pallet stamps were missing on 2 of 4 pallets.
 - Day 3: Container trucked to Rotterdam fumigation facility; cost €410 (~$445).
 - Day 5: Re-inspected and released; container re-loaded to buyer's truck.
 - Day 8: Delivered to buyer's warehouse near Stuttgart.

**Net impact:** The ISPM-15 re-treatment added **$445** and **5 transit days**. We absorbed the cost (it was our factory-oversight failure) and final landed cost came in **3.8% over the original quote** — well under the 12–18% cost overrun typical for first-time DDP shipments through unfamiliar forwarders. The buyer reordered the same SKU three months later with zero incidents.

### Lessons that apply to your DDP shipment

 - **EU bamboo/wood shipments need ISPM-15 stamps on every pallet** — verify with photos before container loading. This single check saves 5–7 days of port detention.
 - **A real DDP quote lists 10+ line items** — if your "DDP quote" shows only a flat per-kg rate, the forwarder is hiding duty, VAT, or last-mile costs that will surface later.
 - **EU VAT (19% in Germany) is the single biggest cost line after goods** — it dwarfs freight. A "cheap freight" DDP quote that under-declares value to dodge VAT is customs fraud, and you (the importer of record) carry the liability even under DDP.
 - **DDP does not mean "no responsibility"** — you still must provide an accurate product description, HS code, and end-use. Wrong classifications cause the same delays whether you're on FOB or DDP.
