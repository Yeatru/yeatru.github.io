## What Are Sourcing Service Models?

A **sourcing service model** defines the scope of procurement responsibilities a [sourcing agent](blog-what-is-sourcing-agent.html) assumes on behalf of the buyer. The three primary models are **Order Management** (agent handles only order placement and tracking 5% flat fee) **Full Sourcing** (agent handles supplier search negotiation QC and logistics 5-8% fee) and **Turnkey/[OEM](blog-private-mold-packaging.html)** (agent manages product development tooling and private label 8-15% fee). The right model depends on order volume product complexity and the buyer's China experience.

## One Service Model Does Not Fit All

Most buyers pick a China sourcing agent before understanding which service model they actually need. The result is paying for full sourcing service when all they needed was order management or vice versa. Different stages of your business need different levels of support. This guide breaks down the two main sourcing service models their fees and how to choose the right one for your order size and experience level.

## Model 1: Order Management Service (You Already Have a Supplier Link)

The order management service is designed for buyers who have already found a factory or supplier link but need a local team to follow up on the order and run [quality control](quality-control.html). You send the supplier contact and product details the agent handles the rest.

### Who It Is For

 - Buyers who already have a verified [Alibaba](blog-alibaba-vs-agent.html) supplier or factory contact
 - Sellers with a working [sample](blog-sample-order-from-china.html) and confirmed product spec
 - Businesses that need someone on the ground to track production and inspect quality
 - Repeat orders where the supplier relationship is already established

### What the Service Covers

 - **Order placement and follow-up:** translating the PI confirming specs tracking the production schedule
 - **Production tracking:** weekly progress updates and photo reports
 - **Basic pre-shipment inspection:** visual QC quantity check packaging check before balance [payment](payment.html)
 - **Shipping coordination:** booking freight forwarder export paperwork consolidation and agreed [Incoterms](https://iccwbo.org/business-solutions/incoterms-rules/?v=20260909k)

### Fee Structure

Order management service charges a flat commission of **5% of the order value** with a **minimum [service fee](blog-how-much-sourcing-agents-charge.html) of $50 per order**. Because the agent is not sourcing the factory the workload is lower and the commission is smaller than full sourcing.

## Model 2: Full Sourcing Service (Starting From Scratch)

Full sourcing service is for buyers who only have a product idea photo or Amazon listing and need the agent to find the right factory from scratch. This is a much deeper engagement that includes [supplier sourcing](supplier-verification.html) price comparison factory audits sampling and full QC.

### Who It Is For

 - First-time importers with no supplier contacts
 - Buyers launching a new private label or OEM product
 - Sellers who have been burned by Alibaba suppliers and want a vetted factory
 - [Amazon FBA](blog-amazon-supplier-guide.html) sellers who need consistent quality and certifications

### What the Service Covers

 - **Supplier sourcing:** identifying 3-5 qualified factories based on your spec
 - **Price comparison:** apples-to-apples quote comparison and negotiation
 - **[Factory audit](factory-audit.html):** on-site or video verification of factory credentials
 - **Sample management:** ordering evaluating and revising samples
 - **Production follow-up:** milestone inspections during production
 - **Pre-shipment QC:** AQL-based inspection with photos and report
 - **Logistics and shipping:** freight booking customs door-to-door coordination

### Tiered Commission Structure

Full sourcing service uses a tiered commission based on order value so larger orders pay a lower percentage:

 - **Orders under $5,000:** 7% to 8% commission
 - **Orders $5,000 - $20,000:** 5% to 6% commission
 - **Orders above $20,000:** 4% commission
 - **Minimum service fee:** $100 per order

The minimum fee protects the agent's labor on small trial orders while the tiered structure rewards buyers who scale up.

* **Important:** Do not send a list of 200 products and ask the agent to "quote everything". Factories and agents treat bulk price-only inquiries as price shoppers. Pick 3-5 target SKUs and brief the agent properly before requesting quotes.

## How to Choose Between the Two Models

The right sourcing service model depends on three factors: your sourcing experience your order volume and your time cost.

 - **You have a verified supplier already:** Choose order management. Paying 7-8% for full sourcing on a factory you already trust is wasted money.
 - **You only have a product photo or Amazon link:** Choose full sourcing. Without a verified factory order management cannot help you.
 - **Your order is below $1,500:** Consider the [Yiwu](blog-yiwu-market-guide.html) market for ready stock or accept a higher unit price. Agent minimum fees will dominate small orders.
 - **Your order is above $5,000 and you have time:** Full sourcing pays for itself through better factory pricing and QC.
 - **You value time over fee savings:** Full sourcing removes weeks of supplier research from your plate.

## Real Cost Comparison Example

Let's compare both models on a $15,000 order for a kitchenware product:

 - **Order management (you already have the supplier):** 3% commission = $450. Total cost: $15,450.
 - **Full sourcing (agent finds the supplier):** 5% commission = $750. But agent negotiation typically saves 8-12% on factory price so a $15,000 order may end up at $13,500 + $675 commission = $14,175. Net saving: $1,275.

On larger orders full sourcing often pays for itself through better factory pricing even after the higher commission.

## Common Mistakes When Picking a Service Model

 - **Choosing full sourcing when you already have a supplier:** Paying 7% instead of 3% for the same outcome.
 - **Choosing order management without a verified supplier:** The agent cannot source under this model so the order stalls.
 - **Mass-inquiring hundreds of SKUs:** Treated as a price shopper quotes go to the bottom of the queue.
 - **Ignoring the minimum fee:** On a $1,000 order a $300 minimum fee equals 30% effective commission. Combine small SKUs into one order.
 - **Switching agents every order:** Each new agent has to relearn your products. Build a long-term relationship.

## When to Switch Models

Many buyers start with full sourcing for their first 1-2 orders then switch to order management once a reliable factory is identified. This is a smart cost optimization: pay for sourcing expertise when you need to find the right factory then pay a lower commission for ongoing order follow-up. As your volume grows past $20,000 per order the tiered commission also drops to 4% making full sourcing increasingly affordable.

Choosing the right sourcing service model is about matching the service to your stage. ## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

- **Choosing the cheapest agent without due diligence.** Commission below 2% is usually subsidized by factory kickbacks or hidden markups — you pay more in inflated goods cost. A real 5–8% fee with transparency costs less.
- **Not verifying the business license.** Look up the agent on gsxt.gov.cn (National Enterprise Credit Information). Confirm the business scope lists 代理 (agency), registered capital $50k+, and no abnormal operation status.
- **No 3 client references in your category.** Ask for 3 buyers who sourced a similar product in the last 6 months and actually call them. Verify delivery, defect rate, and fee transparency.
- **Refusing to share the factory name.** A reputable agent discloses the factory and lets you pay the factory directly. An agent that hides the factory is marking up the goods price, not just charging a fee.
- **No contract with QC and defect-liability terms.** Sign an agreement covering NDA, IP protection, QC pass criteria (AQL level), and who pays for rework. A handshake deal leaves you with no recourse.
