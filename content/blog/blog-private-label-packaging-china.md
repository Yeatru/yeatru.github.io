## What Is Private Label Packaging?

**Private label packaging** is custom-printed retail or shipping packaging bearing your brand name, logo, and design rather than a generic or manufacturer's box. In China, packaging factories specialize in folding cartons, mailer boxes, rigid gift boxes, and labels — and they can produce your branded packaging for a fraction of Western costs. Packaging is where your brand makes its first impression: a premium unboxing increases repeat purchase rate by **30–50%**, and Amazon FBA sellers report **15–25% higher conversion** with branded mailers. Packaging is the final step in the [OEM and private label](blog-oem-private-label-china.html) process — after the product is made, the box sells it.

## Paper Materials and Weight

- **Kraft paper** — eco/rustic look, 100–400 gsm, cheapest, natural brown. Best for mailer boxes and eco-brands.
- **Coated C2S (C2S art paper)** — white, glossy or matte, 250–400 gsm, holds full-color photos. Best for retail boxes.
- **Corrugated board** — E-flute (thin, 1.5 mm) for mailers, F-flute (thinner, 0.8 mm) for premium mailers, B-flute (3.2 mm) for shipping. Crush resistance measured in ECT (Edge Crush Test).
- **Greyboard + wrap** — 800–1500 gsm greyboard wrapped in coated paper or cloth. Used for rigid gift boxes; most expensive but most premium feel.
- **Specialty papers** — textured, metallic, or pearl papers for luxury packaging; add 20–50% cost.

| **Digital** || CMYK || 1–500 || None || Good || Small runs, prototypes, variable data |

| **Flexo** || 1–4 colors || 300+ || $50–$150/plate || Good || Kraft mailers, corrugated shipping boxes |

| **Hot stamping** || Foil (gold/silver/etc.) || 500+ || $80–$200/die || Premium || Logos, brand accents on rigid/retail boxes |

## Finishing Options (Add-Ons)

- **Lamination** — matte (premium, fingerprint-resistant) or gloss (vibrant, durable). Adds $0.02–$0.05/box.
- **Spot UV** — glossy raised coating on specific areas (logo, product image). Adds $0.05–$0.15/box.
- **Embossing / Debossing** — raised or recessed logo. Adds $0.05–$0.20/box, requires a custom die.
- **Soft-touch lamination** — velvety feel, premium unboxing. Adds $0.05–$0.10/box.
- **Foil stamping** — gold/silver/holographic foil. Adds $0.05–$0.20/box.

## The Dieline and Artwork Process

- **Step 1 — Request a dieline**: Give the factory your assembled dimensions (L × W × H) and box type. They send a dieline (AI or PDF) with cut lines, fold lines, bleed area, and safe zone within 1–2 days.
- **Step 2 — Place artwork**: Your designer places the logo, product images, and text on the dieline. Use **CMYK** color mode (not RGB), **300 DPI** resolution, **3 mm bleed**, and outline all fonts. Pantone colors require a separate plate.
- **Step 3 — Approve digital proof**: The factory sends a PDF proof. Check spelling, barcode readability, and color values. This is free.
- **Step 4 — Approve printed sample**: The factory prints 1–5 physical samples ($20–$100, often refundable on bulk order). Check color match, fold quality, glue strength, and fit with your product. This step prevents $1,000+ reprint costs.
- **Step 5 — Mass production**: After sample approval, production runs 7–20 days. Run AQL inspection for color consistency, dimension accuracy, and crush resistance.

## Compliance and Certifications

- **FSC / PEFC certification** — required by Walmart, Target, Amazon Climate Pledge Friendly, and EU packaging regulations. Ask for the certificate number and verify on the FSC database. FSC paper costs 5–15% more.
- **Food-safe ink** — for food packaging, confirm soy-based or water-based inks (no heavy metals). Request a food-contact test report.
- **Regulatory labels** — country of origin ("Made in China"), ingredients list, net weight, warning labels, and UPC/EAN barcode must be printed on retail boxes. Barcodes must be scannable (test with a scanner).
- **EU/US specific** — EU requires the "green dot" recycling symbol and producer responsibility compliance (EPR). US requires FTC labeling guidelines.
- **Child-resistant packaging** — for pharmaceuticals, cannabis, or hazardous products, require certified child-resistant closures.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Sending RGB artwork** — colors will shift dramatically in CMYK printing. Always convert to CMYK before sending.
- **No physical sample** — approving only a digital proof risks color mismatch, wrong dimensions, or folding errors. Always get a printed sample.
- **Ignoring crush resistance** — a mailer box that arrives crushed destroys your brand. Specify ECT 32 (single-wall) or ECT 44 for heavier items.
- **Wrong MOQ** — ordering 100 offset-printed boxes incurs full setup cost, making each box very expensive. Use digital for under 500 pcs.
- **Skipping FSC** — if you later sell to a retailer requiring FSC, you'll need to reprint the entire run.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. What is the MOQ for custom packaging in China?

MOQ varies by box type and printing. Digital-printed mailer boxes start at **100–500** pcs. Offset-printed retail boxes start at **500–1,000** pcs. Rigid gift boxes start at **1,000–3,000** pcs due to higher labor. Kraft boxes with 1-color flexo printing often accept 300–500 pcs.

### 2. How much does custom packaging cost in China?

Custom mailer boxes cost **$0.15–$0.60** each at MOQ 500–5,000, depending on size, paper weight and finishing. Retail boxes cost **$0.20–$1.00**. Rigid gift boxes cost **$0.80–$3.00**. Pricing falls 20–40% at 10,000+ pcs due to setup cost amortization.

### 3. What is a dieline and do I need one?

A dieline is a 2D template (AI or PDF) showing the exact cut, fold and glue lines of your flat box before assembly. Yes, you need one. Most factories provide a **free dieline** once you give them the assembled dimensions (L × W × H) and box type. Your designer places artwork on it with 3 mm bleed.

### 4. Which printing method should I choose?

For full-color CMYK over 1,000 pcs, use **offset printing** (best quality and value). For under 500 pcs, use **digital printing** (no setup fee, slightly lower quality). For kraft mailers with 1–2 colors, use **flexo**. For premium metallic logos, add **hot stamping**.

### 5. Do I need FSC certification for my packaging?

FSC certification is required if you sell to major retailers (Walmart, Target, Amazon's Climate Pledge Friendly) or EU markets with packaging regulations. Many Chinese factories have FSC/PEFC certification; ask for the certificate number and verify it. FSC paper costs 5–15% more.

### 6. How long does custom packaging production take?

Sample/dieline approval takes **3–7 days**. Mass production takes **10–20 days** for mailer/retail boxes and **20–35 days** for rigid gift boxes. Add 5–7 days for ocean freight or 3–5 days for air freight. Total: 3–5 weeks from order to door.

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

- **Using the supplier's test report without verification.** Many factories provide fake or expired CE/FCC reports. Verify the report number on the certification body's website before placing a bulk order.
- **Applying the wrong certification for the destination market.** CE for EU, FCC/CPC for US, UKCA for UK, RCM for Australia. Shipping CE-marked goods to the US = customs seizure and $10k+ fines.
- **No test report for the exact product variant.** A CE report for a red 220V version does not cover a blue 110V version. Each variant needs its own test or a report covering all variants.
- **Forgetting ongoing compliance.** Regulations change (e.g., EU GPSR replacing GPSD, US CPSC updates). A cert valid today may be invalid in 12 months. Track renewal dates and budget for re-testing.
- **Under-declaring product category to skip certification.** Listing a children's toy as 'home decoration' to avoid CPC is customs fraud. The buyer of record (you) carries the liability.
