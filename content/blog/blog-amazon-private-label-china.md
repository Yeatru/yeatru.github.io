## What Is Amazon Private Label from China?

Amazon private label from China is the process of sourcing a generic product from a Chinese factory, applying your own brand (logo, custom retail packaging, insert cards, product manual), registering a trademark, enrolling in Amazon Brand Registry, and selling it as your own brand on Amazon FBA. It is distinct from wholesale (reselling another brand) and arbitrage (buying low, selling high): private label gives you full control over pricing, listing, and brand equity. The China advantage is that OEM branding — silk-screen logos, custom retail boxes, UV printing — costs a fraction of US or EU branding, so you can build a branded product for under **$5/unit** landed and sell it for **$15–$40** on Amazon. For the deep-dive on OEM customization, see OEM private label from China.

## The Private Label Process (5 Steps)

 - **Product research & category selection.** Pick a non-gated category with 30–70% gross margin, under 5 lb, low competition, and no heavy compliance (avoid batteries, supplements, topicals, children's products unless you have the test docs). Validate demand with Jungle Scout or Helium 10 — look for 1,000+ monthly sales at the #10 ranked listing.
 - **Supplier sourcing & OEM quote.** Find 3–5 factories that can do OEM branding. Get quotes for EXW unit price + logo printing + custom retail box + insert card. Verify the factory's business license and ask for Amazon-seller references. See FBA sourcing from China.
 - **Sample approval & branding design.** Order 1–3 pre-production samples with your logo and packaging. Approve print quality, color matching, material, and function. Finalize the retail box dieline, insert card, and manual as print-ready AI/PDF files.
 - **Production + QC + FBA prep.** Place the PO with 30–50% deposit. Run AQL 2.5 QC during/after production, then apply FBA prep (FNSKU labels, polybag, warning labels) at the factory or prep warehouse. See FBA prep in China.
 - **Brand Registry & launch.** File a USPTO trademark, enroll in Amazon Brand Registry 2.0, ship DDP to the FBA fulfillment center, and launch with PPC campaigns. Use A+ Content and Sponsored Brands to differentiate from competitors.

## Logo Printing Methods (Cost, MOQ, Durability)

The logo printing method drives both cost and MOQ. Choose based on product material, color count, and how durable the print needs to be.

 | | Method || Cost / Unit || MOQ || Durability || Best For |

 | Silk screen printing || $0.10 – $0.40 || 300 – 500 pcs || High (won't rub off) || 1–3 colors on flat plastic/metal/glass |

 | Laser engraving || $0.20 – $0.80 || 100 – 300 pcs || Permanent || Metal, wood, leather, acrylic |

 | UV printing || $0.30 – $1.00 || 100 – 500 pcs || High || Full-color photos on curved/irregular surfaces |

 | Pad printing || $0.15 – $0.50 || 300 – 1,000 pcs || Medium-High || Small logos on curved surfaces (bottles, pens) |

 | Sticker / label || $0.05 – $0.20 || 50 – 100 pcs || Low (peels over time) || Low-MOQ launches, temporary branding |

 | Heat transfer || $0.10 – $0.40 || 200 – 500 pcs || Medium || Apparel, fabric, soft goods |

For most Amazon private-label products, **silk screen** (1–2 color logos on plastic/metal) or **UV print** (full-color on any surface) is the sweet spot: durable enough for daily use and cheap enough at 500+ MOQ. Laser engraving is the premium choice for metal and wood products where a permanent, premium look matters.

## Custom Packaging, Inserts & Manuals

Branding extends beyond the logo. The full branding stack includes:

 - **Custom retail box** ($0.30–$3.00/unit) — full-color printed box with your brand, product images, features, and barcode. MOQ 500–1,000 for custom tooling. See [private-label packaging](blog-private-label-packaging-china.html).
 - **Insert card** ($0.05–$0.30/unit) — thank-you message, warranty, product tips, and a request for an honest review. *Amazon TOS prohibits incentivized reviews* — no discounts, free products, or gifts in exchange for reviews. No off-Amazon links or contact info.
 - **Product manual** ($0.10–$0.50/unit) — multi-language quick-start guide. Required for electronics; optional for simple products.
 - **Warranty card** ($0.05–$0.15/unit) — registration card that builds brand loyalty and captures customer emails (compliant as long as it doesn't incentivize reviews).

## Brand Registry & Trademark

Amazon Brand Registry 2.0 is the gate to premium seller tools. To enroll, you need a **registered trademark** (USPTO for the US, EUIPO for the EU, UKIPO for the UK). Key facts:

 - USPTO trademark filing: **$250–$350** per class, takes **8–12 months** to register (but you can list with a pending application).
 - Brand Registry unlocks: **A+ Content** (rich product descriptions), **Brand Analytics** (search term data), **Sponsored Brands** ads, and **trademark protection** (report hijackers and counterfeiters).
 - File the trademark **before** you launch so it's pending when you create the listing — this prevents hijackers from squatting on your brand.
 - For EU sales, you also need a **GPSR Responsible Person** and compliance marks (CE, RoHS, REACH) — see FBA sourcing compliance.

## MOQ Impact on Private Label

Private-label MOQ is driven by branding setup costs, not the product itself. A factory may sell 100 pcs of a stock product, but custom logo printing often needs **300–500 pcs** (printing plate setup) and custom retail boxes need **500–1,000 pcs** (die-cut tooling). This means:

 - **Full private label** (logo + custom box + insert): **500–1,000+ pcs MOQ**.
 - **Light private label** (sticker logo + stock box): **100–300 pcs MOQ**, higher unit cost.
 - Branding setup costs (mold, plate, die-cut): **$100–$500 one-time**, amortized over the MOQ.

For first-time launches, start with **500 pcs** of a single SKU to hit the branding MOQ and validate demand before scaling to 2,000–5,000 pcs.

## Common Private Label Mistakes

 - **No trademark before launch.** A hijacker registers your brand name and you lose Brand Registry access. File USPTO before creating the listing.
 - **Incentivized reviews on insert cards.** Amazon suspends accounts for offering discounts/gifts for reviews. Ask for an honest review only.
 - **Low-MOQ sticker logos that peel.** Customers leave 1-star reviews for peeling logos. Use silk screen or UV print at 500+ MOQ.
 - **Generic packaging with a logo sticker.** Looks cheap and tanks conversion. Invest in a custom retail box — it's the #1 conversion driver after the product itself.
 - **No A+ Content.** Brand Registry sellers with A+ Content see 5–15% higher conversion. Build it from day one.
 - **Ignoring compliance.** Electronics need FCC + UL; toys need CPC; kitchenware needs FDA. Missing docs gate the ASIN. See FBA compliance.

### 2. How much does private label branding cost in China?

Logo printing costs $0.10–$2.00 per unit depending on method (silk screen $0.10–$0.40, laser engraving $0.20–$0.80, UV print $0.30–$1.00, sticker $0.05–$0.20). ### 3. What logo printing methods are available in China?

Silk screen printing is cheapest ($0.10–$0.40/pc) for 1–3 colors on flat surfaces. Laser engraving ($0.20–$0.80) is permanent on metal, wood, and leather. UV print ($0.30–$1.00) does full-color on curved surfaces. Stickers ($0.05–$0.20) are lowest cost but least durable. Choose based on material, color count, and durability needs.

### 4. Do I need Brand Registry for Amazon private label?

Yes, strongly recommended. Amazon Brand Registry 2.0 requires a registered USPTO (or EUIPO/UKIPO) trademark and unlocks A+ Content, Brand Analytics, Sponsored Brands, and trademark protection against hijackers. Trademark registration costs $250–$350 via USPTO and takes 8–12 months; file before launch so it is pending when you list.

### 5. What are the rules for insert cards in Amazon FBA?

Insert cards can include thank-you messages, warranty info, product use tips, and a request for an honest review — but Amazon TOS prohibits incentivized reviews (no discounts, free products, or gifts in exchange for reviews). Never include off-Amazon contact info or links to your own website. Keep inserts under 4×6 inches.

### 6. What is the MOQ for private label in China?

Private label OEM typically requires 500–1,000+ units per SKU because custom logo molds, printing plates, and packaging tooling have setup costs. Logo printing alone often needs 300–500 pcs; custom retail boxes need 500–1,000. Low-MOQ private label (100–300 pcs) is possible with sticker logos and stock packaging, but unit cost is higher.

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

- **No written mold/drawing ownership contract.** By default Chinese law treats the factory as the mold owner. Your contract must state you own the mold, drawings, and tooling upon full payment, with retrieval rights.
- **Signing no NDA before sharing designs.** A factory can legally sell your custom design to competitors if there's no NDA. Sign before sending any CAD files, samples, or spec sheets.
- **Approving production without a signed golden sample.** A verbal 'looks good' leads to 5,000 units that don't match your expectation. Sign and retain a golden sample; production must match it exactly.
- **No MOQ flexibility in the contract.** A 5,000-unit MOQ for your first order is risky. Negotiate a 1,000–2,000 trial MOQ at a slightly higher unit price, then scale up after quality is confirmed.
- **Skipping IP registration in China.** Register your trademark and design patent in China (not just your home country). Chinese trademark law is first-to-file — a factory or competitor can squat your brand if you don't register first.
