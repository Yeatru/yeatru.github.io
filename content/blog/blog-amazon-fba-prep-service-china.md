## What Is Amazon FBA Prep Service?

**Amazon FBA prep service** is the process of preparing products to meet Amazon's fulfillment center requirements before shipment to FBA warehouses. This includes FNSKU barcode labeling poly bagging with suffocation warnings bubble wrapping carton labeling and dimensional compliance. In China FBA prep costs $0.50-$1.50 per unit — 60-70% cheaper than US-based 3PL prep services ($3-5/unit). Chinese FBA prep also includes quality inspection ensuring defective units are removed before they reach Amazon's warehouse.

Amazon FBA has strict fulfillment requirements and a shipment that does not meet them is rejected hit with extra prep fees or stranded at a receiving center. For sellers sourcing from China the smartest move is to do **FBA prep in China** — labeling bagging wrapping and carton [preparation](blog-sourcing-preparation-checklist.html) at the source before the goods ever leave. This 2026 guide explains what FBA prep includes Amazon's requirements the cost advantage of prepping in China versus the US and how to choose a prep service that keeps your shipments compliant.

## What FBA Prep Includes

FBA prep is the set of physical tasks that make a shipment ready for Amazon's fulfillment centers. A complete prep service covers:

 - **FNSKU labeling:** applying the unique Amazon barcode to each unit so Amazon can track it.
 - **Polybagging:** bagging loose or multi-piece items with a suffocation warning.
 - **Bubble wrap:** protecting fragile items from transit damage.
 - **Carton labels:** applying the shipping label and box-content labels to each carton.
 - **Suffocation warnings:** required text on polybags over a certain size.
 - **Palletization:** stacking wrapping and labeling pallets for LTL/FCL shipments.

## Amazon's Strict Prep Requirements

Amazon's FBA prep rules are detailed and enforced. Key requirements include the **50 lb (22.7 kg) carton weight limit** — any carton over this is rejected or subject to a surcharge — precise FNSKU label placement and print quality scannable barcodes suffocation-warning polybags for bagged items and proper expiration-date labeling for applicable products. Cartons must also meet dimensional and durability standards and box-content information must match what Amazon expects. A single non-compliant carton can delay an entire shipment. For the official rules always cross-check Amazon Seller Central as requirements update.

## Why Do Prep in China Not the US

Prepping in China rather than the US saves money and time. The per-unit cost is a fraction of US prep fees and you avoid the extra freight leg of shipping un-prepped goods to a US prep center before they reach Amazon. More importantly prep in China happens in the same warehouse as your [QC inspection](quality-control.html) so defective units are removed before they are labeled and shipped — meaning fewer Amazon returns fewer negative reviews and a lower risk of listing suspension. You also gain control: a single China warehouse handles QC prep labeling and export in one continuous flow.

## Quality Control Before the FBA Shipment

QC is the step that makes FBA prep worth doing in China. Before any unit is labeled or bagged an AQL 2.5 inspection (about USD 120 per man-day) checks the batch against your golden [sample](blog-sample-order-from-china.html). Defective units are pulled the factory reworks or replaces them and only passing units move to prep. Shipping defective units to Amazon is the fastest way to accumulate returns A-to-z claims and suppressed listings — so QC before prep is non-negotiable. ## Common FBA Rejection Reasons

 - Missing or mis-scanned FNSKU labels.
 - Cartons exceeding the 50 lb weight limit.
 - Polybags without suffocation warnings.
 - Box contents not matching the box-content file.
 - Expired or soon-to-expire date labels on applicable products.
 - Insufficient protective wrap causing transit damage.

## How to Choose an FBA Prep Service in China

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

- **Shipping without FBA prep in China.** FBA charges $0.50–$1.50/unit for polybagging and labeling in the US. Doing it in China costs $0.05–$0.15/unit — a 90% saving on 1,000 units = $500–$1,000.
- **Wrong FNSKU labels or case-pack rules.** Mislabeled units = FBA receiving delays, $0.15/unit relabeling fee, and potential account flags. Verify label placement and case-pack quantities before shipping.
- **No compliance documentation for restricted categories.** Children's products need CPC, electronics need FCC, food-contact needs FDA. Missing docs = FBA inventory destroyed, not returned.
- **Underestimating FBA receiving time.** FBA takes 3–7 days to check in inventory after arrival. Factor this into your restock timeline alongside the 25–40 day sea freight.
- **Running out of stock.** A stockout drops your Amazon BSR and you lose ranking to competitors. Maintain a 30-day safety stock and order 45–60 days before depletion.
