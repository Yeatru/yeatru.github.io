## What Is the Yiwu Market?

The Yiwu International Trade City (义乌国际商贸城, Yiwu International Trade Mart) in Yiwu, Zhejiang is the world's largest wholesale market for small commodities — a permanent, indoor complex covering **7.5 million sqm** across 5 districts, with **75,000+ booths** selling **2.1 million+ SKUs** across toys, hardware, kitchenware, jewelry, electronics, garments, and imported goods. Unlike [1688.com](blog-1688-shopping-agent-english.html) (online B2B) or the Canton Fair (twice-yearly), Yiwu is open daily year-round and built for walk-in cash-and-carry wholesale: a buyer can source 200 SKUs from 60 booths, pay 50% deposits, and have everything consolidated and shipped within 3 weeks. It is the default destination for Amazon FBA sellers, dollar-store chains, and TikTok Shop creators running [low-MOQ mixed-category orders](blog-low-moq-sourcing-china.html).

## Booth ≠ Factory: How Yiwu Actually Works

The single most misunderstood thing about Yiwu is that **a booth is a trading company, not a factory**. The person behind the counter sources from one or several factories behind them, marks the goods up 5–20%, holds inventory or orders it on demand, and handles export paperwork through a licensed export-agent entity. Consequences for you:

- **Price is not factory-direct** — but it is not absurd either; the booth markup covers small-quantity flexibility and instant availability.
- **You cannot visit "the factory"** from a booth — the factory is often 1–4 hours away in Wenzhou, Taizhou, or Shantou; an agent can arrange a separate factory audit.
- **The booth's business license** (营业执照) lists "销售" or "贸易" (sales/trade), not "生产" (manufacturing) — verify this before any deposit, using the same method as [verifying any Chinese supplier license](blog-verify-chinese-supplier-license.html).
- **The booth holds the export license** — or, more commonly, uses a separate licensed export agent in Yiwu; you cannot legally clear export yourself without one.

This structure is also why "MOQ 1 carton" is real: the booth already stocks goods, so 1-carton orders cost you list price; 50-carton orders earn 15–25% off.

## Sample & Deposit Workflow at a Yiwu Booth

- **Walk the aisle, photograph SKUs** — note the booth number on the orange cardstock each booth provides; this is your record.
- **Ask for a sample** — most booths hand one free for orders ≥100 cartons, otherwise charge **$5–$50** per sample; pay in cash or WeChat.
- **Negotiate the bulk price** — list drops at 10, 50, and 100 carton tiers; quote your real volume, not aspirations.
- **Pay 50% deposit, get the order card stamped** — the stamped booth card is your contract; specify spec, qty, delivery date, carton marks. Avoid 100% upfront — the deposit logic in [China deposit terms](blog-50-50-deposit-china.html) applies equally at Yiwu booths.
- **Production 7–15 days** — stock SKUs ship in 2–5 days; OEM/private-label runs 15–40 days plus a sample-approval round.
- **Pre-shipment QC at the booth warehouse** — AQL 2.5 inspection with photo/video; reject and rework before balance is paid.
- **Move to consolidation warehouse** — pay the 50% balance only after QC passes; the warehouse combines all your booths into one shipment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. What is Yiwu market?

Yiwu International Trade City (义乌国际商贸城) in Yiwu, Zhejiang is the world's largest wholesale small-commodities market: **7.5 million sqm** across 5 districts, **75,000+ booths**, **2.1M+ SKUs**. Booths are trading companies that aggregate from factories; MOQ starts at 1–10 cartons and the market is open daily year-round.

### 2. Can I buy just one piece at Yiwu?

No — the minimum is **one carton** (often 12–144 pcs depending on SKU). For true 1-piece or sample-quantity orders, use the Yiwu online app or work with an agent who consolidates. For sub-carton volumes, 1688.com with a sourcing agent is usually the better channel.

### 3. Are the booths factories?

No. A booth is a trading company that sources from one or more factories behind it and handles export paperwork. The business license (营业执照) lists "销售" or "贸易" (sales/trade), not "生产" (manufacturing). For factory-direct pricing, ask the booth to disclose the factory or have your agent arrange a separate factory audit.

### 4. Do I need a Yiwu agent?

Not legally — but for first-time or non-Chinese-speaking buyers the math almost always works: an agent saves **10–25% on list price** through accumulated volume, handles booth verification, AQL QC, consolidation, and the export declaration you cannot legally do solo. See Yiwu agent for foreigners for the full breakdown.

### 5. When is Yiwu market open?

Daily **8:30–17:30**, year-round except Chinese public holidays (Spring Festival ~7–10 days in Jan/Feb, National Day Oct 1–7). Best buying windows: **March–June** and **September–early October**; avoid late January and the 2 weeks around Spring Festival when most booths close.

### 6. How do I ship from Yiwu?

Yiwu is inland — there is no seaport. Goods truck **2.5 hours to Ningbo (NGB)** for sea LCL/FCL, or air-freight via **Yiwu-Dongyang airport** or PVG. Quote **DDP door-to-door** through an export agent so the export declaration, customs broker, and last-mile are handled; expect $1.10–$2.20/kg sea LCL DDP to the US for a 7 CBM mixed-SKU consolidation.

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

- **Visiting Yiwu without a pre-trip product list.** The market has 75,000+ booths across 5 districts. Without a targeted list, you waste days walking and buy on impulse. Research products and target districts before you go.
- **Buying from the first booth you see.** The same product is often sold by 20+ booths at different prices and quality. Walk at least 3 booths in the same district before committing.
- **No consolidation plan.** Buying from 10 booths means 10 separate shipments. Arrange a consolidation warehouse before you go — it saves 30–50% on international freight.
- **Accepting 'sample price' as the bulk price.** Booth prices are sample prices (1–5 pcs). Always negotiate the bulk (MOQ) price, packaging, and lead time in writing before leaving the booth.
- **Ignoring minimum order quantities.** Many Yiwu booths have MOQs of 1–2 cartons. If you need smaller quantities, ask about mixed-carton or sample-box pricing before negotiating.
