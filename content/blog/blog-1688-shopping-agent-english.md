## What Is 1688.com?

1688.com is Alibaba Group's **domestic Chinese B2B wholesale marketplace** — essentially the Chinese-language, factory-direct version of Alibaba.com. Whereas Alibaba.com targets international buyers with English product pages, Trade Assurance, and export-ready suppliers, 1688 serves Chinese domestic retailers, Taobao sellers, and small manufacturers. Prices on 1688 are quoted in **RMB (CNY)** at **ex-factory** levels, which is why the same product often costs **30–50% less** on 1688 than on Alibaba.com. The trade-off: the site is Chinese-only, suppliers rarely speak English, there is no buyer-protection escrow, payment requires a Chinese Alipay account, and suppliers ship only within mainland China.

## Why Foreigners Need a 1688 Agent

Browsing 1688 is easy; closing an order from abroad is not. The six blockers that push foreign buyers to use a 1688 agent are:

 - **Supplier verification** — 1688 has many trading companies posing as factories; an agent cross-checks the business license on gsxt.gov.cn before you pay.
 - **No buyer protection** — unlike Alibaba's Trade Assurance, 1688 disputes are resolved under Chinese consumer law in Chinese; a foreign buyer has almost no recourse.

## How to Spot a Real Factory vs a Trading Company on 1688

Many listings on 1688 are from trading companies that add a 15–30% markup. To filter for genuine manufacturers, check these signals on the shop page:

 - **实力商家 (Strength Merchant) badge** — 1688's paid certification that requires a verified factory or registered business; not a guarantee, but a first filter.
 - **Years on 1688** — real factories usually have **5+ years**; trading pop-up shops often disappear within 2 years.
 - **Business scope (经营范围)** — on the shop's 诚信档案 (credit file), check whether the registered business includes 生产 (production/manufacturing) or only 销售 (sales/trading).
 - **Workshop & equipment photos** — factories show production lines, injection machines, or cutting tables; trading companies show only product catalog photos.
 - **gsxt.gov.cn check** — enter the 统一社会信用代码 (Unified Social Credit Code) to confirm the company's registered business type and capital.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. What is 1688.com?

1688.com is Alibaba Group's domestic Chinese B2B wholesale marketplace where Chinese factories and trading companies sell to domestic buyers at ex-factory RMB prices. It is 30–50% cheaper than Alibaba.com, but the interface is Chinese-only, there is no Trade Assurance or international buyer protection, and payment requires a Chinese Alipay account.

### 2. Can foreigners use 1688 directly?

You can browse 1688.com, but placing an order requires a Chinese mobile number, Chinese ID, a domestic Alipay account, and a Chinese bank card. Most suppliers speak only Chinese and do not export or ship overseas. That is why over 95% of foreign buyers use a 1688 purchasing agent.

### 3. How much does a 1688 agent charge?

A 1688 agent typically charges **5–10%** of the order value, with a minimum fee around **$50–100** for small orders. Some agents charge a flat fee per order. The fee covers translation, supplier communication, order placement, QC, consolidation, and export documentation.

### 4. Is 1688 cheaper than Alibaba.com?

Yes, usually 30–50% cheaper for the same product. 1688 prices are ex-factory RMB quotes for the Chinese domestic market, while Alibaba.com prices include export markup, English support, and Trade Assurance fees. A silicone tool listed at ¥15 ($2.10) on 1688 often sells for $3.50–$4.50 on Alibaba.com.

### 5. How do you pay on 1688 as a foreigner?

You cannot pay directly with a foreign credit card or PayPal on 1688. Payment is via Chinese Alipay linked to a domestic bank card. A 1688 agent pays the supplier in RMB on your behalf, and then you pay the agent by wire transfer, PayPal, or XTransfer for the goods value plus agent fee.

### 6. Can 1688 suppliers ship directly to my country?

Generally no. 1688 suppliers handle only domestic logistics within China. To get goods overseas, you need a 1688 agent or freight forwarder to pick up from the factory, consolidate multiple orders, clear export customs, and ship by air, sea, or DDP to your country.

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

- **Visiting Yiwu without a pre-trip product list.** The market has 75,000+ booths across 5 districts. Without a targeted list, you waste days walking and buy on impulse. Research products and target districts before you go.
- **Buying from the first booth you see.** The same product is often sold by 20+ booths at different prices and quality. Walk at least 3 booths in the same district before committing.
- **No consolidation plan.** Buying from 10 booths means 10 separate shipments. Arrange a consolidation warehouse before you go — it saves 30–50% on international freight.
- **Accepting 'sample price' as the bulk price.** Booth prices are sample prices (1–5 pcs). Always negotiate the bulk (MOQ) price, packaging, and lead time in writing before leaving the booth.
- **Ignoring minimum order quantities.** Many Yiwu booths have MOQs of 1–2 cartons. If you need smaller quantities, ask about mixed-carton or sample-box pricing before negotiating.
