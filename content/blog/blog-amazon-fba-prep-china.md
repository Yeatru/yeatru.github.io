## What Is Amazon FBA Prep in China?

Amazon FBA prep in China is the process of making factory-finished units compliant with Amazon's fulfillment-center receiving rules *before* they leave the country. It covers FNSKU barcode labeling, polybagging with suffocation warnings, bubble-wrap for fragile SKUs, set/sticker removal, expiration and lot-code dating for food and beauty, carton consolidation, and palletization — plus the DDP handoff to the assigned Amazon fulfillment center. Done in China at the factory or a prep warehouse, it costs a fraction of US-side prep and avoids Amazon's $0.50–$2.00/unit return fees for non-compliant inbound. Before prep, confirm your SKU isn't gated — check [Amazon restricted products from China](blog-amazon-restricted-products-china.html).

## FNSKU Labeling Rules

The FNSKU (Fulfillment Network Stock Keeping Unit) is Amazon's internal barcode for your ASIN. Amazon scans this at receiving, so a misprinted or unreadable label means the unit is returned or relabeled at your cost.

 - **Cover the original factory barcode** completely — Amazon must not scan two barcodes.
 - Place the FNSKU on a **flat surface**, at least **1 inch (2.5 cm) from edges, folds, and seams**.
 - Use **600-dpi thermal labels**, not inkjet — inkjet smudges in humidity and fails Amazon scanners.
 - Scan every label with a handheld scanner during QC to confirm readability before sealing the carton.

For products where the manufacturer barcode (UPC/EAN) is already registered in Amazon's Brand Registry, you may use **Manufacturer Barcode (Stickerless)** — but most private-label sellers stick with FNSKU labels for control.

## FBA Carton & Pallet Rules

After units are pre-labeled, they go into cartons and then onto pallets for LTL/SPD inbound. Amazon rejects cartons that violate the rules below.

 | | Rule || Requirement || Common Failure |

 | SKU per carton || Single SKU per carton (mixed-SKU needs case-ready prep) || Two ASINs in one box → manual sort fee |

 | Carton weight || Max **50 lb / 22.7 kg** (70 lb if one oversized unit) || Overweight → $25–$50 fee |

 | Box strength || Pass **ISTA 6-Amazon** drop test || Crushed box → refused at dock |

 | Box taping || No taping two boxes together || Bundled boxes → returned |

 | FBA box ID label || Label on **2 sides**, not over seam || Missing label → receiving delay |

 | Pallet size || **40×48 inch** standard pallet || Wrong size → re-pallet charge |

 | Pallet height || Max **72 inches** including pallet || Overheight → refused |

 | Pallet labels || 4-side labels with shipment ID || Missing → lost in FC |

## FBA Prep Process in China (5 Steps)

 - **Prep mapping at PO stage.** Before production, define the prep type per SKU and confirm the factory can do it in-house or route to a prep warehouse. Send FNSKU label PDFs and suffocation-warning artwork.
 - **Inline QC during production.** Factory applies polybag, FNSKU, bubble wrap as units come off the line. Spot-check 5% for barcode scannability and warning-label placement.
 - **AQL 2.5 pre-shipment inspection.** A third-party inspector (or Yeatru QC) checks barcode scannability, polybag seals, warning labels, carton weight, and FBA box ID labels. See the full prep checklist.
 - **Carton & pallet consolidation.** Units packed into single-SKU cartons (≤50 lb), palletized 40×48 × ≤72″, 4-side labeled with the Amazon shipment ID generated from Seller Central.
 - **DDP shipping to the FBA fulfillment center.** Forwarder ships LCL/FCL or air to the assigned FC, clears customs, and handles last-mile. Amazon receives and scans units into inventory. See FBA shipping from China.

## Common FBA Prep Mistakes (and What They Cost)

 - **Inkjet FNSKU labels.** Smudge in transit → 5–15% of units un-scannable → Amazon relabels at $0.20/unit or returns. Use 600-dpi thermal.
 - **Missing suffocation warning.** Units with bag openings ≥5″ lack the warning → Amazon refuses the whole carton. Cost: $25–$100 return fee + re-ship.
 - **Original barcode not covered.** Amazon scans the factory UPC instead of FNSKU → inventory goes to the wrong ASIN → stranded stock.
 - **Overweight cartons.** Over 50 lb triggers $25–$50 fee per carton and can damage units underneath. Weigh every carton.
 - **Taping two boxes together.** Amazon requires single boxes; bundled boxes are returned at the seller's cost.
 - **Wrong pallet size.** Non-40×48 pallets require re-palletizing at $40–$80/pallet at the destination.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. What are Amazon FBA prep requirements?

Every FBA unit needs an FNSKU barcode on a flat scannable surface 1 inch from edges, original factory barcode covered, polybag with suffocation warning on openings 5 inches or larger, bubble wrap for fragile SKUs, expiration dating for food and beauty, and cartons under 50 lb / 22.7 kg with FBA box ID labels on two sides. See the full FBA prep requirements checklist.

### 2. How do I label FNSKU correctly?

Print FNSKU barcodes on 600-dpi thermal labels, cover the factory UPC, place on a flat surface 1 inch from edges and folds, and scan each label with a handheld scanner to confirm readability. Inkjet labels smudge and are rejected by Amazon.

### 3. When is a suffocation warning required on polybags?

Amazon requires an English suffocation warning printed on any polybag with an opening of 5 inches or larger. The warning must be on the bag itself, not a sticker, and the bag must be 1.5 mil minimum thickness with the FNSKU visible through transparent material.

### 4. Do food and beauty products need expiration dates?

Yes. Perishable SKUs must show expiration date, lot number, and manufacture date in YYYY-MM-DD format, printed not handwritten, with at least 90 days remaining shelf life when received by Amazon. Expired units are destroyed at the seller's cost.

### 5. How much does FBA prep cost in China vs the US?

In China, polybag + FNSKU label costs $0.10–$0.30 per unit, bubble wrap $0.15–$0.30, taping $0.05–$0.10. A US prep center charges $0.50–$1.50 per unit for the same work. On 1,000 units, China prep costs $180 vs $750 in the US.

### 6. Should FBA prep be done at the factory or by an agent?

Factory prep is cheapest ($0.05–$0.20/unit) but quality varies; an agent or prep warehouse costs $0.10–$0.30/unit and guarantees compliance with QC photos. Use factory prep for high-volume standard SKUs and agent prep for fragile, regulated, or multi-SKU orders.

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

- **Shipping without FBA prep in China.** FBA charges $0.50–$1.50/unit for polybagging and labeling in the US. Doing it in China costs $0.05–$0.15/unit — a 90% saving on 1,000 units = $500–$1,000.
- **Wrong FNSKU labels or case-pack rules.** Mislabeled units = FBA receiving delays, $0.15/unit relabeling fee, and potential account flags. Verify label placement and case-pack quantities before shipping.
- **No compliance documentation for restricted categories.** Children's products need CPC, electronics need FCC, food-contact needs FDA. Missing docs = FBA inventory destroyed, not returned.
- **Underestimating FBA receiving time.** FBA takes 3–7 days to check in inventory after arrival. Factor this into your restock timeline alongside the 25–40 day sea freight.
- **Running out of stock.** A stockout drops your Amazon BSR and you lose ranking to competitors. Maintain a 30-day safety stock and order 45–60 days before depletion.
