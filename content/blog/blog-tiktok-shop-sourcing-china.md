## What Is Sourcing for TikTok Shop from China?

Sourcing for TikTok Shop from China is the end-to-end process of finding, sampling, ordering, and shipping products that will sell on TikTok's video-driven marketplace. It differs from Amazon sourcing in three ways: **speed matters more than lowest price** (trends die in weeks), **MOQ must be low** (you test before you scale), and **the product must be visually demo-friendly** (it has to look good in a 15-second video). The typical flow takes a product from idea to listed in 2–4 weeks, using small air-freighted test orders and fast-responding Chinese suppliers.

## Step-by-Step Breakdown

 - **Product research** — use [TikTok trend tools](blog-tiktok-shop-product-trends.html) (Creative Center, #TikTokMadeMeBuyIt, Kalodata) to find products with rising demand and fewer than 500 competing sellers. Target lightweight goods ($1–$5 EXW) that demo well on video.
 - **Supplier sourcing** — post RFQs on Alibaba, search 1688, visit Yiwu, or use a sourcing agent. Prioritize suppliers that accept **100–500 pcs MOQ** and produce in **7–15 days**. Request 3 quotes and compare landed cost, not just unit price.
 - **Sample & test** — order **1–3 samples** ($20–$100 each, 3–7 days by express). Check build quality, packaging, and that the product matches the supplier's photos. If it fails, move to the next supplier.
 - **Small test order** — place **100–500 pcs** and ship by **air DDP** (5–9 days). This is your validation batch — small enough to limit loss, large enough to test real demand.
 - **TikTok listing** — create the listing with 3–5 short demo videos, upload required certificates, price at **3–6x landed cost**, and set up 14-day free returns.
 - **Launch & monitor** — post videos and run small ad tests. Watch **conversion rate, cost per click, and sell-through** for the first 7 days. If the product sells through 50%+ of the test batch at target margin, it's a winner.
 - **Scale** — reorder **1,000–5,000 pcs**, switch bulk to sea freight to cut cost, and keep air top-ups for spikes. Add [private label](blog-oem-private-label-china.html) once the SKU is stable.

## TikTok-Specific Requirements

 - **Fast shipping** — TikTok expects delivery in 3–7 days; ship test orders by air and keep US 3PL stock.
 - **Compliance** — forbidden products (counterfeit, weapons, adult, tobacco) are auto-removed; collect FCC/FDA/CPC certificates by category.
 - **Returns** — 14-day free return window; factor return shipping into margin.
 - **Video-ready product** — the product must have a clear visual hook; if it doesn't demo in 15 seconds, it won't sell on TikTok.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. How do I source products for TikTok Shop from China?

Follow a 7-step process: research the product and validate demand, find suppliers on Alibaba/1688/Yiwu or via an agent, order 1-3 samples, place a 100-500 piece test order shipped by air DDP, list on TikTok, monitor the first 7 days, then scale to larger orders and sea freight once proven.

### 2. What is a good MOQ for a first TikTok Shop order?

Start with 100-500 pieces. Many Yiwu and 1688 suppliers accept MOQs as low as 100 for stock items. Keeping the first order small limits risk while you validate that the product actually sells on TikTok.

### 3. How long does TikTok Shop sourcing from China take?

Samples take 3-7 days by express. A 100-500 piece test order takes 7-15 days of production plus 5-9 days of air DDP shipping, so plan 12-24 days total from order to stock in your US warehouse.

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

- **Accepting a flat DDP rate without line items.** A real DDP quote breaks out freight, duty, VAT, clearance, and last-mile. A single per-kg number hides costs that surface as surprise invoices or low-quality service.
- **Choosing CIF for control of freight.** Under CIF the supplier picks the carrier and inflates freight by 15–25%. Choose FOB (you control the line) or DDP (all-in, your forwarder) — never CIF unless you have no other option.
- **No transit-time buffer for FBA restocks.** Sea freight from China runs 25–40 days + 5–7 days FBA receiving. Order 45–60 days before you need stock in FBA, not 30. Running out of stock kills your Amazon ranking.
- **Ignoring ISPM-15 for wooden pallets.** EU and US customs require heat-treated pallets with the IPPC stamp. Missing stamps = container detained, €400–$600 fumigation, 3–7 day delay.
- **No cargo insurance.** Freight forwarders' liability is limited to ~$2/kg by convention. For high-value cargo, buy separate all-risks insurance (0.3–0.5% of value) — it costs $50 and pays out $10,000.
