## What Is Yiwu International Trade City?

**Yiwu International Trade City** is the world's largest wholesale market located in Yiwu Zhejiang Province China. It spans 4 million square meters across 5 districts with 75,000+ stalls selling 400,000+ product categories. Unlike factory-direct sourcing Yiwu serves as a consolidated wholesale hub where buyers can source mixed small orders from multiple suppliers in one location with MOQs as low as 1-10 cartons per item.

## Why Yiwu Is the World's Sourcing Capital

[Yiwu International Trade City](http://en.yiwu.gov.cn?v=20260909k) (also called Futian Market) is the largest small commodity wholesale market in the world. With 75,000+ booths across 5 districts it's the single most concentrated sourcing location for Amazon FBA Shopify and retail buyers. If you're looking for consumer goods at factory-direct prices Yiwu should be your first stop.

## Best Time to Visit Yiwu

The market is open year-round but timing matters:

 - **March-April and September-October:** Best weather new product launches
 - **Avoid:** Chinese New Year (1-2 weeks closure) National Day holiday (crowded)
 - **Daily hours:** 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM most booths close on Sunday afternoon

## Getting There: Transportation Tips

Yiwu is accessible via:

 - **Yiwu Airport (YIW):** Domestic flights from major Chinese cities
 - **High-speed train:** 1.5 hrs from Hangzhou 2.5 hrs from Shanghai
 - **Market shuttle:** Free shuttle connects districts inside the market

* **Pro Tip:** Stay in the Futian District near the market. Most hotels offer free breakfast and are within walking distance of the main entrance. Book 2-3 days minimum — you'll need more time than you think.

## Negotiation Strategy for First-Time Buyers

Yiwu suppliers are used to international buyers but negotiation skills still matter:

 - Ask for the "FOB Ningbo" price not the retail price
 - Mention your expected order volume (even if it's small)
 - Get quotes from at least 3-5 booths selling the same product
 - Don't accept the first price — 10-20% reduction is normal with volume
 - Ask for samples before placing bulk orders

## MOQs: What to Expect

MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity) varies by product and supplier:

 - **Small accessories:** 100-500 pcs per style
 - **Household items:** 50-200 pcs per style
 - **Textiles:** 500-1,000 pcs per design
 - **Mixed orders:** Many suppliers accept mixed products from the same category

## Working with a Local Yiwu Agent: Is It Worth It?

For first-time buyers a local agent usually saves more than their fee. Here's why:

 - They know which booths are factories vs. trading companies
 - They negotiate in Mandarin and get better prices
 - They handle quality inspection and [shipping](logistics-shipping.html) logistics
 - They can consolidate orders from multiple suppliers
 - They provide product photos and samples without you traveling

## [Payment](payment.html) Methods: Safe Options

Stick to these payment methods for security:

 - **50% deposit + 50% before shipment** (standard for established suppliers)
 - **Trade Assurance** through [Alibaba](blog-alibaba-vs-agent.html) for first orders
 - **Bank transfer (T/T)** for larger orders
 - **Avoid:** 100% upfront payment to new suppliers

## Final Checklist Before You Leave

 - Collected business cards from all suppliers you're interested in
 - Taken photos of products with booth numbers
 - Confirmed MOQ price lead time and payment terms in writing
 - Arranged [sample](blog-sample-order-from-china.html) shipping (DHL/FedEx typically 3-5 days)
 - Verified supplier has export license and can handle FOB terms

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

- **Visiting Yiwu without a pre-trip product list.** The market has 75,000+ booths across 5 districts. Without a targeted list, you waste days walking and buy on impulse. Research products and target districts before you go.
- **Buying from the first booth you see.** The same product is often sold by 20+ booths at different prices and quality. Walk at least 3 booths in the same district before committing.
- **No consolidation plan.** Buying from 10 booths means 10 separate shipments. Arrange a consolidation warehouse before you go — it saves 30–50% on international freight.
- **Accepting 'sample price' as the bulk price.** Booth prices are sample prices (1–5 pcs). Always negotiate the bulk (MOQ) price, packaging, and lead time in writing before leaving the booth.
- **Ignoring minimum order quantities.** Many Yiwu booths have MOQs of 1–2 cartons. If you need smaller quantities, ask about mixed-carton or sample-box pricing before negotiating.
