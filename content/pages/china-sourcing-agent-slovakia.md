## One agency, five handovers removed

A buyer dealing directly with Chinese suppliers typically coordinates five separate parties: the factory, an inspection company, a freight forwarder, a customs broker and a warehouse. The point of one-stop procurement is that these handovers stop being your job.

## What a Slovak order has to get right

### VAT and EORI

Slovakia raised its standard VAT rate to **23%** on 1 January 2025, with a 19% reduced rate for specified goods. Commercial imports need an EU EORI number and a Slovak VAT registration to recover import VAT; without them the 23% is a permanent cost, so the importer of record is agreed before quoting.

### Product rules

CE marking, REACH, RoHS, GPSR and the applicable product directives apply in full. Consumer information has to be in Slovak, and the technical file has to exist before the goods are placed on the market — not after a customer complaint.

### Extended producer responsibility

Packaging registration through an authorised Slovak scheme such as ENVI-PAK or NATUR-PACK, electrical goods through a WEEE collective scheme, batteries separately. These are producer obligations; the factory cannot absorb them and neither can the agent.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do you only source certain categories?

   No. Yeatru is a procurement agency, so the category follows the buyer's business, not our catalogue. The compliance file is rebuilt for every product — a toy needs EN 71, a lamp needs LVD and EMC, a textile needs fibre and care labelling — but the sourcing, sampling and inspection process is the same.

### Is the VAT rate in Slovakia still 20%?

   No. The standard rate became 23% on 1 January 2025, with a 19% reduced rate for specified goods. If a supplier quotes you "20% Slovak VAT", the figure is out of date and the landed cost will be short by three points.

### Which port serves Slovakia best?

   There is no single answer. Koper with a rail or truck leg into Bratislava is usually the fastest southern route; Hamburg or Bremerhaven suits full containers and northern sailings. We price both for the same shipment when the volume justifies it, then let you choose on cost and transit rather than on habit.

### What is the minimum order?
   50 pieces per SKU for custom and private-label work, with mixed SKUs consolidated into one shipment; stock items from a single piece. Orders below USD 1,000 carry the published 10% low-order rate.
  Who handles packaging EPR registration?

   The producer placing packaged goods on the Slovak market. We flag it before production, explain what has to be registered and when, and can point you to the scheme — but your company is the registrant, because the obligation is attached to the seller, not to the factory or the agent.
