## Key Considerations for Kitchen Appliances & Small Home Appliances Sourcing

 - All electric kitchen appliances need CE (EU) / ETL/UL (US) safety marking and RoHS (EU) compliance. Yeatru verifies test reports before factory approval.
 - Battery-powered portable appliances (juicers, blenders) need UN38.3 battery safety certification for air shipping.
 - Waffle makers and heating appliances should use food-grade non-stick coatings (LFGB/FDA). Avoid uncertified PFOA-based coatings.
 - Countertop appliances are high-volume Amazon categories — order 2–3 months before holiday season (Q4) to avoid factory bottlenecks.
