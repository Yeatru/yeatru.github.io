## C · Lithuania set — GPSR compliance focus

Lead hook: your label is the first thing a customs officer reads.

Your label is the first thing a customs officer reads. Is it compliant?

A Lithuanian founder worked with Yeatru Sourcing to launch his brand's first product — a 7-component own-brand set (YCS-LTW-902A, all black). Before a single label was printed, Yeatru's team:

✅ Wrote the spec sheet — component, material, dimensions, colour, per-set quantity — so factory and buyer worked from one document
✅ Designed the label with the required safety block: not a toy / do not ingest activated-charcoal pouches / keep away from fire
✅ Checked it against EU GPSR (in force since Dec 2024) requiring an EU-based responsible person and traceability on the product

Result: the set shipped compliant, with documentation that survives a customs check.

A wrong label isn't a small mistake — it's a seized shipment, a marketplace takedown, or a product recall. Yeatru Sourcing catches it before it goes to print.
