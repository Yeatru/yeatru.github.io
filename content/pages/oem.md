 FAQ

## OEM Customization FAQs

 What is the minimum order quantity for OEM?

Logo and label customisation on existing products starts at 50-100 pcs. Printed packaging and inserts usually start at 200-500 pcs, and private mold development falls in the 1,000–3,000 pcs range depending on tooling complexity. We always present the lowest-MOQ option available for your product.

 How long does a private mold take?

Private mold tooling takes 15-25 days. Sample production from new mold: 7-15 days. Total concept-to-sample: 25-40 days. Rush options available.

 Can you match a product I found on Amazon or Alibaba?

Yes. Send us a product link or photo. We identify the factory quote modifications (logo color packaging) and produce samples. Reverse-OEM is our specialty.

 Do I need to provide print-ready artwork?

We accept AI PSD PDF or even a rough sketch. Our design team creates print-ready files from your concept at no extra charge. We also design from scratch if you need it.

 Is my design protected?

Yes. All clients sign an NDA before we share any designs. We do not share client products logos or mold designs with other buyers. Full IP protection policy in place.
