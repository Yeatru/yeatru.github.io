 FAQ

## Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our service plans and pricing

### What's the difference between Plan 1 and Plan 2?

**Plan 1 (Order Management — 5%, flat):** You already have supplier links. We arrange orders, follow production and conduct basic inspection. Minimum USD 50 per order.

**Plan 2 (Full Sourcing — 5–8%, tiered):** You only have photos or model names. We source manufacturers from scratch, compare prices, verify specifications and handle everything end-to-end. Minimum USD 100 per formal order.

### How is the commission calculated for Plan 2?

Plan 2 is charged at **5–8%, of order value**, tiered by order size and complexity. The exact rate is quoted in writing before production starts.

The minimum service fee is USD 100 per formal order. Larger orders benefit from lower commission rates within the 5–8%, range.

### Why can't I get quotes for my entire product catalogue at once?

Continuous full-catalogue inquiry for dozens or hundreds of items consumes massive manpower. More importantly, factories will treat us as price shoppers without real orders, which means they won't offer their best pricing.

To provide accurate competitive quotes, we need you to first **select target items** and **confirm estimated order quantities** for each model. This ensures factories take the inquiry seriously and offer their best rates.

### What information should I prepare before contacting you?

To help us serve you efficiently, please prepare:

 - Product styles/models — clear photos, reference links or design files
 - Estimated order quantity for each item (MOQ-related)
 - Specification requirements — material, size, colour, packaging
 - Target market (affects certification requirements)
 - Budget range and timeline

Check our detailed [sourcing preparation checklist](blog-sourcing-preparation-checklist.html) for more guidance.

### Can I switch between plans?

Yes, you can switch between Plan 1 and Plan 2 based on your project needs. Some clients use Plan 2 for initial product sourcing, then switch to Plan 1 for repeat orders once the supplier is established. There are no lock-in contracts.

### What value-added services does a China sourcing agent provide?

Beyond finding factories and negotiating prices, Yeatru provides value-added services that most overseas buyers cannot run themselves:

 - **Supplier verification and factory audits**
 - **AQL 2.5 pre-shipment inspection** with photo and video evidence
 - **Consolidation and warehousing** in Yiwu
 - **Private-label and custom packaging** coordination
 - **DDP door-to-door shipping**

These value-added services are bundled into our service plans at no extra markup — you pay the commission only on confirmed orders.

### Are there any hidden fees besides the commission?

No hidden fees. The commission covers our core service. The only additional costs are third-party expenses passed through at cost:

 - Lab testing fees (if certification is required)
 - Specialized third-party inspection (beyond standard AQL 2.5)
 - Express shipping for samples (if requested)
