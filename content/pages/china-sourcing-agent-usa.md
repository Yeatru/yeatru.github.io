## Navigating US Compliance from the First Sample

The USA has a complex product safety landscape. CPSIA for children's products, FDA for food contact, FCC/UL for electronics, Prop 65 for California chemical warnings, and TSCA for new chemical substances. Section 301 tariffs (25% on many Chinese goods) are a cost factor. We help US importers navigate every step: coordinating compliant factories, securing test reports at SGS/Intertek partner labs, preparing CPSC documentation, and filing correct customs entries with your EIN or IOR.

For Amazon US FBA sellers we also do full FNSKU labeling polybags case-ready palletization and pallet size matching for ONT8 / LAX9 / SBD1 / BWI3 or any other FBA code.

## Frequently Asked Questions — USA · Canada · North America

###

 **How do you handle US compliance (CPSIA, FDA, FCC, UL) for my product?**

 Before production we review your product category. Children's products need CPSIA (lead ≤100ppm, phthalates ≤0.1%). Food-contact products need FDA food-safe declarations. Electronic devices need FCC Part 15 and UL. We only quote from factories with valid test reports and co-ordinate SGS/Intertek third-party testing at cost — you see the original lab invoice, no markup.

###

 **Can you do DDP delivery to Amazon FBA warehouses in the USA?**

 Yes — and we do it daily. Our DDP to Amazon FBA includes: China export, freight to LA/Chicago airport or seaport, US customs clearance, all duties including Section 301, ISF filing, and final delivery to your FBA warehouse (ONT8, LAX9, SBD1, etc.) with proper pallet labeling. ETA: 6–10 days air, 21–28 days sea.

###

 **I am a small US LLC with no customs broker. Can you handle everything?**

 Yes. Yeatru provides full Importer of Record (IOR) services under our licensed US customs broker. Small orders under $800 are duty-free (de minimis). Larger shipments need an EIN (we help you register for free). We handle ISF, HTS classification, duty calculation, and delivery to your door or FBA warehouse.
