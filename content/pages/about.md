### Company Profile

Yeatru Sourcing (YIWU ETRUE TRADING CO., LTD.) was registered in Yiwu on 21 February 2022 — four years in business. We are a young, founder-led company, and we do not pretend otherwise. What we do have is 14+ years of founder-level experience in product compliance, quality management, factory auditing, procurement and export logistics.

That experience is the service. New sellers do not need another agency talking down to them — they need someone who has already made the expensive mistakes. We take the low-MOQ and mixed small orders larger agents decline, we publish our commission instead of quoting it in private, and we stay on the order from supplier vetting through AQL inspection to DDP delivery. First-time importers, Amazon and Shopify sellers testing a product, and small B2B buyers opening a new category are who we are built for.

Established B2B buyers are equally welcome — wholesalers, distributors and private-label brands who want a Yiwu-based buying arm without the fixed cost of a full buying office. Same team, same published fees, larger volumes.

 Our Story

## From Yiwu to the World

### Founded Where the World's Best Products Are Made

Yeatru Sourcing was registered in 2022 in Yiwu, Zhejiang Province — home to the Yiwu International Trade City, the world's largest small-commodity wholesale market with over 75,000 booths across 26 product categories. Four years on, we are still a small company, and that is deliberate: it keeps us close to the orders we handle.

Our founder, Neil Liu, brought 14+ years of hands-on experience in China manufacturing, supplier management, quality control and international logistics into the company from day one. That operator background — not a long corporate history — is what we put to work on every order. We have helped Amazon FBA sellers launch private-label products, local retailers source cost-competitive inventory, and wholesale buyers build long-term supplier partnerships.

Our mission is simple: make sourcing from China transparent, affordable and survivable for people placing their first or fiftieth order — one order at a time.

 Common Questions

## About Working with Yeatru

 What types of buyers do you work with?

We serve Amazon FBA sellers TikTok Shop sellers local retailers wholesale distributors and e-commerce entrepreneurs. From first-time importers to established brands expanding their China supply chain.

 How is Yeatru different from a trading company?

Trading companies mark up product prices 15-30% above factory cost. We charge a transparent flat fee or percentage — and our fee is disclosed upfront. You always get the factory-direct price.

 Do you work with small orders?

Yes. We're built for small-to-medium buyers. Many of our suppliers accept MOQs from 1 pc on in-stock SKUs, up to 50-100 pieces on custom items. We help you start small and scale up.

 What happens if a supplier fails to deliver?

We mediate between you and the supplier. If the supplier is at fault we negotiate compensation source alternative factories and cover the difference in costs if applicable under our service guarantee.

 How do I get started?

The easiest way: send us your product requirements via our contact form or WhatsApp. We'll reply within 24 hours with initial quotes and a sourcing plan.
