 Definition

## What “value-added services” means in sourcing

A sourcing agent’s core job is to find a factory, agree a price and place the order. Everything a buyer needs after that point — proving the factory is real, checking the goods before they ship, holding cartons from three suppliers in one place, printing your logo on the packaging, sticking FNSKU labels on for Amazon, and moving the goods door-to-door — is what the industry calls a value-added service.

The term is deliberately vague, which is why two quotes for the same product can differ by 15% and still be described as “the same service”. Below is exactly what we run, and whether it sits inside the commission or is billed at cost.

## Eight value-added services we actually run

### Supplier verification & factory audit

Business licence and scope check, then a factory visit covering capacity, QC system and packaging lines. You get photo and video evidence, not a supplier name.

### AQL 2.5 pre-shipment inspection

Sampling to AQL 2.5 before the balance payment. Defect counts, measurements and photos are reported against your spec sheet, so the decision to ship or rework is yours.

### Multi-supplier consolidation

Cartons from different factories arrive at our Yiwu warehouse, get checked in, and stay there free for 15 days — then ship as one consignment instead of six.

### Private label & custom packaging

Artwork and dieline review, print proof approval, then the production run. Custom packaging carries 8–12% commission and typically a 50–100 pc MOQ.

### Amazon FBA prep

FNSKU labelling, poly bagging with the suffocation warning, carton marking and weight checks, so cartons enter Amazon’s inbound flow without a rework invoice.

### Sample management

We collect samples from several factories, photograph each one against the spec, and send a single consolidated parcel instead of five separate courier bills.

### DDP door-to-door shipping

Air, sea or rail with duty and import VAT included, quoted per shipment against your address and HS code. No surprise charges at destination.

### Export documents & payment milestones

PI, CI, PL, certificate of origin and HS codes issued in-house; supplier payments released in stages against inspection and loading evidence.

## Value-added services — questions buyers ask

### What are value-added services in China sourcing?

Services a buyer needs beyond finding a factory and agreeing a price: supplier verification and factory audit, AQL 2.5 pre-shipment inspection, multi-supplier consolidation and storage, private label and custom packaging, Amazon FBA prep, sample management, DDP shipping and export documentation.

### Are value-added services included in the commission?

Verification, inspection, consolidation, 15 days of storage and export documents sit inside the 5–8% Full Sourcing commission. Freight, duty, lab tests, certifications and packaging tooling are billed at cost with no markup added by us.

### How is Amazon FBA prep priced?

FNSKU labelling, poly bagging and carton marking are quoted per carton alongside the freight quote, so preparation and shipping appear as one landed cost instead of three separate invoices.

### Do you provide these services if I already have my own suppliers?

Yes. Order Management covers inspection, consolidation, documentation and shipping on orders you place yourself, at a flat 5% with a USD 50 minimum fee per order.

### Can I buy inspection as a standalone service?

No. An inspector who did not source the goods cannot judge a spec sheet against the factory’s real capability, so inspection only runs on orders we place or manage. For standalone inspections we recommend a dedicated third-party inspection company.

### How much storage is included before shipping?

Consolidated cartons stay in our Yiwu warehouse free for 15 days from check-in — normally enough to collect goods from several suppliers before a single shipment leaves.
