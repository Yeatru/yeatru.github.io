## Navigating EU Compliance from the First Sample

Spain and the EU enforce some of the strictest product safety and import rules in Europe. CE marking, WEEE registration for electrical goods, REACH SVHC compliance for chemicals, AECOSAN food-contact testing and GPSR (General Product Safety Regulation) are often deal-breakers for new Spanish importers. We help Spanish and Iberian clients navigate every step: coordinating compliant factories, securing test reports at SGS/TÜV/Intertek partner labs, preparing EU Declaration of Conformity documentation and filing correct customs entries with your Spanish EORI. For Amazon.es sellers we also handle FNSKU labeling, polybags, Spanish-language choking-hazard warnings and marketplace compliance paperwork.

For Amazon.es FBA sellers we also do full FNSKU labeling, polybags, "Peligro de asfixia" Spanish-language choking warning stickers required by Amazon Spain and case-ready palletization for MAD1 / BCN1 / VLC1 / SEV1 or any other Amazon.es FBA code.

When we provide a **written DDP quote** specific to your product destination and weight the total price explicitly INCLUDES the following 6 items:

## Frequently Asked Questions — Spain · Portugal · Canary Islands · Iberian Peninsula

###

 **How do you handle EU compliance (CE / REACH / GPSR) for my product?**

 Before production we review whether your product category is regulated (toys electronics cosmetics PPE food contact medical). If yes we only send you quotes from factories that already hold valid CE/LFGB/ROHS test reports. If certification must be freshly issued we coordinate TÜV/SGS/Intertek testing at cost — you see the original lab invoice no markup. DoC files are yours to keep.

###

 **Can you do DDP delivery to a residential address in Spain or Portugal?**

 Yes. Our DDP ES/PT service includes: China export, international freight, EU customs clearance, EU import duty, 21% VAT (or 10%/4% reduced rates), and final-mile courier via Correos/SEUR/GLS/DHL to any código postal in Spain and Portugal. Canary Islands require separate customs paperwork — just mention it upfront.

###

 **I am a private individual (particular) in Spain, no registered business. Can I still import?**

 Yes. For private individuals in the EU small sample orders under ~€22 are usually duty-free and VAT-free. Larger commercial shipments (≥ €150) are much simpler with a registered business + EU EORI but we can still ship DDP to your home address using an importer-of-record. We'll explain the options before you pay anything.
