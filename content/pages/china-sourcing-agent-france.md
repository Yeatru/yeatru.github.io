## Navigating EU Compliance from the First Sample

France and the EU enforce some of the strictest product safety and import rules in Europe. CE marking, WEEE registration for electrical goods, REACH SVHC compliance for chemicals, DGCCRF food-contact testing and GPSR (General Product Safety Regulation) are often deal-breakers for new French importers. We help French and Francophone EU clients navigate every step: coordinating compliant factories, securing test reports at SGS/TÜV/Intertek partner labs, preparing EU Declaration of Conformity documentation and filing correct customs entries with your French EORI. For Amazon.fr and Cdiscount sellers we also handle FNSKU labeling, polybags, French-language choking-hazard warnings and marketplace compliance paperwork.

For Amazon.fr FBA sellers we also do full FNSKU labeling, polybags, "Danger d'étouffement" French-language choking warning stickers required by Amazon France and case-ready palletization for FR1 / FR2 / FR3 / FR4 / FR5 / FR6 / FR7 or any other Amazon.fr FBA code.

## Frequently Asked Questions — France · Belgium · Luxembourg · Francophone Europe

###

 **How do you handle EU compliance (CE / REACH / LFGB) for my product?**

 Before production we review whether your product category is regulated (toys electronics cosmetics PPE food contact medical). If yes we only send you quotes from factories that already hold valid CE/LFGB/ROHS test reports. If certification must be freshly issued we coordinate TÜV/SGS/Intertek testing at cost — you see the original lab invoice no markup. DoC files are yours to keep.

###

 **Can you do DDP delivery to a residential address in France or Belgium?**

 Yes. Our DDP FR/BE service includes: China export, international freight, EU customs clearance, EU import duty, 20% VAT (or 6% reduced rate), and final-mile courier via Colissimo/Chronopost/GLS/DPD to any code postal in France and Belgium. Luxembourg requires separate customs paperwork — just mention it upfront.

###

 **I am a private individual (particulier) in France, no registered business. Can I still import?**

 Yes. For private individuals in the EU, small sample orders under about €22 are usually duty-free and VAT-free. Larger commercial shipments (≥ €150) are much simpler with a registered business + EU EORI, but we can still ship DDP to your home address using an importer-of-record. We will explain all options before you pay anything.
