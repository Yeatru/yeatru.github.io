Buying from China as a Ghanaian importer usually means chasing a factory in a different time zone, trusting photos, and finding out at Tema that a document is missing. A procurement agency exists to close those gaps before the container leaves.

## Ghana: what decides the landed cost

### The levy stack

Ghana's VAT is 15%, but NHIL, GETFund and the COVID-19 health recovery levy are charged alongside it, so the effective rate on the CIF value is around 21%. A quote built on 15% alone understates the cost by roughly six points before duty is counted.

### Standards and conformity

The Ghana Standards Authority sets requirements for many consumer and electrical goods, and some categories need registration or a conformity certificate before release. Food, cosmetics and medical items additionally fall under FDA Ghana. Which route applies depends on the product, so it is decided at quotation stage.

### Port and inland costs

Tema handles most container traffic; Takoradi serves the west. Port charges, terminal handling and the inland haulage to Kumasi or Tamale are frequently underestimated, and they are quoted as their own lines rather than folded into a single vague freight number.

## Frequently asked questions

### What taxes should I budget for an import into Ghana?

   Budget for 15% VAT plus NHIL, GETFund and the COVID-19 health recovery levy — about 21% on the CIF value at current rates — on top of customs duty for your HS code. Rates change, so the final figure should come from a Ghanaian broker or your accountant on the day of entry, and our quotation shows the estimate as its own line rather than hiding it in the freight.

### Do my products need Ghana Standards Authority approval?

   Some categories do. The Ghana Standards Authority regulates a defined list of consumer and electrical goods, and certain products need registration or a conformity certificate before they are released. Food, cosmetics and medical devices additionally involve FDA Ghana. We check which route applies to your product before quoting, so the certificate is not discovered at the port.

### Can you deliver DDP to Accra?

   Yes, DDP to Accra or Kumasi with duty and the levy stack handled. DDP is priced per shipment because it depends on volume, the lane and the duty rate. If you have your own clearing agent and want to control the entry, FOB or CIF with your forwarder is usually cheaper, and we quote both.

### What is the minimum order for Ghana?

   50 pieces per SKU for custom work, with mixed SKUs consolidated into one shipment; stock items from one piece. Below USD 1,000 the 10% low-order rate applies, which keeps a first trial commercially sensible.

### Can you source several product types in one shipment?

   Yes, and it is usually the cheapest way to start. Different suppliers, different categories, one consolidated shipment and one set of freight charges. Each product keeps its own compliance file, but the cartons travel together from the Yiwu warehouse.
