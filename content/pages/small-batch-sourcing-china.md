## What changes between 50, 100 and 300 units

Honest guidance on where a batch size stops paying for itself.

      |
        | Batch size || Best used for || Packaging and labelling || What we usually recommend |

        | 50 units || Testing one variant, one colour or one market before committing cash || Your logo and a printed label are usually possible; printed retail boxes often are not || Keep to a stock carton plus a label, and reinvest after the first sell-through |

        | 100 units || A first Amazon listing or a Shopify drop with two variants || Custom label, hang tag and polybag printing become realistic || The best balance of test size and unit price for most first launches |

        | 300–500 units || Proven demand, a second colour or a wholesale enquiry || Custom retail packaging and inserts usually make sense here || Move to sea freight and a custom box once the listing is converting |

Thresholds vary by material and factory line. We confirm the real minimum for your product in the quotation rather than quoting a number we cannot hold.

## Where small batches go wrong — and what we check

Most first-order losses are not price problems. They are specification, labelling and compliance problems.

### Compliance route

Before production we confirm which rules apply to the destination: CE marking for the relevant directives, REACH restricted substances, EN71 for toys, UKCA for Great Britain and GPSR for the EU. GPSR, in force since 13 December 2024, also requires an EU-based responsible person and traceability details on the product or packaging — a requirement many first-time sellers discover at customs.

### Label and artwork

Symbols, warnings, material composition, country of origin, batch code and importer details have to fit on a small label without losing legibility. We review the artwork before the print run, because reprinting labels costs more than the labels.

### Material and safety

A factory's standard material is not always the material your market accepts. We flag food-contact, children's-product and chemical restrictions early and coordinate the lab tests the factory must supply — the importer of record still holds legal responsibility.

### Tolerances and packaging

Colour drift against an approved sample, size tolerance, carton drop strength and net weight are checked against the spec sheet, not against memory. On a 50-unit run one bad carton is 2% of your stock.

## Small-batch sourcing questions

The answers buyers ask for before the first order.

###
    What is the minimum order quantity for a small-batch order from China?

    Yeatru Sourcing accepts custom and private-label orders from 50 pieces per SKU, and stock catalogue items from 1 piece. Mixed SKUs from different factories can be consolidated into a single shipment.

###
    Can I order only 100 units with my own logo and packaging?

    Yes. Logo printing and custom labels are viable from 50–100 pieces on ready-made products. Private-label packaging is quoted from roughly 200–500 pieces, and custom molds typically start at 1,000–3,000 pieces.

###
    How much does a 50 or 100 unit order cost?

    The service fee is 5–8% of order value for regular sourcing and 8–12% for OEM or private-label work. Orders below USD 1,000 carry a 10% low-order surcharge plus a USD 50 handling fee, and samples are USD 50 per SKU refundable on the first purchase order. Factory unit price and shipping are itemised separately.

###
    Is it cheaper to order 500 units instead of 50?

    The unit price is usually lower at 500 pieces because the factory spreads setup and printing costs, but a 50-piece run needs less cash and leaves less dead stock. We quote both quantities so you can compare landed cost per unit before deciding.

###
    Do you work with first-time importers?

    Yes. Many of our clients place their first China order with us — 200+ buyers in 50+ countries since the company was registered in Yiwu on 21 February 2022. A first order starts with a written brief, a factory shortlist and a paid sample before any production payment.

###
    Can you help me create a brand from zero?

    Yes. For a new brand we prepare the product spec sheet, review label and packaging artwork, check the certification route for the destination market, and run the first production batch through AQL 2.5 inspection.

###
    What do you need from me to quote?

    Send product photos or a reference link, target quantity, target landed price, destination country and any certification requirement. A written quotation with unit price, MOQ, lead time, Incoterms and commission is returned within 24 hours.

###
    How is quality checked on a small batch?

    Every batch is inspected before the balance payment under AQL 2.5 Level II, with dated photos and a written report. Amazon FBA orders can also be checked piece by piece for FNSKU labelling, polybag warnings and carton marking.

###
    Who is responsible for EU and UK compliance?

    The importer of record remains legally responsible, but we flag the applicable rules before production — CE, REACH, EN71, UKCA and GPSR — and coordinate the lab tests and documents the factory must supply. GPSR also requires an EU-based responsible person and traceability details on the product or packaging.

###
    Can you ship only 50 units DDP?

    Yes. Small batches move by air or express under DDP terms to 50+ countries, including Amazon FBA warehouses. Duty, import VAT and last-mile delivery are quoted per shipment against your address and HS code.

###
    What if the factory insists on a 500-piece MOQ?

    We negotiate a trial run, split the order across colours or sizes already scheduled, or move the enquiry to a factory whose line suits shorter runs. If none of that works, we tell you the real minimum instead of promising an MOQ the factory cannot hold.

###
    Do you only work with small orders?

    No. We also act as a Yiwu buying arm for established wholesalers, distributors and private-label brands that want sourcing, inspection and consolidation without the fixed cost of a full buying office.
