 FAQ

## Factory Audit FAQs

 What is the difference between supplier verification and factory audit?

Verification is a basic check (1-day visit license capacity). Audit is a deep-dive (2-3 days 60+ points compliance social R&D). Audit is recommended for high-value or long-term partnerships.

 How much does a factory audit cost?

Standard audit (quality + production) starts at $400 for 1-day on-site. Full audit (quality + social + environmental) starts at $600 for 2-day on-site. International auditor rates are 2-3x our local rates.

 Do you use international auditors?

We have both. Local Chinese auditors (lower cost) for standard audits and international auditors (higher cost native language) for compliance-sensitive audits. You choose.

 How long is an audit report valid?

Reports are valid for 6-12 months. Annual re-audit is recommended for ongoing partnerships. We can schedule recurring audits at discounted rates.

 Can the factory fail the audit?

Yes. If critical issues are found (fake licenses no export rights child labor safety violations) we mark the audit as 'Failed - Not Recommended' and suggest alternative suppliers.
