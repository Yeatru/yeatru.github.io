## DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) — What Is & Is NOT Included

When we provide a **written DDP quote** specific to your product destination and weight the total price explicitly INCLUDES the following 6 items:

 Included in DDP

 - International freight (air / sea / rail / express)
 - China export customs clearance + export permits (per HS)
 - Destination country import customs clearance filing
 - Destination import duty (per applicable HS schedule)
 - Destination VAT / GST / Sales tax
 - Final‑mile delivery to the buyer's exact door address

 EXCLUDED Unless Stated

 - Storage / warehousing fees after destination delivery
 - Demurrage beyond carrier free‑days
 - Product compliance certs (CE UKCA FCC FDA RoHS SASO SFDA UKCA CCC Halal…)
 - Bonded‑warehouse deferment VAT‑ID rep fees importer‑of‑record setup
 - Returns re‑shipments or reverse logistics

 Specific Market Prep Capabilities

 - Amazon TR FBA prep: FNSKU Turkish TR safety labels
 - DDP to home address or 3PL in any Turkish il
 - Can issue proforma invoices suitable for TÜRKVAT & bank FX controls
 - Small sample order friendly — MOQs from 1 pc off-the-shelf

 Destination Customs & Tax — Turkey (Türkiye) — İstanbul Ankara İzmir Bursa Antalya Adana Trabzon Gaziantep

For Türkiye imports: Turkish Customs Administration (Gümrük) rules apply. DDP quotes to Türkiye include: international freight Turkish customs clearance (gümrük işlemleri) import duty (ithalat vergisi varies by HS code — typically 0%–45%) KDV (20% standard VAT — OTEVAT) and final-mile trucking. DDP pricing does NOT include antidumping duties if your HS code triggers them (e.g. certain textiles/steel) nor additional inspections by Gümrük. We always confirm HS code + duty schedule in writing before shipment.
