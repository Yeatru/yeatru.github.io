## What a Slovenian buyer has to get right before production

Slovenia applies the full EU product-safety stack, and the paperwork is checked at the border rather than after the goods reach your warehouse. Three items decide whether a shipment clears without delay.

### VAT and EORI

Slovenian standard VAT is 22% (9.5% reduced). Commercial imports need an EU EORI number; B2C parcels under €150 can use IOSS so VAT is collected at checkout instead of at the border.

### Packaging EPR

Anyone placing packaged goods on the Slovenian market must register with a packaging producer-responsibility scheme (Slopak or equivalent) and report packaging weight. Registration is per producer, not per shipment.

### Toys: CE, EN 71, GPSR

Plush toys are toys under EU law: CE marking, EN 71-1/-2/-3 test reports, a technical file, and GPSR safety information with warnings in the language of the destination market.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you source plush toys from China to Slovenia?

   Yes. Plush toys are a regular category for us. The buyer above ordered four characters in 24 cm and 45 cm sizes plus two four-piece sets. Toy imports need CE marking, EN 71-1/-2/-3 reports and GPSR safety information, so the compliance check happens before the factory is booked, not after the goods are made.

### What is the minimum order for a custom hanger card?

   The card itself is priced per piece, and the printer's minimum depends on the card stock and print method. On the order above it was $0.56 per piece across 16 pieces, including assembly, labour and repack. For 50–100 pieces the per-piece cost usually falls; we quote the card as a separate line so you can see exactly what the customisation adds.

### How does DDP delivery to Slovenia work?

   We consolidate at our Yiwu warehouse, ship to Luka Koper, clear EU customs, pay the 22% import VAT and deliver to your address. You receive one all-in price and one invoice. If you already have a forwarder and an EU EORI number, FOB or FCA is cheaper because you control the freight.

### Do I need packaging EPR registration before importing into Slovenia?

   Yes, if you are the producer placing packaged goods on the Slovenian market. You register with a packaging producer-responsibility scheme such as Slopak and report the packaging weight you put on the market. This is a producer obligation, not something the factory or the agent can absorb for you, so we flag it before production starts.
