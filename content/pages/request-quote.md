## Quote Request FAQs

 How long does it take to get a quote?

We respond to all quote requests within 24 hours. You'll typically receive supplier options, unit prices, and MOQ within 24 hours of submitting your request.

 Is there any obligation or cost?

No. Our quote service is completely free and carries no obligation. You can take our supplier recommendations and pricing and decide whether to work with us or not. We only charge a sourcing fee if you confirm an order through us.

 What information should I provide?

The more detail you provide the better we can match you with the right suppliers. Include: product description target quantity target price range any certifications needed and your target market or sales channel. Photos or reference links are very helpful.

 Can I get a quote for multiple products at once?

Absolutely. You can list multiple products in the "Additional Information" field. We'll source them all and provide a consolidated quote. For 5+ products we may recommend a dedicated sourcing plan for better pricing.

 Will my information be kept confidential?

Yes. We take confidentiality very seriously. Your product ideas pricing and business information are never shared with third parties. We can also sign an NDA (Non-Disclosure Agreement) before you share sensitive details if requested.
