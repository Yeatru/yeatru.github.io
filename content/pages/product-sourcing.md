 FAQ

## Product Sourcing FAQs

 How long does the sourcing process take?

Initial supplier quotes: 24-48 hours. Sample delivery: 7-14 days. Full project including sample approval: 2-4 weeks depending on complexity.

 Do you have a minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

MOQ depends on the product. Many products support 50-100 piece MOQs. For OEM custom products typical MOQ is 200-500 pieces. We'll always present low-MOQ options first.

 Can you source products not in your catalog?

Yes. Tell us what you need and we'll source it from our factory network in Yiwu, Guangzhou and Shenzhen. We handle custom specifications packaging and branding.

 Do you charge a sourcing fee?

For clients with their own suppliers our Plan A fee is ¥500/task. For full sourcing (Plan B) we charge 0% — paid by the supplier as a referral fee. You get the factory-direct price.

 Can you source in any language?

We communicate in English, Mandarin, Spanish, French, Russian and Arabic. Our local team handles all Chinese-language supplier communications.
