Product Description

Product Overview

Our men’s mesh athletic sneakers combine breathability, cushioning and daily fashion into one pair of versatile walking shoes. Crafted with lightweight breathable fly woven mesh, these running trainers solve the common stuffy foot problem in warm seasons, while the built-in air cushion PVC sole offers reliable shock absorption for all daily sports. Two classic solid colorways (white / black) fit all casual styles, ideal young men footwear for spring, summer and autumn.

Key Features

  • Ultra-Breathable Mesh Construction
  • Whole shoe upper & inner lining adopt porous mesh fabric, continuous air circulation prevents sweat buildup, keeps feet fresh even after hours of walking or training. Round toe design reserves enough space for toes, no squeezing.
  • Shock Absorbing Air Cushion Outsole
  • PVC injection molded sole embedded with visible air cushion blocks at front and rear palm. Vertical striped anti-slip texture boosts friction with ground, flexible bending performance adapts to natural foot movement, greatly buffer walking impact.
  • Simple Versatile Fashion Look
  • Streamlined curve print logo decorates the side, low-top slip-on lace-up design with heel pull loop for easy on/off. Solid white and solid black two color options match all daily clothes, from sweatpants to denim jeans.
  • All-Round Daily Application
  • Universal athletic shoes fit multiple occasions: daily commute, street walking, gym fitness, jogging, short trip, outdoor leisure, shopping etc. Flat low-heel structure suitable for long-time standing work.
