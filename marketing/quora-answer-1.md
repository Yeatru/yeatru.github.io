# Quora Answer: What is the best sourcing agent in China for Amazon FBA?

---

I've been a China-based sourcing agent for 14+ years, serving 200+ Amazon FBA sellers across 50+ countries. So I'll skip the marketing fluff and give you a practical framework.

There's no single "best" agent — the right choice depends on your product category, order volume, and experience level. But after working with hundreds of FBA sellers, here are the **5 criteria that actually matter**:

---

## 1. Local Team in China (Not a Remote Middleman)

Many "sourcing agents" are actually one-person operations run from a laptop in Shenzhen or even overseas. They never visit factories. They just forward your inquiries to Alibaba suppliers and mark up the price.

**What to look for:**
- Physical office in a major sourcing hub (Yiwu, Guangzhou, Shenzhen)
- Team that speaks Chinese natively and can visit factories in person
- Willingness to do video calls showing their office/warehouse

A Yiwu-based agent, for example, has direct access to 75,000+ suppliers in the world's largest wholesale market. That's a massive advantage over someone working remotely.

---

## 2. Transparent Pricing (No Hidden Fees)

This is where most agents lose trust. Common pricing traps:

- **Hidden commission**: They say "no commission" but mark up factory prices 10-15%
- **Mystery fees**: Inspection fees, document fees, handling fees that appear after you commit
- **Inflated shipping**: They quote 2x the actual freight cost

**What to look for:**
- Published commission rates (typically 3-8% depending on service level)
- Clear breakdown of all costs before you place an order
- Minimum fee transparency (e.g., $150/order minimum, not "it depends")

For reference, our pricing at Yeatru is publicly listed: 3-4% for Order Management (when you already have suppliers), 4-8% tiered for Full Sourcing (when we find suppliers from scratch). No hidden fees.

---

## 3. Quality Control with AQL 2.5 Standard

Amazon FBA has strict quality requirements. If your products arrive defective, you'll face:

- Customer returns (hurting your account health)
- Negative reviews (destroying your ranking)
- Potential listing suspension

**What to look for:**
- Pre-shipment inspection using AQL 2.5 standard (industry benchmark)
- Photo and video documentation of the inspection
- Three-level QC: incoming materials, in-process, pre-shipment

Without QC, the average defect rate from Chinese factories is 12-15%. With proper AQL 2.5 inspection, it drops to under 1%.

---

## 4. Proven Track Record with FBA Sellers

A general "import/export" agent may not understand Amazon's specific requirements:
- FBA prep (labeling, poly bagging, bubble wrap)
- ASIN/SKU management
- FBA shipment creation and routing
- HS code classification for US customs

**What to look for:**
- Case studies with specific Amazon sellers (not just "we serve FBA clients")
- Measurable results: cost reduction %, defect rate %, lead time
- Understanding of Amazon's compliance requirements (CPC for toys, FDA for food contact, etc.)

---

## 5. Responsive Communication (24-Hour Guarantee)

The #1 complaint I hear from sellers about their previous agents: "They take 3 days to reply to a simple question."

When you're running an FBA business, delays cost money. A production delay of 1 week can mean:
- Stockout on Amazon → lost BSR ranking
- Missed Q4 launch window → $10,000+ in lost sales

**What to look for:**
- Guaranteed response within 24 hours (1 business day)
- Direct WhatsApp/WeChat access (not just email)
- Dedicated account manager (not a rotating support team)

---

## Quick Decision Framework

| Your Situation | Recommended Agent Type |
|---|---|
| First-time FBA seller, < $5k budget | Agent with low MOQ (50 pcs) and full service |
| Experienced seller, $50k+/month | Agent with dedicated QC and logistics team |
| Niche products (electronics, toys) | Agent with category-specific compliance knowledge |
| Multiple products/categories | Agent in Yiwu (broad supplier network) |
| Single product, high volume | Agent in Guangzhou/Shenzhen (factory-direct) |

---

## Red Flags to Avoid

- 🚩 Refuses to share factory contact info (even after order is placed)
- 🚩 No physical address or office photos
- 🚩 Payment only via Western Union or crypto
- 🚩 Promises unrealistically low prices (50% below market)
- 🚩 No quality control process or inspection reports
